# BD Pilot 001 — UAE Multi-Location Restaurants — Digital Visibility

**Status:** Draft / not activated  
**Parent programme:** [DRF #150](https://github.com/tbhrc/drf-main/issues/150)  
**BD Skill:** [tbhrc/skills#158](https://github.com/tbhrc/skills/issues/158)

## Hypothesis

UAE restaurant groups with meaningful public review/discovery volume but fragmented listings, booking/contact routes, customer-data capture or multi-location consistency are strong targets for a **value-upfront Digital Presence Brief Audit**.

The report creates a credible first conversation without starting with a generic AI-services pitch.

## Why this is the recommended first pilot

The live VB World case has already proven the report-production motion on a real UAE restaurant context:
- strong public review/rating evidence is easy to sight and source;
- opening-hours/address/listing inconsistencies can be objectively shown;
- booking, delivery, phone, WhatsApp and social routes create measurable customer-journey questions;
- multi-location growth increases the cost of inconsistent public information;
- downstream opportunities can include customer capture, feedback, reputation, booking/enquiry conversion, CRM/WhatsApp, AI visibility and management reporting.

This is **proof of process**, not proof of outbound conversion. Pilot 001 must measure the commercial response.

## Offer / first-touch asset

**Complimentary Digital Presence Brief Audit Report**

The asset should normally contain:
- meaningful independently sighted public numbers;
- clickable sources at the point of use;
- strengths as well as gaps;
- specific listing/customer-journey findings;
- at least 3 Quick Wins the restaurant can implement itself immediately;
- no generic “AI transformation” filler;
- no detailed technology architecture or hidden-platform pitch.

Commercial next step:

> Discuss the findings and, if useful, identify which improvements are worth implementing or automating first.

## Target account criteria

Prefer restaurant businesses that satisfy most of these:
- UAE presence;
- 2+ locations **or** clear expansion/new-location signal;
- meaningful review/rating volume on public platforms;
- active website/social/delivery/listing presence;
- identifiable booking/enquiry/catering/contact route;
- enough public evidence to create a useful report without inventing facts;
- visible inconsistency, conversion friction or customer-capture opportunity;
- management/business scale sufficient to buy implementation/managed services.

## Priority signals

Strong signals include:
- conflicting hours/addresses across public platforms;
- multiple locations without clean location architecture;
- strong review volume but inconsistent review-response/reputation handling;
- website booking/catering/contact route that is difficult to measure or fragmented;
- clear paid/social activity without an obvious owned-data/CRM path;
- separate WhatsApp/phone/location channels with weak central visibility;
- strong public demand proof but limited customer relationship capture;
- new branch opening/expansion;
- outdated/unfinished landing pages or public information;
- weak AI/search answer consistency for location/opening/contact questions.

## Exclude / deprioritise

- single micro-operators with insufficient ability to pay;
- businesses with almost no public evidence to audit;
- targets where the only possible hook would be speculative/negative criticism;
- businesses already showing mature integrated CRM, listing governance, first-party booking attribution and customer lifecycle automation unless another clear problem is evidenced;
- records with no legitimate business contact route;
- targets where outreach would create a material consent/policy concern.

## Batch 1

Target **10 companies** only.

Do not generate a huge scraped list before the first 10 prove the motion.

For each target:
1. qualify against this ICP;
2. record the live account/opportunity in GHL when operational;
3. sight enough public evidence to justify the report;
4. prepare the audit only for Priority/strong Qualified accounts;
5. founder-review the first outbound batch;
6. send through the verified iMPLEMENTAi email/GHL route;
7. track replies and stage changes in GHL.

## Suggested downstream service paths

The report is the acquisition motion. The eventual paid work depends on the evidence and discovery.

Potential iMPLEMENTAi implementation families include:
- reputation + local visibility operations;
- customer-data capture / CRM;
- review request + service-recovery automation;
- WhatsApp/call/enquiry routing and follow-up;
- booking/catering lead tracking;
- customer feedback and management alerts;
- repeat-customer/reactivation workflows;
- AI visibility / AEO / GEO readiness;
- multi-location data/listing consistency;
- commercial reporting/management dashboards.

Do not force every target into the same implementation package.

## Success metrics

Measure:
- accounts researched;
- accounts qualified;
- audits prepared;
- audits sent;
- replies;
- positive replies;
- meetings booked;
- discovery completed;
- qualified paid opportunities;
- recommendations/proposals;
- Won;
- setup revenue;
- MRR;
- time per audit;
- target → send → reply → meeting → Won cycle time.

## Initial proof thresholds

Do not pretend a 10-account pilot establishes statistical certainty. It is an execution proof.

Useful signals:
- reports can be produced quickly enough to justify the motion;
- recipients engage with the findings rather than treating them as generic spam;
- at least one real meeting/qualified commercial discussion results;
- the downstream implementation opportunity is economically meaningful;
- GHL/agent operations reduce manual effort rather than adding admin.

## Stop / change conditions

Rework or stop the pilot if:
- audit production time is disproportionate to response;
- targets lack useful public evidence;
- response consistently indicates the report is too broad/generic;
- the GHL/email operating layer creates more manual reconciliation than value;
- the first-touch asset produces attention but no credible paid implementation path.
