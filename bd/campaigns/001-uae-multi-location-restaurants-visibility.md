# BD Pilot 001 — UAE Multi-Location Restaurants — Digital Visibility

**Status:** Draft / not activated  
**Parent programme:** [DRF #150](https://github.com/tbhrc/drf-main/issues/150)  
**Canonical BD Skill:** [`tbhrc/skills/drf-business-development`](https://github.com/tbhrc/skills/tree/main/drf-business-development)  
**Current live-CRM selection/proof:** [DRF #157](https://github.com/tbhrc/drf-main/issues/157)

## Hypothesis

UAE restaurant groups with meaningful public review/discovery volume but fragmented listings, booking/contact routes, customer-data capture or multi-location consistency are strong targets for a **value-upfront Digital Presence Brief Audit**.

The report creates a credible first conversation without starting with a generic AI-services pitch.

## Why this pilot

The VB World case proved the report-production motion on a real UAE restaurant context: strong public review/rating evidence is sourceable; listing/hour/address inconsistencies can be shown; booking, delivery, phone, WhatsApp and social routes create measurable customer-journey questions; and multi-location growth increases the cost of inconsistent public information.

This is **proof of process**, not proof of outbound conversion. Pilot 001 must measure the commercial response.

## Offer / first-touch asset

**Complimentary Digital Presence Brief Audit Report**

The asset should normally contain:
- independently sighted public numbers where useful;
- clickable sources at the point of use;
- strengths as well as gaps;
- specific listing/customer-journey findings;
- at least 3 Quick Wins the restaurant can implement itself immediately;
- no generic “AI transformation” filler;
- no invented technology architecture, traffic, ROI or performance claims.

Commercial next step:

> Discuss the findings and, if useful, identify which improvements are worth implementing or automating first.

## Target account criteria

Prefer businesses satisfying most of these:
- UAE presence;
- 2+ locations or a clear expansion/new-location signal;
- meaningful public review/rating volume;
- active website/social/delivery/listing presence;
- identifiable booking/enquiry/catering/contact route;
- enough public evidence to create a useful report without invention;
- visible inconsistency, conversion friction or customer-capture opportunity;
- sufficient management/business scale to buy implementation/managed services.

Deprioritise micro-operators with weak ability to pay, accounts with insufficient evidence, mature integrated operators without another clear problem, invalid contact routes, or targets with material consent/policy concerns.

## Batch 1

Target **10 companies only**. Do not create a huge scraped list before the first 10 prove the motion.

For each target:
1. qualify against this ICP;
2. use the **single currently selected live CRM** from #157 for ordinary company/contact/opportunity state when the verified write route exists;
3. sight enough public evidence to justify the report;
4. prepare the audit only for Priority/strong Qualified accounts;
5. founder-review the first outbound batch unless a separately current bounded autonomy rule already covers the exact action;
6. send only through a verified approved email route under current authority;
7. track actual replies, stage and next action in the selected live CRM.

Do not establish GitHub as a backup per-lead CRM if the selected CRM route is unavailable; record only the bounded campaign work needed to continue safely.

## Potential downstream service paths

The report is the acquisition motion. Paid work depends on evidence and discovery. Potential iMPLEMENTAi implementation families include:
- reputation + local visibility operations;
- customer-data capture / CRM;
- review request + service-recovery automation;
- WhatsApp/call/enquiry routing and follow-up;
- booking/catering lead tracking;
- customer feedback and management alerts;
- repeat-customer/reactivation workflows;
- AI visibility / AEO / GEO readiness;
- multi-location data/listing consistency;
- commercial reporting/management dashboards.

Do not force every target into the same package.

## Success metrics

Measure from the selected live CRM wherever possible:
- accounts researched and qualified;
- audits prepared/sent;
- replies / positive replies;
- meetings booked;
- discovery completed;
- qualified paid opportunities;
- recommendations/proposals;
- Won;
- setup revenue and MRR;
- time per audit;
- target → send → reply → meeting → Won cycle time.

A 10-account pilot is an execution proof, not statistical certainty.

## Stop / change conditions

Rework or stop if:
- audit production time is disproportionate to response;
- targets lack useful public evidence;
- response indicates the report is too broad/generic;
- the selected CRM/email operating layer creates more manual reconciliation than value;
- the first-touch asset produces attention but no credible paid implementation path.

## Activation gate

This file does **not** authorise outbound.

Activate Batch 1 only after:
1. one live CRM is selected and operational for the required state under #157;
2. the approved email route is verified;
3. the target list is qualified;
4. first reports pass the applicable review gate;
5. the current founder/autonomy policy authorises the first send batch.
