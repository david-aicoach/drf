## Wave logic

### Wave 1 — cash and the Blueprint channel

Rebuild the most commercially immediate revenue lanes plus Whop first. These opportunities can generate real cash/evidence fastest or become the distribution channel for other proven systems.

### Wave 2 — strong productisation and recurring models

Rebuild opportunities that may become durable products but require clearer buyer economics, delivery boundaries or repeatability.

### Wave 3 — longer-horizon, liquidity, audience or platform businesses

Rebuild only after the faster lanes are operating or when new evidence materially changes the case.

## Migration rules

- Do not create 28 Issues before execution starts.
- Open one Issue when an opportunity enters active rebuild.
- Start from the Version 2.0 template; do not edit the old score into a new result.
- Preserve old research as source history.
- Replace `Pending` only with sourced work.
- Capital remains `$0` until the required proof gate unlocks it.
- Update the return profile from the canonical workbook.
- Re-rank only after a meaningful cohort of opportunities has been rebuilt; do not compare new RBS scores directly with old v1.5 scores.

## Completion definition

The portfolio migration is complete when every active opportunity shows:

- Revenue Blueprint Score;
- Proof Level;
- Investment Gate;
- capital and founder-time requirement;
- monthly/annual Return Profile;
- correct model card;
- next proof milestone;
- investor-ready yes/no;
- linked evidence and financial model.
