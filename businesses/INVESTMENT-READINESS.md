## Important

Opportunity Scores are the current **Layer 1 structural business-attractiveness scores**. They remain first-class DRF decision inputs, but they are **not investor-readiness ratings** and do not replace Niche Score, RBS, DRF Proof, Stage, Capital or Return.

Until a selected Business × Niche has completed the relevant commercial underwriting in the canonical Opportunity Factory, read deeper underwriting fields as Pending rather than zero or failure.

The current decision format is:

> **Business opportunity × best scored niche · Opportunity Score __/100 · RBS __/100 · Niche __/100 · DRF Proof P_ · Stage ______ · Capital unlocked $_____ · Return ______**

Canonical operating Skill: `skills/drf-opportunity-factory/SKILL.md`  
Canonical underwriting standard: `skills/drf-opportunity-factory/references/commercial-underwriting-proof-capital.md`

Legacy Revenue Blueprint Factory workflow references in Git history are compatibility/history only. RBS, DRF Proof P0–P6, Stage, Capital and Blueprint certification remain subordinate controls inside the Opportunity Factory.

### Outcome × niche × channel rule

A platform, CRM, AI model or delivery vendor is not the commercial opportunity. DRF first defines the **measurable result sold**, then ranks the niches where that result has the strongest pain, economics and reachability.

**Current parent opportunity count: 27.** Website/launch-kit assets are downstream Business Blueprint product formats and are not separately ranked.

For digital products, distribution is a separate downstream layer:

```text
Business opportunity / result
→ ranked niches
→ selected Business × Niche
→ offer / pricing / GTM / delivery
→ RBS / Return / DRF Proof / Stage / Capital
→ optional Blueprint productisation
→ compatible distribution channels
→ channel-level economics
```

The niche score remains separate from RBS. A niche can be attractive while the delivery business is weak, and a structurally strong result can become exceptional only in the right niche.

## Wave logic

### Wave 1 — cash and Business Blueprints

Rebuild the most commercially immediate revenue lanes plus the Business Blueprints productisation layer first. Proven systems can generate cash directly and later be packaged into digital products distributed across multiple compatible endpoints.

### Wave 2 — strong productisation and recurring models

Rebuild opportunities that may become durable products but require clearer buyer economics, delivery boundaries or repeatability.

### Wave 3 — longer-horizon, liquidity, audience or platform businesses

Rebuild only after the faster lanes are operating or when new evidence materially changes the case.
