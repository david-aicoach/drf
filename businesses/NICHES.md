## Purpose

This register is the second commercial-selection layer below `businesses/OPPORTUNITIES.md` and the source for the **Niche attractiveness ranking** rendered in root `index.html`.

The portfolio table scores the **business/product**. This register scores the **specific market target for that product and delivery rail**.

Atomic target:

`outcome/product × delivery rail × vertical × sub-niche × geography × ICP × pain/trigger`

Scores are desk-research scores until live prospect/customer evidence exists. **Niche Evidence Confidence is separate from Niche Attractiveness Score.** Grok Bot, HighLevel and other systems are delivery rails; the commercial offer remains the measurable business outcome.

## Grok Bot rules

Grok Bot rows follow these controls:

1. Sell the measurable outcome, not generic Bot setup.
2. Prefer narrow recurring read/research/prepare/update work first.
3. Use human approval for consequential outreach, payments, refunds, contractual commitments, legal/medical judgement or destructive actions.
4. Treat public/shared Bot configurations as accelerators, not proprietary moat.
5. Credentials, client data and private memory must not be embedded in shareable templates.
6. Measure support minutes and browser/UI failure rate because early-beta computer-use can create hidden service labour.

## Decision rule

A niche enters the primary execution lane only when:

1. Niche Score is high enough;
2. Evidence Confidence is adequate;
3. qualified prospect volume is verified;
4. decision-makers are reachable;
5. pricing/ROI logic survives direct buyer interviews;
6. the offer can be delivered repeatedly without bespoke consulting.
