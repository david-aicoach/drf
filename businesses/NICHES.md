## Purpose

This register is the second commercial-selection layer below `businesses/OPPORTUNITIES.md` and the source for the **Niche attractiveness ranking** rendered in root `index.html`.

The portfolio table scores the **business/product**. This register scores the **specific market target for that product and delivery rail**.

Atomic target:

`commercial layer × outcome/product × delivery rail × vertical × sub-niche × geography × ICP × pain/trigger`

Scores are desk-research scores until live prospect/customer evidence exists. **Niche Evidence Confidence is separate from Niche Attractiveness Score.** Grok Bot, HighLevel and other systems are delivery rails; the commercial offer remains the measurable business outcome or recurring operating foundation.

Issue #40 adds one operating control to Product-System Fit and Sales/Fulfilment Simplicity: **technical computer-use capability is not enough**. A high score requires representative production economics, including quota, overage, browser/UI, authentication and human-recovery burden. High-frequency native actions should not be routed through computer use merely because an agent can click them.

### Commercial layers

- **Foundation** — persistent customer/revenue infrastructure such as WhatsApp + CRM Revenue Core.
- **Outcome** — a measurable commercial result such as Revenue Recovery, Missed Lead, Voice, Instant Quote, Support or Reputation.
- **Vertical Platform** — a bundled vertical operating product such as HighLevel Rentals + Direct Booking.
- **Agentic Operations** — persistent cross-system operating work where a general agent creates enough incremental value to justify its variable execution cost.

The same ICP may appear in multiple rows because the layers are separately sellable and can form one account-expansion stack. This is intentional, not duplicate scoring.

## AI delivery economics resolution — issue #40

The new evidence changes how agent-heavy niches are interpreted:

1. **Subscription access is not labour capacity.** Grok Bot's US$20–30 entry price does not establish a fixed number of useful autonomous worker-hours.
2. **Measure quota and job economics.** Record weekly quota consumption, `sand-*` tokens where exposed, completed jobs, failures/retries, human recovery minutes, on-demand spend and cost per successful job.
3. **Use native execution for high-frequency events.** CRM, workflow, messaging, voice, fleet/PMS/e-commerce APIs or other native actions should handle predictable recurring state changes when practical.
4. **Reserve computer use for its comparative advantage.** Grok Bot is strongest where the task crosses systems, requires research/judgement, or lacks a robust native/API path.
5. **A score can fall while Evidence Confidence rises.** Better research on quota and browser risk reduces uncertainty while also reducing Product-System Fit for some workloads.

Canonical rationale: `research/ai-delivery-economics-portfolio-rescore-2026-08-29.md`.

## Grok Bot rules

Grok Bot rows follow these controls:

1. Sell the measurable outcome, not generic Bot setup.
2. Prefer narrow recurring **high-value** read/research/prepare/exception work first.
3. Use human approval for consequential outreach, payments, refunds, contractual commitments, legal/medical judgement or destructive actions.
4. Treat public/shared Bot configurations as accelerators, not proprietary moat.
5. Credentials, client data and private memory must not be embedded in shareable templates.
6. Measure support minutes and browser/UI failure rate because computer-use can create hidden service labour.
7. Treat the base subscription as **access**, not evidence of production capacity.
8. Do not use Grok Bot to repeatedly click through work that a robust native/API/workflow action can execute more cheaply and reliably.

## WhatsApp + CRM foundation rule

WhatsApp + CRM Revenue Core is a first-class Foundation opportunity rather than hidden infrastructure.

Use this sequence for UAE service businesses where the foundation fits:

```text
WhatsApp + CRM foundation
→ highest-value measurable outcome module
→ deterministic/native execution for recurring volume
→ native AI where sufficient
→ second module only when the data proves the leakage
→ external Grok Bot/agent layer only for justified cross-system gaps/exceptions
```

This prevents both extremes:

- selling generic CRM software with no business result; and
- selling an outcome module without the durable customer/pipeline foundation required to operate and measure it properly.

## Decision rule

A niche enters the primary execution lane only when:

1. Niche Score is high enough;
2. Evidence Confidence is adequate;
3. qualified prospect volume is verified;
4. decision-makers are reachable;
5. pricing/ROI logic survives direct buyer interviews;
6. the offer can be delivered repeatedly without bespoke consulting.

## Evidence gate after Issue #40

Desk research is complete enough to select market tests. The next score movement must come from live operating evidence:

- clean prospect universe;
- 10–20 buyer/workflow interviews where practical;
- real onboarding time;
- baseline leakage/time/cost;
- staff adoption;
- automation/agent failure rate;
- quota/usage consumption where relevant;
- measured commercial outcome;
- support/recovery minutes;
- cost per successful completed job;
- gross margin;
- MRR retention;
- module expansion rate.

Those are validation metrics, not additional desk-research prerequisites.
