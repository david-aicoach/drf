`product × vertical × sub-niche × geography × ICP × pain/trigger`

Scores are desk-research scores until live prospect/customer evidence exists. **Niche Evidence Confidence is separate from Niche Attractiveness Score.**

## Evidence notes — user-defined priority niches

### UAE MEP / HVAC

Current desk evidence supports strong market size and growth. One April 2026 market estimate places the UAE MEP services market at **USD 8.4B in 2025**, forecasting **USD 14.9B by 2032 (8.8% CAGR)**, with Dubai estimated at about **44%** of the UAE market. Mechanical services, including HVAC, are described as the largest service category.

Source: https://www.igtps.com/report/u-a-e-mep-services-market

This is market-level evidence, not proof of DRF's revenue-recovery economics. Direct contractor evidence is still required.

### Drywall / gypsum / false ceilings

Current research supports meaningful underlying demand but market estimates vary materially by provider. Grand View Research reports UAE gypsum-board revenue of about **USD 499.1M in 2024** and projects **13.8% CAGR from 2025–2030**. A Dubai business directory currently exposes thousands of records in the broader `Partitions and False Ceilings Contracting` category, supporting reachability but **not** serving as a verified count of qualified DRF prospects.

Sources:
- https://www.grandviewresearch.com/horizon/outlook/gypsum-board-market/uae
- https://dcciinfo.com/uae/dubai/partitions-and-false-ceilings-contracting/10835

The critical commercial unknown is quotation standardisation: DRF must establish which scopes can be quoted/ranged from structured inputs and which still require a mandatory site survey.

### Dubai holiday homes / HighLevel Rentals

HighLevel's current Rentals product supports:

- multi-day bookings;
- listings/categories;
- inventory and variants;
- advanced pricing/discounts;
- booking calendar management;
- external calendar sync;
- payments, invoicing and security deposits;
- a public customer booking page.

Sources:
- https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000006649-guide-to-the-rentals-calendars
- https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000006579-rentals-create-edit-listings

Dubai DET confirms that holiday-home operation is regulated, requires unit registration/permits and recognises professional operators as a distinct service user. Individual homeowners can register up to eight holiday-home units, which reinforces the usefulness of targeting professional multi-unit operators as a separate ICP.

Sources:
- https://www.dubaidet.gov.ae/our-services/for-consumers-and-students/register-as-a-holiday-home-operator
- https://www.dubaidet.gov.ae/en/our-services/for-consumers-and-students/issue-a-new-holiday-homes-permit

A secondary Dubai operator source reports **22,000+ licensed holiday homes** as of 2024, citing DET; treat this as supporting evidence rather than a current official unit count until directly verified against DET statistics.

Source: https://royalestays.com/how-many-holiday-homes-are-there-in-dubai/

### Compliance boundary for direct booking

Do not build the holiday-home economics around taking OTA-provided guest contact data and using it for unauthorised off-platform solicitation. The model should create first-party demand and consent lawfully through the operator's owned website, CRM, direct enquiries and permitted guest lifecycle processes while respecting OTA/platform terms and applicable privacy/marketing rules.

## Decision rule

A niche enters the primary execution lane only when:

1. Niche Score is high enough;
2. Evidence Confidence is adequate;
3. qualified prospect volume is verified;
4. decision-makers are reachable;
5. pricing/ROI logic survives direct buyer interviews;
6. the offer can be delivered repeatedly without bespoke consulting.

Current priority for deep validation:

1. **HVAC maintenance contractors × Revenue Recovery — 91/100**
2. **Dubai holiday-home operators × HighLevel Rentals + Direct Booking + CRM — 88/100**
3. **Specialist MEP contractors × Revenue Recovery — 88/100**
4. **Drywall/gypsum/false-ceiling installers × Instant Quote — 83/100**

The lower-confidence rows remain useful hypotheses but should not displace these user-defined priority niches until researched.
