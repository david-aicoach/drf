## Status legend

- **Idea** — captured but not yet researched enough to act.
- **Researching** — market/problem/economics being validated.
- **Candidate** — evidence is strong enough to design a bounded experiment.
- **Testing** — live commercial experiment underway.
- **Active** — producing measurable commercial value.
- **Paused** — intentionally inactive.
- **Retired** — rejected or no longer relevant.

## Strategic portfolio interpretation

The five new opportunities are not intended to become five disconnected companies. Their strongest configuration is a compounding stack:

```text
vertical AI operating system
        ↓
website / launch kit
        ↓
ChatGPT plugin / app
        ↓
agentic-commerce / AI visibility layer
        ↓
reusable niche data/API capability
        ↓
stronger vertical operating system
```

The practical DRF rule is therefore:

> **Prove one commercially valuable vertical, then extract multiple reusable revenue assets from the same operating knowledge.**

This avoids building five separate acquisition engines and lets research, data, workflows, integrations and customer knowledge compound across products.

## Research-gap rule

Every opportunity must use the scoring factors as its research checklist. If a factor cannot be supported, mark the underlying evidence as **Verified**, **Estimated** or **Missing** and reduce Research Completeness accordingly.

The intended loop is:

```text
idea
→ preliminary scoring
→ score exposes missing evidence
→ targeted research
→ rescore
→ bounded commercial test
→ real operating evidence
→ rescore
→ scale / pause / retire
```

### Capital gate

As a default, do not commit **material capital** based solely on a high Opportunity Score when **Evidence Confidence is below 60%**. A cheap, reversible validation experiment is allowed when the experiment itself is designed to close the evidence gap.

All seven current opportunities are now above the desk-research confidence gate, but **none has sufficient DRF operating evidence for material scale capital based on research alone**. The next score movement should come from real sales, usage, conversion, retention, support load, payouts, CAC and contribution margin.
