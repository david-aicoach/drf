## Status legend

- **Idea** — captured but not yet researched enough to act.
- **Researching** — market/problem/economics being validated.
- **Candidate** — evidence is strong enough to design a bounded experiment.
- **Testing** — live commercial experiment underway.
- **Active** — producing measurable commercial value.
- **Paused** — intentionally inactive.
- **Retired** — rejected or no longer relevant.

## Strategic portfolio interpretation

### MRR changes the ranking for the right reason

Framework v1.1 intentionally rewards opportunities where revenue can recur without repeatedly recreating the product or reacquiring the same customer.

This moves recurring SaaS/API/recovery products upward and one-time asset businesses downward even when those assets remain highly autonomous and profitable.

### HighLevel is now a first-class DRF opportunity family

The seven HighLevel opportunities are strongest as modules around one client/vertical, not seven independent businesses:

```text
HighLevel Vertical SaaS core
      ↓
Missed Lead Conversion
      ↓
Instant Quote
      ↓
AI Support
      ↓
AI Voice
      ↓
Revenue Recovery
      ↓
Reputation / Local Visibility
```

This enables **land-and-expand MRR**: acquire one customer once, then increase recurring value and revenue through additional modules.

### Portfolio operating rule

> Prefer opportunities with high MRR and high AI Autonomy where the recurring value is embedded in the customer's ongoing operating flow.

A monthly invoice alone does not create a high MRR score. Recurring value, retention logic and low proportional service labour do.

## Research-gap rule

Every opportunity must use all 15 factors as its research checklist and mark evidence **Verified**, **Estimated** or **Missing**.

```text
idea
→ preliminary scoring
→ targeted research
→ rescore
→ bounded commercial test
→ real MRR / retention evidence
→ rescore
→ scale / pause / retire
```

### Capital gate

Do not commit material capital based solely on a high Opportunity Score when Evidence Confidence is below 60%. All current opportunities above the gate still require real operating evidence before meaningful scale capital.

The next score movement should come primarily from:

- MRR actually collected;
- churn / gross revenue retention;
- expansion revenue / net revenue retention;
- CAC and payback period;
- contribution margin;
- support burden;
- automation exception rates;
- measurable client ROI.
