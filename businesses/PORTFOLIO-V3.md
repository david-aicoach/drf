# Register interpretation

## Why many fields are Pending

Only a small current Business × Niche cohort has RBS/P2 underwriting in `INVESTMENT-READINESS.md`. The remaining parent opportunities retain their Layer 1 research and scores but have not yet completed the deeper Layer 2/Layer 3 workflow.

Pending does not mean zero, failed or unattractive. It means the required workflow output has not yet been produced.

## Ranking

Rank follows the canonical Opportunity Score portfolio order. Tied rounded scores retain a stable order rather than implying false precision.

## Best niche

The best-niche fields are pointers only. All ranked relationships remain in `businesses/NICHES.md`. For Autonomous AI Revenue Operations, the existing 84 MEP/HVAC niche is explicitly delivery-rail evidence from the prior Grok-heavy thesis and must not be treated as newly revalidated for the broader parent.

## Evidence/proof distinction

External Market Proof and DRF Proof are separate. The Autonomous AI parent has **EMP2 Active market / 90% confidence** based on broad commercial agent-category evidence while its DRF Proof remains Pending/early. Business Blueprints has **EMP3 Market proven / 90% confidence** from independent operating-system/template/snapshot businesses while DRF remains **P2 Backtested**; EMP reduces what must be tested but does not award P3–P6.
