# Businesses

Each folder here is a revenue-producing venture, productised service or commercial experiment with its own operating truth.

A business folder should begin with a `README.md` covering:

- customer;
- painful problem / trigger;
- measurable outcome sold;
- niche: vertical, sub-niche, geography and ICP;
- customer channel;
- system of record;
- agent / delivery layer where relevant;
- offer and pricing/economics;
- acquisition channel;
- current stage;
- success metric;
- stop / scale condition.

Use the canonical commercial deployment model:

`Outcome × Niche × Customer Channel × System of Record × Agent Layer`

Do not define a business primarily by a vendor such as HighLevel, Kapso, HubSpot, Grok Bot, ChatGPT or Claude unless the vendor itself is the product being sold. Vendors and models are delivery components and should remain replaceable where practical.

For UAE service-business opportunities, treat WhatsApp as the default first-class customer channel unless niche evidence shows another channel dominates.

Canonical architecture: `knowledge/architecture/outcome-first-modular-revenue-architecture.md`.

Add campaign, operations or finance subfolders only when real work exists.
