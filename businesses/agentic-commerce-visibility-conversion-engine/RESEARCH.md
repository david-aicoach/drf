## Current thesis

AI-shopping referral behaviour and transaction infrastructure are accelerating, creating demand for merchant visibility, feed/data quality and conversion optimisation. A recurring optimisation/monitoring subscription is plausible, but retention economics and attribution remain unproven.

## Supported evidence

- AI shopping/discovery is becoming a material commerce surface.
- Merchant data quality and machine-readable product information are recurring operational needs.
- Monitoring/optimisation can be automated substantially.

## Key unknowns

- Stable attribution from AI inclusion to sales.
- Merchant willingness to retain after initial optimisation.
- Platform volatility and cross-surface measurement consistency.

## Minimum commercial test

Select 3–5 merchants, record baseline AI visibility/referral metrics, implement bounded changes, and measure inclusion, traffic, conversion and ongoing maintenance minutes.
