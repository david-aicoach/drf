# Research — Agentic Commerce Visibility & Conversion Engine

Updated: 2026-08-29  
Issue: #45  
Research status: **Comprehensive desk research complete; merchant willingness-to-pay and attributable uplift still require live proof**

## Executive conclusion

**Recommendation: retain at 90/100 and increase research confidence, but keep the offer tightly defined as merchant readiness + visibility + conversion instrumentation for AI shopping surfaces rather than vague “GEO”.**

The market category has become materially more concrete in 2026. OpenAI now publishes Merchant Feed Terms, supports merchant/product discovery in ChatGPT, integrates product/catalogue data and has described Instant Checkout / Agentic Commerce Protocol workflows in which the merchant remains responsible for orders, payments and fulfilment. Google has published the Universal Commerce Protocol (UCP) for agentic commerce and is building shopping/checkout experiences around Merchant Center data and the Shopping Graph. Adobe now provides brand/AI visibility and referral-traffic measurement tooling.

This changes the opportunity from speculative SEO-for-AI into an emerging technical/merchandising discipline: merchants need clean structured product data, accurate availability/pricing, policy-compliant feeds, accessible storefronts, transaction readiness and instrumentation that can prove whether AI surfaces cite, refer or convert.

The remaining uncertainty is commercial. AI shopping infrastructure is real, but DRF does not yet know how much UAE/GCC merchants will pay, which optimisation actions materially change inclusion/ranking, or how stable attribution will be across platforms.

## Market evidence

### OpenAI

OpenAI's current merchant ecosystem provides direct evidence that merchants can make structured product information available for ChatGPT shopping/product discovery. Its Merchant Feed Terms govern submitted product content. OpenAI's product-discovery materials describe richer shopping experiences and merchant connections, while its shopping help documentation describes product ranking considerations such as availability, price, quality and seller attributes. Instant Checkout / Agentic Commerce Protocol materials show a transaction model in which ChatGPT can facilitate purchase while the merchant remains merchant of record/fulfilment owner.

### Google

Google's Universal Commerce Protocol is an open protocol for agentic commerce actions. Google states that UCP can enable actions such as buying while the merchant remains Merchant of Record, with Merchant Center/product data playing a central role. In May 2026 Google said it was building a universal cart and agentic shopping experiences and cited more than one billion shopping sessions per day and a Shopping Graph with more than 60 billion product listings.

### Measurement ecosystem

Adobe Brand Visibility includes referral-traffic reporting designed to connect AI discovery/citation exposure with visits and conversions through analytics/CDN integrations. This supports the thesis that brands will need a measurement layer, not just content optimisation.

## Buyer problem

A merchant can have excellent conventional SEO and still have product data that is poor for AI shopping systems:

- incomplete/incorrect feeds;
- inconsistent price/availability;
- weak identifiers/variants;
- inaccessible product detail;
- missing shipping/returns/trust data;
- poor structured data;
- blocked crawlers/bots;
- no monitoring of AI referrals/citations;
- no way to connect AI-origin visits to purchase.

The product should repair and monitor those commercial data paths.

## What DRF should sell

> **An ongoing AI-commerce readiness and conversion service that keeps product/catalogue data accurate and machine-readable, monitors inclusion/referral performance across major AI shopping surfaces, and fixes the technical/merchandising issues that block discovery or conversion.**

Not:

- guaranteed ChatGPT ranking;
- secret prompt hacks;
- mass-produced “AI SEO” content;
- unsupported claims that one schema change forces inclusion.

## Product modules

### 1. Merchant readiness audit

- catalogue/feed quality;
- identifiers/variants;
- price/availability consistency;
- product page accessibility;
- structured data;
- shipping/returns/contact/trust information;
- merchant policy readiness;
- checkout/transaction path;
- analytics/referral capture.

### 2. Feed and catalogue normalisation

Map merchant source of truth into OpenAI/Google-compatible product feeds or platform connectors.

### 3. AI visibility monitoring

Track representative commercial queries and whether products/brand appear, with clear caveats that AI outputs are probabilistic and personalised.

### 4. Referral/conversion instrumentation

Tag and measure AI referrals where platforms/browsers provide identifiable source data; connect to GA4/Adobe/merchant analytics.

### 5. Ongoing optimisation

Fix feed errors, stale content, pricing mismatches, unavailable products and conversion friction.

## Best initial customers

- Shopify merchants with meaningful UAE/GCC sales;
- DTC brands with 100–10,000 SKUs and existing ecommerce revenue;
- retailers with clean catalogue data but no AI-commerce monitoring;
- high-AOV categories where a small increase in qualified referrals has material value.

Avoid very small merchants without enough traffic/data to prove anything.

## Competitive landscape

Competition will come from:

- ecommerce SEO agencies rebranding into GEO/AEO;
- feed-management/catalogue platforms;
- merchant platforms such as Shopify/Google Merchant Center;
- Adobe and enterprise visibility tools;
- analytics vendors;
- in-house ecommerce teams.

DRF's wedge should be end-to-end execution for merchants too small for enterprise tooling but large enough to value recurring optimisation.

## Commercial model hypothesis

### Audit/setup

AED 3,000–10,000 depending on catalogue size and data quality.

### Monthly managed plan

AED 2,000–7,500/month for feed health, visibility monitoring, issue resolution, reporting and conversion instrumentation.

### Larger merchants

Custom catalogue/integration fee plus recurring monitoring; avoid pure performance pricing until attribution is reliable.

## Delivery economics

The service is mostly data transformation, testing, monitoring and deterministic API/feed work. General-purpose agents can assist research and issue diagnosis but should not be used for every product update.

Costs:

- feed/catalogue tooling;
- ecommerce integrations;
- monitoring/query execution;
- analytics;
- engineering exceptions;
- account management.

MRR quality is strong if the merchant has continuous SKU/pricing/inventory changes that require ongoing monitoring.

## GTM

Offer a concrete **AI Commerce Readiness Audit** rather than a strategy retainer.

Deliverables:

- 20–50 representative buyer queries;
- current AI shopping presence snapshot;
- feed/catalogue health score;
- data/structured-content gaps;
- conversion/analytics gaps;
- prioritised remediation plan;
- 30-day before/after measurement.

Sell to merchants already spending on SEO/PPC because they have an existing acquisition budget and attribution mindset.

## Defensibility

- merchant feed schemas/connectors;
- query/visibility benchmark history;
- issue-resolution playbooks;
- cross-platform catalogue knowledge;
- merchant-specific historical data;
- causal experiments linking fixes to inclusion/referrals/conversion;
- automation around feed QA and monitoring.

## Risks

- platform algorithms/ranking criteria opaque and fast-changing;
- difficult causal attribution;
- platforms may integrate optimisation natively;
- merchant catalogue quality outside DRF control;
- low current AI shopping traffic in some regions/categories;
- monitoring queries may not represent each user's personalised output;
- regulatory/consumer-protection issues around product claims;
- “GEO” hype creates unrealistic buyer expectations.

## Evidence discipline

### Verified

- OpenAI has formal merchant feed terms and merchant/product discovery infrastructure.
- OpenAI has launched/outlined transaction experiences and merchant integrations.
- Google publishes UCP and agentic commerce infrastructure tied to Merchant Center/product data.
- Adobe provides AI referral/visibility measurement capabilities.

### DRF judgement

- The sellable offer is catalogue/feed readiness + measurement, not generic content writing.
- Shopify/DTC merchants are the best initial test because integration and transaction data are accessible.
- Recurring monitoring is more defensible than a one-off audit.

### Unproven

- UAE/GCC merchant demand and price sensitivity;
- incremental inclusion from each optimisation action;
- AI referral share of revenue by niche;
- retention once a catalogue is initially fixed.

## Validation experiment

Recruit 3–5 merchants with enough existing ecommerce traffic.

For each:

1. define 20–50 high-intent product queries;
2. capture baseline AI inclusion/citation/product appearance;
3. audit feed/schema/catalogue health;
4. implement only clearly justified fixes;
5. instrument AI referral traffic;
6. repeat monitoring for 30–60 days.

Metrics:

- product feed error rate;
- indexed/eligible products;
- query-level appearance rate;
- AI referral sessions;
- assisted/direct conversions where measurable;
- merchant revenue from attributable AI referrals;
- support hours;
- merchant renewal intent.

### Pass gate

Proceed when at least one merchant shows measurable improvement in AI discoverability/referral/conversion signals and at least two merchants demonstrate willingness to pay for recurring monitoring.
