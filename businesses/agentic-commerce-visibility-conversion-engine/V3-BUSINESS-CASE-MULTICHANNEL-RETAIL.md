## Founder decision

**ADVANCE TO A BOUNDED MERCHANT ATTRIBUTION TEST; DROP GENERIC SHOPIFY FEED-SETUP POSITIONING.**

AI-search visibility is now an active software/services market and agentic commerce infrastructure is real (**EMP3 / 91% confidence**). But OpenAI currently states Shopify merchant product data is already integrated through Shopify Catalog with no additional merchant work required. That makes simple “get your Shopify feed into ChatGPT” work a weak wedge.

The remaining business is **cross-platform commerce readiness + measurement + continuous conversion optimisation** for merchants with enough catalogue complexity and revenue to care.

## Offer

### AI Commerce Readiness & Revenue Attribution

1. **Commercial-query baseline** — 30–100 representative buyer queries across major AI surfaces.
2. **Catalogue/product truth audit** — identifiers, variants, price, stock, policy/trust, structured product detail and cross-channel consistency.
3. **Eligibility/readiness fixes** — only technically justified actions; no guarantee of ranking.
4. **AI visibility monitoring** — product/merchant/brand appearance and competitive share.
5. **Referral/conversion instrumentation** — connect identifiable AI-origin visits to analytics and commerce outcomes where possible.
6. **Recurring optimisation** — detect stale catalogue/availability/content issues and prioritise measurable commercial fixes.

Do not sell vague GEO. Do not sell unnecessary ChatGPT feed submission to ordinary Shopify merchants.

## Pricing / commercial model

**Desk hypothesis — not approved public pricing.**

- Audit/setup: **AED5,000–12,000**.
- Managed monitoring + optimisation: **AED3,000–8,000/month**.
- Custom PIM/feed/connectors or multi-market/catalogue work: separately scoped.
- Avoid pure performance pricing until attribution is reliable.

## GTM

Target merchants already spending meaningfully on SEO/PPC and capable of measuring ecommerce conversion.

Lead with an audit:

> “For the product queries your buyers ask AI systems, where do your products appear today, what information do those systems see, and can you connect any AI-origin visit to an order?”

First cohort:
1. identify 20–30 UAE/GCC brands with complex catalogues and visible paid/organic acquisition;
2. disqualify tiny merchants and simple Shopify-only catalogues where no meaningful problem exists;
3. secure 3–5 permissioned baselines;
4. implement only clear feed/catalogue/measurement fixes;
5. seek recurring monitoring commitments only after measurable value.

Founder approval is required before outreach or merchant-data access.

## Delivery architecture

```text
PIM / ecommerce / catalogue source of truth
→ deterministic cross-channel feed & metadata QA
→ AI query/visibility monitoring
→ issue prioritisation
→ bounded content/catalogue optimisation
→ analytics/referral attribution
→ merchant revenue reporting
```

The commerce platform/PIM remains authoritative. AI agents assist analysis and prioritisation; product truth must not be generated from model memory.

## One Next Proof

Run a **3–5 merchant** baseline/optimisation study, including at least two complex multi-channel/non-simple-Shopify catalogues.

**Pass:**
- one or more merchants show measurable improvement in relevant AI appearance/referral/conversion after justified fixes;
- **at least 2 merchants agree to pay for recurring monitoring/optimisation**;
- integration/support effort remains bounded enough for positive contribution.

**Fail / recycle:** if AI-origin traffic is too small, changes are not measurable, or merchants will only buy one-off audits, keep the capability as an attached ecommerce service rather than a standalone recurring opportunity.

## Counter-evidence

- Shopify Catalog already removes a large feed-onboarding pain for ChatGPT shopping.
- Semrush, Adobe, AthenaHQ and Scrunch increasingly commoditise monitoring.
- AI rankings are probabilistic and opaque.
- AI traffic growth in U.S. retail does not guarantee UAE/GCC volume.
- A provider cannot responsibly guarantee inclusion or ranking.

## Current read

**Strong emerging commercial category, but the wedge is narrower than the original thesis.** The business survives as merchant-specific cross-platform readiness + measurement + conversion operations, not generic GEO or Shopify feed submission.
