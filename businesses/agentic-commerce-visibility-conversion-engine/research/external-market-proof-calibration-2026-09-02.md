# Agentic Commerce Visibility & Conversion Engine — External Market Proof Calibration

**Date:** 2 September 2026  
**Issue:** #142  
**Parent:** `agentic-commerce-visibility-conversion-engine`

## Decision

**EMP3 Market proven · EMP Confidence 91%.**

AI-search/visibility optimisation and measurement are now active paid categories with multiple independent software and managed-service operators. Agentic product discovery/commerce infrastructure is also real and rapidly expanding. However, **merchant-specific attributable sales lift and optimisation causality are not established enough for EMP4**, and OpenAI's direct Shopify Catalog integration materially weakens the original “fix Shopify feeds for ChatGPT” wedge.

The parent remains strong, but the sellable product must move from vague GEO/feed setup toward **multi-channel commerce readiness, AI visibility measurement and attributable conversion operations**.

## Current first-party infrastructure evidence

### OpenAI / ChatGPT shopping
OpenAI's current Shopping help documentation states merchants are ranked using merchant/product metadata and factors such as availability, price, quality and whether the seller is the maker/primary seller. Crucially:

- **Shopify product data is already integrated into ChatGPT through Shopify Catalog.**
- OpenAI says **no additional work is required from individual Shopify merchants** for that product-data integration.
- Merchants can still apply to provide direct product feeds for the most up-to-date information.

This is direct counter-evidence against selling “ChatGPT feed submission” as a service to ordinary Shopify merchants.

Source: https://help.openai.com/en/articles/11128490

### Adobe Brand Visibility
Adobe announced Brand Visibility in June 2026 and made it generally available on **4 August 2026**. Adobe states AI traffic to U.S. retail sites grew **1,324% between October 2024 and May 2026**. The platform combines Semrush AI visibility intelligence, optimisation workflows and measurement intended to connect AI discovery to business performance.

Sources:
- https://news.adobe.com/news/2026/06/introducing-adobe-brand-visibility
- https://experienceleague.adobe.com/en/docs/brand-visibility/using/essentials/release-notes

### Managed GEO / AI visibility services
Independent managed providers are charging recurring retainers. One transparent example, The Rank Collective, lists **US$3,500/month**, **US$7,500/month** and **US$15,000+/month** tiers. A Dubai provider, Internext, publicly lists managed AI SEO/GEO from **AED500–5,000/month**.

Sources:
- https://therankcollective.com/pricing/

These prices validate current spend on AI visibility, but are not direct proof that merchant-specific agentic-commerce optimisation produces incremental sales.

## Transferability to DRF

### Transfers well
- buyers already pay recurring fees for AI visibility monitoring/optimisation;
- AI search/discovery traffic is growing rapidly;
- ecommerce merchants already operate structured catalogue, feed and analytics systems;
- monitoring, feed QA and measurement are highly automatable;
- multi-channel retailers have continuing product/price/inventory changes that support MRR.

### Does not transfer automatically
- enterprise Adobe demand to UAE mid-market retailers;
- causality between schema/content changes and model recommendations;
- attributable AI sales volume in UAE/GCC;
- Shopify feed setup as a unique service;
- stability of ranking/merchant-selection logic across AI platforms.

## Counter-evidence / failure modes

1. **Shopify integration is native:** ordinary Shopify merchants do not need extra ChatGPT product-feed work.
2. **Tool commoditisation:** Semrush, AthenaHQ, Scrunch and Adobe already provide monitoring/optimisation tooling.
3. **Attribution remains difficult:** AI outputs are probabilistic/personalised; referral source data may be incomplete.
4. **Platform-native optimisation will grow:** ecommerce platforms can absorb more of the workflow.
5. **Low regional volume:** some UAE/GCC categories may not yet receive enough AI shopping traffic for measurable ROI.
6. **Opaque ranking criteria:** no responsible provider can guarantee ChatGPT/AI ranking.

## Commercial conclusion

Do not sell generic “GEO” or Shopify feed submission. The stronger DRF product is:

> **AI Commerce Readiness & Revenue Attribution** for multi-channel retailers/brands with complex catalogues and existing ecommerce acquisition spend — normalise cross-platform product truth, monitor AI discovery, instrument attributable referrals/conversions and continuously fix measurable discovery/conversion gaps.

The next DRF test should prove **merchant willingness to pay and attributable improvement**, not the existence of agentic commerce infrastructure.
