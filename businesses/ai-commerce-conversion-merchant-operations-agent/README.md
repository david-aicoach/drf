## What we sell

A branded AI shopping + merchant-operations layer for mid-market ecommerce retailers, integrated with their existing commerce platform, to improve guided product discovery/conversion and reduce repetitive ecommerce support, catalogue and merchant-operations work.

**This is not a Shopify replacement and not a “Claude consulting” business.** Shopify/another commerce platform remains the system of record and checkout. Claude Commerce Agents is one open technical accelerator; the business identity is vendor-neutral.
