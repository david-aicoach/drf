## Current thesis

Subscription/API/listing MRR is plausible, but a broad horizontal directory is constrained by incumbents, acquisition economics and data/permission rights. The opportunity should start with one narrow rights-safe vertical where AI-first discovery solves a specific buying problem.

## Supported evidence

- Discovery/marketplace demand can support listing, subscription and API monetisation.
- AI can automate classification, enrichment, matching and maintenance.
- Narrow vertical focus can create stronger utility than a generic horizontal directory.

## Key unknowns

- Permissioned supply/data rights.
- Recurring seller/API willingness to pay.
- CAC, churn and defensibility versus incumbents.

## Minimum commercial test

Choose one narrow vertical with lawful/permissioned supply, manually prove buyer demand and recurring seller/API payment before expanding horizontally.
