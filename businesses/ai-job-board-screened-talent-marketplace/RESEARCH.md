# Research — AI Job Board + Screened Talent Marketplace

Updated: 2026-08-29  
Issue: #45  
Research status: **Comprehensive desk research complete; two-sided UAE niche liquidity and paid employer demand remain unproven**

## Executive conclusion

**Recommendation: keep Stage = Researching and treat the current 84/100 score as provisional. Do not build a broad job board. Prove one narrow screened-talent market manually first.**

The infrastructure is commoditised. Niceboard currently sells a complete job-board platform from **US$399/month** (US$319/month billed annually) with thousands of jobs, tens of thousands of seeker accounts, applicant tracking, subscriptions, upsells, a talent pool and monetisation. Wellfound also demonstrates how job listings, talent access, AI sourcing and managed recruiting can coexist in one platform.

That means DRF's problem is not software. It is liquidity: enough relevant employers, enough genuinely available candidates, enough trust in the screening evidence and enough transactions to make both sides return. Talent Bridge's strongest wedge is therefore a **screened UAE/GCC niche talent market**, not another general job board.

## Product definition

> **A narrow UAE/GCC hiring marketplace where candidates have verified role-relevant screening/assessment evidence and employers pay for jobs, access or successful hiring.**

The “AI” component should improve matching, evidence normalisation and workflow; it is not the reason the marketplace exists.

## Strong first wedge

Choose one professional role family already supported by Talent Bridge employer/candidate activity. Examples:

- finance managers/controllers;
- HR managers/HRBPs;
- hospitality finance;
- selected engineering/commercial roles.

Start as a curated pool + employer brief service. A public job board is optional later.

## Marketplace architecture

```text
candidate opt-in + profile
     ↓
structured screening / assessment evidence
     ↓
freshness + availability check
     ↓
searchable/matched talent pool
     ↕
employer role + criteria
     ↓
AI-assisted matching + human quality controls
     ↓
interview / application / hire
     ↓
outcome feedback improves matching
```

## Competitive landscape

- Wellfound and major job boards;
- LinkedIn;
- Gulf recruitment/job platforms;
- specialist recruitment agencies;
- Niceboard-powered niche boards;
- pre-assessed talent pools;
- ATS sourcing features.

The broad market has enormous incumbents. Niche density and evidence quality are the only rational entry point.

## Commercial model hypothesis

Potential employer revenue:

- job postings / promoted listings;
- monthly access to screened talent;
- candidate unlock/contact credits;
- subscription bundles;
- success/placement fee where Talent Bridge actively intermediates;
- assessment/evaluation upsells.

Candidates should generally remain free during liquidity creation.

## Economics

Platform cost is not the major concern. Track:

- candidate acquisition + verification cost;
- employer acquisition cost;
- assessments/licences;
- support/moderation;
- available candidates per active role;
- matches/interviews per employer;
- paid employer ARPA;
- hire/success revenue;
- repeat hiring/renewal.

## GTM

Do not launch an empty public marketplace.

1. recruit 20–50 permissioned, current candidates in one role family;
2. manually approach 20 relevant employers;
3. match roles and candidates concierge-style;
4. charge for access, a live role or success;
5. only automate/publicise features that support proven behaviour.

## Defensibility

- verified candidate evidence;
- GCC candidate relationships;
- freshness/availability operations;
- employer relationships;
- role-specific data and benchmarks;
- transaction/hiring outcome history;
- assessment/evaluation integration.

## Risks

- classic two-sided cold start;
- broad job-board competition;
- stale profiles;
- employer bypass/disintermediation;
- low willingness to pay for postings;
- candidates duplicate across platforms;
- privacy/consent issues;
- weak screening credibility;
- insufficient density in chosen niche.

## Evidence discipline

### Verified

- Full job-board/talent-pool software can be bought rather than built.
- Wellfound monetises job distribution, sourcing and managed recruitment.
- Talent Bridge already has access to employer/candidate networks.

### DRF judgement

- liquidity, not code, determines success.
- screened evidence + UAE/GCC niche depth is the viable wedge.
- manual concierge validation should precede marketplace build.

### Unproven

- employer willingness to pay;
- candidate supply/freshness;
- match/interview rates;
- repeat employer demand;
- marketplace acquisition economics.

## Validation experiment

One role family, 30–60 days:

- 20–50 verified candidates;
- 20 employer conversations;
- at least 5 live roles or serious hiring briefs;
- manual matching before product build.

Measure paid employer conversions, candidate response, interviews, hires, employer repeat intent and support minutes.

### Pass gate

Proceed only if real employers pay and the same narrow pool produces repeated interviews/hiring activity.

## Ranking implication

**84/100 may be too generous until liquidity is proven.** The technology/build score should be easy, but distribution risk is substantially higher than the current top revenue modules. Review downward in the next formal rescore if no paid niche traction appears.
