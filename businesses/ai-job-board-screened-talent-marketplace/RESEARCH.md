## Current thesis

Talent Bridge can differentiate candidate supply using assessment and structured screening, but the two-sided liquidity problem makes this materially harder than a white-label portal or assessment product. It should be demand-tested before marketplace build work.

## Supported evidence

- Talent Bridge has candidate/recruitment supply and assessment capability.
- Screened evidence can differentiate from generic CV listings.
- AI can automate matching, summaries and much of supply preparation.

## Key unknowns

- Two-sided acquisition cost and liquidity.
- Employer recurring willingness to pay.
- Candidate freshness, consent and repeat usage.

## Minimum commercial test

Publish one role-family landing page with a small screened pool and sell employer access manually before building broad job-board functionality.
