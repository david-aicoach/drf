## Executive founder decision

**GOLDEN PRIORITY / TEST.** Sell a measured AI-recommendation visibility service to businesses that depend on recommendation-style discovery: baseline how often and how accurately they appear across relevant AI/search surfaces, fix evidence-backed technical/entity/citation gaps, re-test with the same instrument, and monitor the result monthly. Do **not** promise a ChatGPT/Gemini ranking and do not build a proprietary monitoring SaaS first.

Recommended beachhead: **Dubai company-formation / business-setup consultancies serving foreign founders and SMEs**. These firms sell high-value, comparison-heavy services, are easy to enumerate, compete heavily on digital trust/content and can economically justify a recurring visibility layer if it produces or protects qualified demand.

Current commercial test hypothesis: **AED2,500 setup/baseline + AED1,500/month managed monitoring/optimisation** as the forward-test floor, with larger implementation work separately scoped. This is a DRF estimate, not a market actual.

**Next Proof:** after founder approval for outreach, take one fixed 10–15-prompt buyer-intent instrument to 10 qualified Dubai business-setup consultancies and require **2 unrelated paid pilot deposits** at or above the pre-committed floor, with at least one buyer acquired outside a direct personal favour/relationship. Stop/recycle if fewer than two paid commitments emerge after the bounded test or if the baseline cannot produce decision-useful measurement at bounded delivery cost.

## 1. Business / money model

> We sell repeatable AI recommendation-visibility measurement, optimisation and monitoring to growth-oriented service businesses for an upfront baseline/setup plus recurring monthly management because buyers increasingly ask AI/search systems which provider to choose before contacting a business.

Revenue streams:

- upfront AI Visibility Baseline / technical-citation-prompt audit;
- optimisation sprint / implementation work;
- recurring prompt/citation/competitor monitoring and monthly optimisation;
- optional content/entity/local-profile implementation only when supported by the baseline;
- optional commercial monitoring-tool pass-through at scale.

MRR is genuine only when the monthly service continues to re-measure a stable core prompt set, detect losses/gains/citation changes and execute justified corrections. A monthly report with no action is not sufficient recurring value.

Fatal conditions:

- seller promises deterministic AI rankings or guaranteed recommendation positions;
- buyer has too little economic value or search/recommendation demand to justify monitoring;
- measurement cannot be repeated consistently enough to guide decisions;
- delivery becomes manual screenshot collection rather than a governed repeatable instrument;
- product duplicates generic SEO/reputation software without incremental outcome evidence.

## 4. Counter-evidence / failures

- Google states normal Search fundamentals remain applicable to AI features and that no special AI markup/file is required; there is no credible universal “AI ranking recipe”.
- AI answers vary by model, retrieval state, prompt wording and time. One screenshot is not proof.
- SOCi reports substantial incorrect local-business information and continued user verification, limiting the claim that AI has replaced Google/reviews.
- Semrush and other tools commoditise measurement at roughly US$99/month and up; DRF must deliver interpretation and implementation value above software access.
- The field is still heterogeneous; vendor “visibility scores” are not interchangeable.
- Attribution from recommendation visibility to enquiries/revenue can be incomplete.
- Implementations touching health, finance or other sensitive claims require stronger review and compliance controls.

## 7. Market-ready offer

### AI Recommendation Visibility Growth System

**Baseline / setup**
- technical crawler/indexability readiness;
- versioned 10–15 unbranded buyer-intent prompt pack;
- repeated tests across 3–4 genuinely testable priority engines/surfaces;
- citation/source footprint and competitor frequency;
- entity/category/location/business-fact consistency;
- prioritised actions with evidence level.

**Optimisation sprint**
- fix technical/indexing/crawler issues;
- strengthen missing entity/service/location clarity;
- improve answerable first-party content only where evidence supports it;
- correct structured data/business facts;
- pursue defensible citation/third-party-source opportunities;
- preserve a change log for re-test.

**Monthly managed layer**
- same-instrument core prompt monitoring;
- limited rotating discovery set;
- new/lost citations and competitor changes;
- material technical/entity changes;
- concise action list and bounded optimisation.

**Exclusions**
- guaranteed AI ranking/position;
- fabricated reviews/citations;
- mass low-value content generation;
- special “AI schema” claims;
- consumer-UI scraping that breaches terms;
- replacement of normal SEO/GBP/reputation fundamentals.

Success metric: improved stable recommendation/mention/citation visibility on the fixed instrument plus attributable AI referral/enquiry evidence where available, not a proprietary vanity score.

## 14. Risks and kill/recycle triggers

- **Measurement risk:** unstable answers make trend interpretation noisy → repeated/versioned instrument and sample-size disclosure.
- **Claim risk:** client expects guaranteed ranking → explicit no-guarantee boundary; sell measurement and evidence-led improvement.
- **Attribution risk:** visibility improves but revenue cannot be tied back → instrument AI referrals/enquiries where possible; retain visibility outcome as separate metric.
- **Commoditisation:** tools make measurement cheap → charge for diagnosis, implementation and ongoing operations rather than dashboard access.
- **Platform change:** engine APIs/methods change → provider-neutral instrument and replaceable tools.
- **Privacy/data:** public baseline first; authorised analytics only; no unnecessary client/private data in research.
- **Low retention:** client sees monitoring as one-off → kill/recycle monthly service if recurring action/decision value is absent after the first cycle.
