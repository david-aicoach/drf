# AI Support & Sales Assistant

**Stage:** Candidate  
**Opportunity score:** 93/100

## Offer

A vertical knowledge, support, qualification and booking assistant with explicit human handoff.

## Outcome sold

Faster first response, more resolved enquiries, better qualification, more bookings and lower repetitive support load.

## Delivery shape

Use the customer channels native to the niche; CRM/helpdesk owns durable state; AI handles conversation and knowledge retrieval; rules handle permissions, routing and escalation.

## Commercial model

Setup/knowledge onboarding plus recurring managed assistant subscription.

## Next action

Launch one vertical assistant and measure resolution, booking, escalation, message volume, failure modes and fully loaded cost.
