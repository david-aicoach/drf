## Current thesis

Conversation AI with predictable current HighLevel economics can support high-volume support, qualification and booking across supported channels. Human escalation must be designed into the product rather than treated as an exception after launch.

## Supported evidence

- Conversation AI and multichannel workflows are live.
- Recurring per-location AI plans improve pricing predictability.
- Knowledge/support work has clear measurable resolution and escalation metrics.

## Key unknowns

- Vertical knowledge accuracy and maintenance burden.
- Escalation rate and human support minutes.
- Incremental sales/booking uplift and churn.

## Minimum commercial test

Launch in one knowledge-bounded vertical. Track containment/resolution, qualified leads, bookings, escalations, hallucination/incorrect-answer incidents and total fulfilment cost.
