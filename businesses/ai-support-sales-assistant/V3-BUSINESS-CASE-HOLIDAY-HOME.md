## Founder decision

**ADVANCE TO A BOUNDED NATIVE-GAP TEST, NOT A GENERIC CHATBOT BUILD.**

The broad AI support category is established (**EMP4 / 94% confidence**), but Dubai holiday-home operators already have increasingly capable AI inside Guesty, Hostaway and related PMS ecosystems. The business only survives where DRF proves **incremental value beyond the incumbent PMS**.

**Payer:** Dubai professional holiday-home operator / property manager.  
**ICP:** roughly 20–100 managed units, high multi-channel guest-message volume, existing PMS, and demonstrable unresolved human workload or unsupported cross-system cases.

## Offer

### AI Guest Support Optimisation & Escalation Layer

1. **Message-resolution benchmark** — classify at least 1,000 recent messages into safe-native, reservation-context, task/action, human-judgement and safety/financial cases.
2. **Native PMS AI optimisation** — knowledge, rules, auto-reply boundaries, language, escalation and task routing.
3. **QA and governance** — transcript sampling, critical-answer checks, stale-knowledge detection and escalation auditing.
4. **External agent only where proven necessary** — unsupported WhatsApp/cross-system action or materially better safe resolution.
5. **Monthly operating report** — safe resolution, handoff, staff minutes, critical errors, response time and measurable booking/support outcomes.

Do not sell “a chatbot”. Do not replace a capable PMS inbox.

## Pricing / commercial model

**Desk hypothesis — not approved public pricing.**

- Diagnostic / configuration: **AED5,000–12,000** one-time depending on unit count, PMS and message volume.
- Managed QA / optimisation: **AED2,000–5,000/month**.
- External AI/channel costs: pass-through or transparently rebilled only where the benchmark proves external tooling is needed.
- Custom integration outside standard supported connectors: separate scoped fee.

## GTM

Lead with an operational benchmark rather than AI:

> “What percentage of your guest messages still need a human after your PMS automation, and how many staff hours do those unresolved messages consume?”

Target founders/COOs/guest-experience leaders already using Guesty, Hostaway or similar systems. The first sale is a **native-AI performance audit**, not a software migration.

Acquisition sequence:
1. identify 10–20 professional Dubai operators and their PMS;
2. disqualify operators already achieving high safe containment with native tools;
3. secure one permissioned message sample;
4. quantify the unresolved workload and failure modes;
5. sell configuration/QA only if measurable value exists;
6. add external agent components only after native gap is demonstrated.

Founder approval is required before outreach or client-data access.

## Delivery architecture

```text
PMS unified inbox / native AI
→ approved property + policy knowledge
→ deterministic task / escalation rules
→ external AI only for proven unsupported cases
→ human handoff for refunds, safety, disputes and exceptions
→ transcript QA + outcome metrics
```

The PMS remains the system of record. DRF must not create duplicate reservation or guest truth.

## One Next Proof

Obtain permissioned message data from **3 Dubai holiday-home operators**, ideally across at least two PMSs, and benchmark **≥1,000 recent messages per operator**.

**Pass:** on at least 2 of 3 operators, a clearly defined optimisation/external layer can improve safe resolution or staff time by **≥25% over the operator's current native capability**, with no material critical-answer/safety failure, and at least one operator agrees to pay for the managed layer.

## Risks / counter-evidence

- A second inbox/agent can worsen operations.
- Refunds, safety, disputes and policy exceptions need human control.
- Headline vendor resolution rates are not portable base rates.
- External channel/API costs and bespoke integrations can erase margin.
