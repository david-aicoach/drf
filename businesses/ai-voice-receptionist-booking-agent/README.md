# AI Voice Receptionist & Booking Agent

**Stage:** Candidate  
**Opportunity score:** 94/100

## Offer

Answer inbound calls, qualify callers, book appointments, route urgent cases and recover missed demand with AI voice plus human escalation.

## Customer and pain

Call-heavy service businesses losing bookings because staff cannot answer every call quickly or consistently.

## Outcome sold

Higher answered-call rate, faster response, more booked appointments and lower front-desk load.

## Delivery shape

Voice/phone is the primary channel; CRM is the system of record; deterministic routing/calendar logic surrounds the voice agent; human escalation is mandatory.

## Commercial model

Setup plus recurring per-location service with carrier/usage costs clearly separated or rebilled.

## Next action

Deploy one narrow inbound-call agent and measure booking, transfer, AI minutes, carrier cost, failure rate and human recovery.
