## Current thesis

Current HighLevel Voice AI supports inbound/outbound/widget use and the Unlimited AI option materially improves predictable production economics, subject to fair-use limits. The commercial constraint is no longer basic capability; it is call quality, escalation reliability, carrier cost and niche-specific conversion.

## Supported evidence

- Voice AI and booking workflows exist in the current platform.
- Recurring per-location economics are compatible with a managed service.
- Calls create a direct and measurable booking/transfer funnel.

## Key unknowns

- Accent/noise/edge-case performance in UAE target niches.
- True carrier plus AI cost per completed booking.
- Client tolerance for failures and escalation design.

## Minimum commercial test

Deploy on one narrow call type with a human fallback. Measure answered rate, qualified rate, booked rate, transfer success, failed calls, cost per call and support minutes.
