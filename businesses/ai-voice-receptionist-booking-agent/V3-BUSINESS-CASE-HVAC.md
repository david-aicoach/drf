## Founder summary

Sell **24/7 inbound after-hours and overflow call coverage** for UAE HVAC contractors: answer legitimate calls, capture caller details, qualify safely, book or request dispatch, transfer exceptions, and write the result to the CRM.

The external category is already established. DRF does not need to prove that AI receptionists exist or that businesses pay recurring fees for them. The remaining risk is UAE-specific execution quality and unit economics.

## Offer and price

**Offer:** Emergency HVAC AI Receptionist & Booking Agent

- Setup/configuration: **AED3,000–7,500**.
- Managed inbound voice: **AED1,500–3,500/month** plus carrier/telephony usage where not bundled.
- Multi-location, multilingual or deeper dispatch integrations: custom.

### Revenue streams

- setup/configuration fee;
- recurring managed voice fee;
- telephony/usage pass-through or markup where appropriate;
- integration/advanced routing upsells;
- later same-niche modules such as missed-call rescue and revenue recovery.

## GTM

Lead with a **call-leakage audit** using real call logs:

- inbound calls/week;
- unanswered and after-hours calls;
- callback delay;
- booking/dispatch rate;
- average gross contribution per completed service job;
- current receptionist/answering-service cost.

Sell coverage, consistency and measurable booked outcomes — not “human replacement”.

## Delivery architecture

```text
inbound phone number
→ AI receptionist with narrow approved knowledge/actions
→ capture intent + caller data
→ service-area / urgency / qualification rules
→ calendar/dispatch request OR human transfer
→ SMS/WhatsApp confirmation where appropriate
→ CRM writeback + outcome tag
→ QA/failure review
```

Keep outbound marketing calls out of phase 1. Use deterministic routing and approved facts; AI handles conversation/judgement within bounded rules.
