## Counter-evidence / constraints

- Voice quality, accents, latency, interruption handling and multilingual performance can materially affect trust.
- Existing human reception may already be strong, reducing incremental value.
- Generic AI receptionist capability is commoditising quickly; the moat is niche workflow, integration, QA and outcome measurement.
- Telephony/carrier cost, fair-use controls and support remain real even when AI is marketed as unlimited.
- Outbound marketing calls in the UAE introduce materially stricter compliance risk; the initial offer should remain inbound/overflow-first.

## Underwriting consequence

Do not run a generic “will businesses pay for AI receptionists?” experiment. That has strong external proof.

The DRF test is:

> **Can one UAE emergency HVAC contractor run inbound after-hours/overflow AI voice at acceptable factual/booking/transfer reliability, with fully loaded cost well below attributable booking value and support low enough for repeatable managed MRR?**

## Current DRF commercial design

- Setup/configuration: **AED3,000–7,500**.
- Managed inbound voice: **AED1,500–3,500/month** + carrier/telephony usage where not bundled.
- Higher-volume/multi-location: custom based on integrations, languages and routing complexity.
