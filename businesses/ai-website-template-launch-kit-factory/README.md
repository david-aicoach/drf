# AI Website Template & Launch Kit Factory

**Stage:** Candidate  
**Opportunity score:** 79/100

## Offer

Rapid vertical website/launch kits produced from reusable AI-assisted templates, copy, automation and deployment patterns.

## Outcome sold

A fast, professional launch for a narrow business type with much lower build time than bespoke web development.

## Commercial model

Template/setup sale plus recurring hosting, optimisation, AI operating layer or lead/reputation service; do not rely on one-off template revenue alone.

## Relationship to Business Blueprints

This remains a **separate client-implementation business**. Its primary customer pays for a deployed launch/revenue system, not merely a digital template.

Once real deployments prove that the core assets are reusable, they can become input to the separate **Business Blueprints** digital-product opportunity:

```text
client deployments
→ prove commercial outcome + high asset reuse
→ standardise reusable website/workflow assets
→ package a Business Blueprint / template product
→ distribute through Framer/Webflow + compatible general digital-product channels
→ retain iMPLEMENTAi implementation as an optional upsell
```

This creates two monetisation paths from the same proven IP without blending their economics or proof levels.
