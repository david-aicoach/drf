## Current thesis

Template/launch-kit production is extremely automatable and potentially high-margin, but the underlying sale is structurally transactional. The opportunity becomes stronger when the launch kit is the entry product into recurring hosting, optimisation, CRM/AI or visibility services.

## Supported evidence

- Modern AI tools materially compress design/copy/development time.
- Reusable vertical templates can lower marginal fulfilment cost.
- DRF has adjacent recurring services that can attach naturally.

## Key unknowns

- Paid demand and acquisition cost per vertical kit.
- Support/customisation creep.
- Recurring attach rate and retention.

## Minimum commercial test

Create three vertical kits, sell before adding more templates, and measure build hours, customisation hours, gross margin and recurring-service attach rate.
