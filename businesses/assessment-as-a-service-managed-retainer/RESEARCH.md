# Research — Assessment-as-a-Service Managed Retainer

Updated: 2026-08-29  
Issue: #45  
Research status: **Comprehensive desk research complete; recurring employer volume and consultant-minute economics require live proof**

## Executive conclusion

**Recommendation: retain at 85/100 but move materially higher in execution priority. This is one of the shortest paths from existing Talent Bridge capability to recurring revenue.**

Unlike the broader Assessment & AI Interview Platform, this offer does not require a self-service software product before revenue. Talent Bridge can sell a managed monthly assessment desk using existing licensed tools, assessment interpretation, structured candidate reports and consultant judgement. The category is externally validated by providers such as Talogy, which sells assessment access, bundles and consulting/assessment-centre services, and TestGorilla, which sells recurring assessment software.

The recurring product should solve an employer operating problem: “every candidate for these roles is assessed and reported consistently without my HR team administering tools or interpreting raw scores.”

## Product definition

> **A recurring employer assessment desk: Talent Bridge configures role-specific assessment packs, administers candidate assessments, quality-checks evidence and delivers decision-ready reports each month.**

## Service components

- role/JD calibration;
- appropriate licensed assessment selection;
- candidate invitation/reminders;
- integrity/completion monitoring;
- score normalisation and interpretation;
- structured AI-assisted synthesis;
- human consultant QA;
- client-facing candidate report;
- interview questions/follow-up recommendations;
- monthly utilisation/insight report.

## Best initial clients

- existing Talent Bridge employers with recurring hiring;
- hospitality groups;
- professional-services employers;
- finance/accounting hiring teams;
- firms without an internal psychometric specialist;
- recruitment agencies wanting outsourced assessment capability.

## Market/competition

- Talogy: validated assessment catalogue, PAYG/bundles, consulting and assessment centres;
- TestGorilla: self-service recurring assessment platform;
- HireVue: assessment + structured interview enterprise platform;
- SHL and other established assessment vendors;
- independent occupational psychologists/consultants.

The managed-service wedge is convenience + interpretation + local hiring context, not ownership of every assessment instrument.

## Commercial model hypothesis

### Monthly minimum

AED 2,500–7,500/month including a defined number of candidate assessments/reports and consultant review.

### Candidate overage

Pass through licensed assessment cost plus administration/reporting margin.

### Custom role pack

One-time fee for bespoke competency/assessment architecture where needed.

### Enterprise

Volume pricing, branded reports, ATS integration and monthly calibration review.

These are test prices; existing Talent Bridge assessment pricing should be used as the anchor when setting final commercials.

## Unit economics

Track:

```text
monthly retainer + overage
- assessment licence cost
- AI/report cost
- consultant QA minutes
- candidate support
- account management
= monthly contribution
```

The key optimisation target is consultant minutes per completed candidate without reducing evidence quality.

## GTM

Offer this to existing clients before software development:

> For a fixed monthly fee, Talent Bridge runs the assessment and candidate-evidence layer for your recurring professional hiring. Your managers receive comparable reports; your HR team does not administer multiple assessment tools.

Pilot one role family or one department.

## Defensibility

- assessment interpretation capability;
- role-specific pack library;
- reporting quality;
- GCC recruitment context;
- client calibration history;
- benchmarked candidate outcomes;
- ability to escalate into recruitment/search.

## High-stakes controls

- use only authorised/licensed assessments;
- keep assessment use job-relevant;
- human review of AI-generated interpretation;
- candidate notice/consent;
- privacy and data-retention controls;
- accommodations/accessibility route;
- no clinical claims unless instrument/professional scope supports them;
- document evidence limitations and residual gaps.

## Risks

- irregular monthly hiring volume;
- licence cost compresses margin;
- too much manual report work;
- clients want ad-hoc use rather than retainers;
- assessment vendor restrictions;
- bias/validity concerns;
- candidate drop-off;
- overlap/confusion with the future platform product.

## Evidence discipline

### Verified

- Established assessment vendors monetise both software and managed/consulting assessment services.
- Talent Bridge already has client-facing assessment/report capability.

### DRF judgement

- Managed retainer is the correct revenue-first step before building a platform.
- Existing clients are the best acquisition channel.
- Standard role packs and bounded QA are essential to margin.

### Unproven

- monthly candidate volume;
- retainer willingness-to-pay;
- consultant minutes/candidate;
- renewal after first hiring cycle.

## Validation experiment

Offer a three-month retainer to 5–10 existing employers and secure at least one paid pilot.

Measure:

- candidates assessed/month;
- completion rate;
- licence cost;
- consultant QA minutes;
- report revision requests;
- hiring-manager usefulness rating;
- gross margin;
- renewal/expansion intent.

### Pass gate

Proceed when at least one employer renews or expands and the monthly retainer remains profitable after real consultant time.

## Ranking implication

**Keep structural score at 85/100 but increase execution priority materially.** It is less autonomous than the platform opportunity, but it can produce paid recurring evidence immediately and de-risk the future platform.
