## Current thesis

Existing Talent Bridge assessment capability can be sold immediately as a recurring managed service before a self-service platform is complete. This is a faster revenue path and a proving ground for future SaaS.

## Supported evidence

- Talent Bridge already delivers assessments and employer-facing reports.
- Existing commercial/pricing history reduces offer-design risk.
- Managed delivery can generate recurring usage while platform automation matures.

## Key unknowns

- Retainer conversion and minimum monthly volume.
- Servicing burden per candidate/client.
- Churn and recurring gross margin.

## Minimum commercial test

Sell a 3-month retainer to one existing employer with a monthly minimum plus usage and record candidate volume, support hours, turnaround and margin.
