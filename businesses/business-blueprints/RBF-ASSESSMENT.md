## 8. Forward-test economics

### Capital cap

**Up to $3,000 total.**

Capital buys evidence, not activity.

### Core viability hurdle

Aim for:

- **≥30% contribution after attributable acquisition cost**;
- refund/credit rate **<10%**;
- support burden low enough to preserve digital leverage.

Each channel:

`sale price - channel fees - refunds - support - attributable CAC = channel contribution`

### Revised P3 pass — test remaining uncertainty only

Pre-write one launch price within **US$199–399**.

Pass when the bounded test produces:

- **2 unrelated paid buyers/deposits** at the chosen price or equivalent founder-approved commercial commitments;
- at least **1 buyer with genuine deployment/activation intent**;
- at least **1 buyer from a channel that is not merely a direct personal favour/relationship**;
- source/conversion/fees and attributable CAC measured where possible;
- refund signal and expected support captured;
- no fatal rights/platform issue.

This is deliberately smaller than the old five-buyer category-validation test because **EMP3 already proves the broad category**.

P4 then requires actual paid delivery/activation and actual cost capture.
