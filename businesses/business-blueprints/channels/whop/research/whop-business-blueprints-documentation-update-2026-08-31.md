## 2. Reported Blueprint-specific economics

Multiple current ecosystem sources now converge on the same Blueprint-specific economic model:

- a Blueprint creator earns **10% of Whop's profit / gross profit** attributable to businesses onboarded through the Blueprint;
- the economics are recurring while qualifying downstream business activity continues;
- the percentage is **not 10% of gross sales**.

Sources carrying the current claim include:

- https://zamantika.com/profile/eptwts
- https://www.twstalker.com/PhedEU
- https://www.instalker.org/EXM7777/status/2093083982409969866

One current report explicitly characterises the Docs clarification as **10% of Whop's profit on the clone's sales, not 10% of sales**.

### Evidence classification

**Strongly corroborated secondary reporting / pending direct Docs capture.**

The live Whop Blueprint page visibly links Docs, but the exact Docs-body text was not directly retrievable through the public web index used in this pass. DRF should therefore avoid writing "Whop officially confirms 10%" until the precise first-party text is captured directly from Whop.

### Important economic boundary

The reported formula appears narrower than the original early-access shorthand of "10% of Whop gross profit from the business". Current reporting indicates attribution may apply to qualifying sales generated through the deployed Blueprint/clone website rather than every transaction the downstream business ever processes.

That distinction matters for modelling:

```text
10% of sales                    = incorrect
10% of Whop profit on qualifying Blueprint-attributed sales = current reported model
```

Do not forecast creator revenue until the direct Docs text and actual payout ledger are captured.

## 6. AI discovery and agentic distribution

Whop now presents itself publicly as a platform to create or run businesses with AI, while its CLI already lets agents operate business functions.

This creates a credible emerging loop:

```text
AI/search discovers niche problem
→ AI evaluates explicit Blueprint description
→ user/agent deploys Blueprint
→ Whop CLI/API operates the business
→ performance data feeds optimisation
```

DRF should therefore design Blueprints with machine comprehension in mind:

- precise niche;
- measurable outcome;
- prerequisites;
- required inputs;
- explicit setup sequence;
- system boundaries;
- cost assumptions;
- validation metrics;
- failure modes;
- agent-operable instructions where appropriate.
