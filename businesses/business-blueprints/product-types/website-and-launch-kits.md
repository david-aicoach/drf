# Product Type — Website & Revenue Launch Kits

**Status:** Supporting Business Blueprint product type — **not a separate parent opportunity**  
**Parent:** [`Business Blueprints`](../README.md)  
**Taxonomy corrected:** 31 August 2026 — Issue #72

## What this is

A website / revenue launch kit is one possible **Business Blueprint SKU format**.

The valuable asset is not “an AI website”. The valuable asset is a reusable, proof-backed commercial deployment package that may include:

- niche-specific page structure;
- service/offer catalogue;
- conversion copy framework;
- landing pages;
- WhatsApp, phone or form capture;
- CRM/pipeline configuration;
- booking or quote workflow;
- tracking and attribution;
- review/reputation hooks;
- automation recipes;
- implementation instructions;
- optional localisation variants.

## Why it belongs inside Business Blueprints

The earlier DRF research established two important facts:

1. generic AI-assisted website production is heavily commoditised and is not a strong standalone moat;
2. specialist template marketplaces can still provide attractive distribution economics when the asset is a genuinely useful, reusable vertical system.

Therefore the website/template is a **component and product format**. The parent commercial logic remains:

```text
prove Outcome × Niche
→ package reusable operating IP
→ include website/launch assets when useful
→ distribute the Blueprint through compatible channels
→ offer optional iMPLEMENTAi implementation/customisation
→ measure contribution and buyer activation
```

## Product gate

A website/launch-kit SKU should not be published merely because AI can generate it quickly.

Require:

- a proven or strongly underwritten underlying business/result;
- at least **80% core asset reuse** across repeated deployments where client proof is used;
- bounded customisation and revision rules;
- a clear lead/quote/booking/revenue path;
- measurable deployment or conversion objective;
- channel-compliant rights and original assets;
- a self-deployment path or clearly priced optional implementation service.

## Channel fit

| Channel type | Role |
|---|---|
| **Framer Marketplace** | Specialist distribution when the Blueprint contains a Framer-native site/template. |
| **Webflow Templates** | Specialist distribution when the Blueprint contains a Webflow-native site/template. |
| **Whop** | Full Business Blueprint / operating-business package where appropriate. |
| **Gumroad / Payhip** | General packaged files, guides, ZIP assets and implementation materials. |
| **Contra** | Product + optional implementation/customisation bridge. |
| **Shopify / Lemon Squeezy** | Owned checkout, bundles, licences or recurring digital components where appropriate. |

Do not force the SKU onto a specialist website marketplace if no native website asset exists.

## Historical evidence retained

The pre-consolidation research found that:

- very low-cost AI website builders weaken willingness to pay for generic site creation;
- Framer and Webflow creator economics can make specialist template distribution attractive;
- the stronger commercial asset is a niche-specific conversion system rather than a brochure website;
- implementation/customisation can remain an optional iMPLEMENTAi revenue stream without becoming a separate DRF parent opportunity.

The detailed pre-consolidation wording remains available through Git history. Current DRF truth is this file plus the parent Business Blueprints documents.

## Score treatment

There is **no separate opportunity score or RBS** for this product type.

Its economics are evaluated inside:

1. the underlying Outcome × Niche proof;
2. the parent **Business Blueprints** RBF;
