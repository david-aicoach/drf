## 4. Lemon Squeezy — base fee is incomplete without effective-fee add-ons

### Current first-party facts

The base ecommerce fee remains **5% + US$0.50 per transaction** and Lemon Squeezy remains Merchant of Record.

Current fee documentation also identifies potential add-ons:

- **+1.5%** for international transactions;
- **+1.5%** for PayPal transactions;
- **+0.5%** for subscription payments;
- payout costs can apply depending on destination/method, including non-US payout fees;
- some optional marketing/affiliate/recovery features carry additional fees.

### DRF implication

`5% + US$0.50` remains the correct base anchor, but it is not always the fully loaded channel cost. For a UAE-operated Blueprint business, the live channel model must use the actual payment mix, subscription use and payout route rather than assuming the base fee is the total fee.

This reinforces Lemon Squeezy as strong **merchant-of-record checkout/software infrastructure**, not necessarily the cheapest endpoint in every geography/payment mix.
