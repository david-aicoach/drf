## Current thesis

Conversational distribution and build economics are attractive, but native recurring monetisation is less mature than API/SaaS models. The app should therefore front an independently valuable recurring backend, data product or managed service.

## Supported evidence

- Conversational app distribution can reduce interface friction.
- DRF can reuse existing workflow/agent assets.
- Build and iteration can be highly AI-assisted.

## Key unknowns

- Discovery/activation economics.
- Native platform monetisation and retention.
- Platform dependency risk.

## Minimum commercial test

Choose one workflow with an existing buyer/problem, expose it through a ChatGPT app, and charge for the backend/service rather than waiting for marketplace revenue.
