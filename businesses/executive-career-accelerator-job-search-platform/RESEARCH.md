## Current thesis

Talent Bridge already has career-package, recruitment-network, CV/profile and assessment IP that can be productised quickly. The model is attractive but naturally finite-duration, so recurring economics depend on extensions, premium support or adjacent products.

## Supported evidence

- Existing six-stage career/product assets reduce content and delivery risk.
- Talent Bridge has recruiter-market context and candidate credibility.
- Many production tasks can be automated while retaining human judgement where valuable.

## Key unknowns

- Paid acquisition cost and conversion.
- Scalable human-support burden.
- Renewal/extension behaviour after the core programme.

## Minimum commercial test

Sell one fixed digital-first cohort/package, instrument time spent by stage, completion, outcomes, referrals, upgrades and any continuation revenue.
