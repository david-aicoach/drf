## Current thesis

Snapshots, SaaS plans, automatic install, rebilling and current native AI economics support a repeatable recurring deployment model. The winning product should be one measurable vertical outcome stack, not a broad feature catalogue.

## Supported evidence

- HighLevel supports reusable snapshots and SaaS-plan deployment.
- Native recurring billing/rebilling supports agency economics.
- AI and deterministic workflow components can be packaged at location level.

## Key unknowns

- Best first vertical and willingness to pay.
- Support exceptions that break snapshot repeatability.
- CAC, churn and actual onboarding minutes.

## Minimum commercial test

Sell/deploy the same vertical outcome stack to two customers. Measure percentage of reused configuration, custom work minutes, support load, gross margin and retention intent.
