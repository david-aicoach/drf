## Current decision

**ACTIVE — `Vertical SaaS / Snapshot Business-in-a-Box — GoHighLevel Anchor` remains a first-class DRF opportunity.**

The #166 retirement applied a useful vendor-neutrality principle too aggressively. Founder direction in #169 restores the opportunity and establishes the balancing rule:

- taxonomy should improve comparison;
- shared platform/vendor/technology/outcome overlap is not enough to retire a business;
- deduplication applies to exact commercial duplicates;
- platform-led businesses remain valid when the platform materially defines packaging, recurring economics, reusable deployment, provisioning, rebilling, marketplace/distribution or buyer proposition;
- founder-origin / strategically designated core opportunities cannot be retired/merged/demoted by taxonomy cleanup without explicit founder approval unless they are provably exact commercial duplicates with all unique value preserved.

GoHighLevel is a foundational/origin DRF opportunity and is explicitly founder-protected.
