## Current thesis

The owned-media concept is highly automatable and strategically useful as distribution into Talent Bridge products. Its weakness is that direct recurring monetisation is indirect and current traffic/conversion evidence is incomplete.

- UAE job-search demand is closely aligned with Talent Bridge's candidate market.

## Key unknowns

- Current organic traffic and SEO difficulty.
- Conversion from content audience into paid products.
- Best recurring monetisation layer.

## Minimum commercial test

Resume a bounded content cadence across selected channels, drive traffic to one owned conversion page and track search impressions, visits, leads and paid downstream conversion.
