## 1. Business in money terms

### Result sold

> **Turn a qualified WhatsApp/web enquiry into a properly scoped commercial next step in minutes, then make sure every quote, revision and variation is followed until there is a decision or payment.**

This is deliberately broader and safer than “AI instant quote”. The system may give an indicative range for standard scopes, but it must force a survey/QS gate whenever drawings, site conditions, fire/acoustic specification or measurement uncertainty make an instant figure unsafe.

### Who pays

UAE specialist drywall/gypsum/false-ceiling contractors and small fit-out contractors with recurring quote volume, repeated scope patterns and meaningful WhatsApp/email intake.

### Revenue streams

| Revenue stream | Buyer pays for | Type | Current test range |
|---|---|---|---:|
| Implementation | Rate library, intake, quote workflow, CRM/pipeline and training | Upfront sale | AED 3,000–10,000 |
| Managed quote-to-cash layer | Workflow monitoring, controlled improvements, support and reporting | Recurring fee | AED 1,000–3,500/month |
| Earned modules | Recovery, WhatsApp CRM foundation, tender/RFQ or collections modules | Recurring fee / fixed project | Pending by module |

These are existing DRF pricing hypotheses, not realised prices.

### Delivery mechanism

```text
WhatsApp / website / form
→ structured scope intake
→ controlled rate library / rule logic
→ indicative range or mandatory survey gate
→ CRM opportunity
→ estimator approval where required
→ quotation
→ deterministic follow-up / revision / variation
→ ERP/accounting/project system when won
```

The result is the business. AI, HighLevel, an ERP or another tool is a replaceable implementation layer.

## 5. Backtest and economic sensitivity

### Existing quote-delay scenario from the niche dossier

Illustrative only:

```text
40 qualified enquiries/month
× AED 12,000 average quoted value
× 20% historical close probability
= AED 96,000 expected monthly booked value

If 10% of expected wins are lost through avoidable quote delay/follow-up:
≈ AED 9,600/month influenced booking value
```

This is not a typical-contractor claim. It is a sensitivity showing why a **AED1,000–3,500/month** managed price may be rational when client data confirms comparable quote leakage.

### Estimator-time scenario

The existing dossier also models **40 enquiries × 30 minutes** repetitive first-pass intake = **20 estimator hours/month**. Cutting only the repetitive half saves about **10 hours/month**, before any conversion uplift.

### Margin guardrail

The steady-state target is **70%+ gross margin after support**, excluding initial product development. If iMPLEMENTAi has to manually estimate every quote, the business fails its repeatability test.

## 6. Forward-test card

### Hypothesis

A qualified Dubai gypsum/drywall specialist will commit money to a safe, productised quote-to-cash workflow if it demonstrably shortens repetitive intake/quotation work while preserving estimator control.

### Offer tested

- Implementation: **minimum AED 4,000**.
- Managed layer: **minimum AED 1,250/month**.
- Pilot deposit: **AED 1,000**, credited to implementation.
- First diagnostic: replay a sample of historic quotations against structured intake/rules before live automation.

### Audience

Up to **50 qualified UAE specialist contractors**, priority Dubai.

### Pass threshold for P3

Within 30 days:

- at least **6 qualified workflow/quote audits**;
- historical quote replay on at least **20 quotes** from one willing contractor or an equivalent anonymised sample;
- at least **2 paid AED1,000 pilot deposits** against the same minimum offer;
- test spend stays within **$3,000 Tier 1 maximum**;
- founder time remains **≤ 60 hours**;
- standard-scope replay establishes a usable safe-automation boundary rather than pretending every project can be instantly priced.

### Safety/accuracy gate

A live pilot must never let a general agent invent technical specifications or uncontrolled rates. Any low-confidence, fire/acoustic, complex BOQ, hidden-condition or measurement-sensitive scope must route to survey/estimator review.

### Recycle / fail threshold

Recycle if contractors value the workflow but the first wedge should be intake/follow-up rather than automated pricing. Kill this niche/version if historical replay shows standard scopes cannot be bounded safely or if buyers will not pay enough to support the required estimator involvement.
