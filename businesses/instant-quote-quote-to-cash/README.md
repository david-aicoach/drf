# Instant Quote Generator & Quote-to-Cash System

**Stage:** Candidate  
**Opportunity score:** 91/100

## Offer

Turn structured customer inputs into an immediate rules-based quote, then move the customer through estimate, contract, payment and booking.

## Outcome sold

Shorter time-to-quote, higher quote completion, more booked/paid jobs and less administrative quoting labour.

## Delivery shape

Forms/calculators and conditional logic stay deterministic; CRM owns customer/opportunity state; AI is used only where free-text interpretation or assistance adds value.

## Commercial model

Setup plus recurring platform/optimisation support; potentially paired with payment or lead-conversion services.

## Next action

Build one rules-based quote flow in a simple service vertical and measure completion, booking/payment conversion and time-to-quote.
