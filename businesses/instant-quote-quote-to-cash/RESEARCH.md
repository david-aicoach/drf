## Current thesis

HighLevel calculators, conditional logic, estimate sending, contracts and payments make a largely deterministic quote-to-cash flow technically available with limited AI-cost exposure. The main risk is niche-specific quote logic and commercial proof, not core automation.

## Supported evidence

- Required form/calculator, estimate, contract and payment primitives exist.
- Deterministic quoting can be audited and tested before AI is introduced.
- Quote speed and conversion are directly measurable.

## Key unknowns

- Which verticals have sufficiently structured pricing rules.
- Accuracy/exception rate once real customer inputs arrive.
- Subscription retention after initial implementation.

## Minimum commercial test

Choose a vertical with bounded price rules. Compare manual versus automated time-to-quote, completion, acceptance, booking/payment conversion and exception handling minutes.
