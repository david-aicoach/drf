## Counter-evidence and pressure on the DRF offer

The category is **not** an empty software gap.

- **Drywall Quote** is free and provides finish-level, material-takeoff and PDF quote capability.
- **Trowla** offers free, no-sign-up contractor estimates and branded PDFs.
- Low-cost specialist tools and mature construction/FSM suites compress willingness to pay for a generic calculator or standalone quote app.
- Existing ERP/estimating software can already solve parts of the workflow for better-run contractors.

Therefore DRF should not sell “drywall estimating software”. The stronger offer is the **implemented quote-to-cash operating layer** around the contractor's own controlled rates, enquiry channels, survey/estimator gates, CRM opportunity, quote follow-up, revisions/variations and payment/next action.

## EMP judgement

### Why EMP3

The evidence clears EMP3 because:

1. multiple independent operators sell materially comparable recurring/usage products;
2. direct drywall/drylining products exist alongside broader construction estimating suites;
3. recurring prices span low-cost trade software through hundreds of US dollars per month for broader construction platforms;
4. current products cover estimating → quotation/proposal → customer action, not merely calculation;
5. counter-evidence and substitution pressure were explicitly reviewed.

### Why not EMP4

EMP4 would overstate transferability. Missing:

- recurring price acceptance from UAE drywall/gypsum contractors;
- local rate-library maintenance cost;
- percentage of common enquiries safe to quote without site survey/QS review;
- actual quote acceptance/contribution uplift;
- fully loaded implementation/support minutes;
- DRF acquisition cost and repeatability.

## Commercial implication

Keep the existing test economics rather than raising price because the category is proven:

- implementation test floor: **AED4,000**;
- managed layer test floor: **AED1,250/month**;
- pilot deposit: **AED1,000 credited to implementation**.

Existing V3 range of AED3,000–10,000 setup and AED1,000–3,500/month remains a reasonable hypothesis, not realised pricing.

## Critical test-scope reduction

Strong external proof means DRF should **not** spend a pilot proving that contractors pay for quoting/estimating tools or that digital quoting exists.

The next proof targets only the largest DRF-specific uncertainty:

> Can one UAE drywall/gypsum contractor safely standardise a meaningful share of repeated quote intake/rates, reduce quote cycle time and support effort, and accept the pre-committed DRF price with a credible path to a second materially similar client?

### P3 threshold

Within one bounded local test:

- one qualified UAE contractor provides **20+ historic quotes** or an equivalent representative set;
- define a pre-written “safe auto/range vs mandatory survey/estimator” rule before replay;
- replay shows a useful standardisable share with low correction/error burden;
- obtain **2 unrelated paid AED1,000 pilot deposits** at the same minimum commercial terms, or equivalent founder-approved current-market commitments;
- measure quote turnaround, exception rate, corrections, support minutes and expected contribution;
- no uncontrolled rate/specification generation.

DRF Proof remains **P2 Backtested** until qualifying current-market evidence occurs.
