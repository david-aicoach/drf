## Current thesis

Existing web, AI and HighLevel capability makes local lead assets capital-efficient and operationally automatable. The model is attractive because successful assets can become recurring rentable properties, but every niche/location must prove ranking time and lead economics.

## Supported evidence

- DRF already has web/automation/CRM capabilities needed for fulfilment.
- A ranked asset can generate recurring leads without linear labour.
- The renter model creates recurring revenue and potential asset resale value.

## Key unknowns

- Time/cost to rank per UAE niche.
- Qualified lead volume and renter economics.
- Search/AI-discovery volatility and asset retention.

## Minimum commercial test

Launch one tightly scoped niche/location site, track ranking and qualified leads, and do not clone until one operator pays recurring rent from proven lead flow.
