## Current thesis

Missed-call text back, multichannel response, AI qualification, calendars, reminders and no-show rescue can protect acquisition spend at low marginal labour. Current native AI economics strengthen delivery viability.

## Supported evidence

- HighLevel supports the required response, calendar and lifecycle primitives.
- Much of the workflow is deterministic and therefore cheap and reliable.
- Value is measurable against existing inbound lead volume and booking conversion.

## Key unknowns

- Incremental booking uplift by niche.
- Channel-specific response and opt-out behaviour.
- Retail pricing and retention once the initial uplift stabilises.

## Minimum commercial test

Use one high-lead-volume account and compare baseline versus automated response/contact/booking metrics, including fully loaded AI/messaging/support cost.
