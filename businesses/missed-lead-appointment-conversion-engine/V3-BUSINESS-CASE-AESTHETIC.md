## Founder summary

Sell a measurable **lead-conversion overlay** that catches missed calls and slow/unanswered inbound enquiries, responds immediately on the appropriate channel, qualifies only what is needed, offers booking/callback, manages reminders/no-show rescue and keeps every outcome visible in the CRM.

External evidence already establishes the category. DRF does not need to prove that immediate automated follow-up can save missed leads or book appointments. The remaining question is the incremental uplift and contribution in a Dubai aesthetic clinic, under tighter patient-communication and brand constraints.

## GTM

Lead with a **conversion leakage audit**:

- inbound calls/messages/forms per week;
- missed/unanswered rate;
- median response time;
- contact rate;
- booked consultation rate;
- show rate;
- downstream conversion/gross contribution where available.

The pitch is: **convert more of the demand the clinic already pays to generate**, not “install AI”.

## Delivery architecture

```text
inbound call / WhatsApp / form / social lead
→ missed/no-response condition
→ consent/DND/channel-policy gate
→ immediate bounded acknowledgement
→ minimal qualification
→ booking/callback path
→ owner assignment + reminders
→ human escalation for clinical/sensitive questions
→ no-show/stale rescue
→ CRM outcome + attribution
```

Keep deterministic workflow for timing/routing. Use AI only where conversation materially improves qualification or booking. Do not allow medical advice, unsupported treatment claims or policy improvisation.
