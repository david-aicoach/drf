## Counter-evidence / constraints

- Automation cannot fix poor-quality acquisition or weak offers.
- Businesses with already-fast human response have less headroom.
- Duplicate outreach, aggressive cadence and poor consent handling can damage customer experience.
- Aesthetic/medical contexts require stricter approved claims, privacy discipline and human escalation.
- Booking lift is not the same as realised revenue; show rate, treatment suitability, close rate and gross contribution matter.
- Long-term MRR depends on continued lead volume and ongoing optimisation, not merely fixing one missed-call workflow once.
