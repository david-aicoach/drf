## Current thesis

Talent Bridge can retain the high-value client, commercial and evaluation layer while outsourcing sourcing/delivery to a capable partner. This improves founder/team leverage, though the underlying revenue remains more placement-driven than pure MRR.

## Supported evidence

- Talent Bridge already has client and recruitment capability.
- Partner delivery directly removes labour-heavy fulfilment work.
- Quality can be governed by role briefs, assessment/evaluation standards and SLA controls.

## Key unknowns

- Partner consistency and speed.
- Margin split after replacements/failures.
- Client ownership, non-circumvention and repeat-role economics.

## Minimum commercial test

Run one real vacancy end to end under a written SLA and measure internal hours saved, shortlist quality, time-to-fill, gross margin and client experience.
