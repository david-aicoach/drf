## Current thesis

Talent Bridge's candidate ecosystem plus assessment/evaluation capability could become recurring employer access to fresh, pre-assessed supply. The hard parts are permission, freshness and recurring demand, not assessment production.

## Supported evidence

- Existing candidate/recruitment ecosystem provides a starting supply source.
- Talent Bridge can add differentiated screening/assessment evidence.
- Employer value is direct if time-to-shortlist falls materially.

## Key unknowns

- Candidate consent and profile freshness.
- Employer subscription willingness and access frequency.
- Operational cost of maintaining current availability/data.

## Minimum commercial test

Create a consented 100-candidate pool in one role family, keep freshness dates explicit and offer paid access to a small set of employers before scaling supply.
