# Research — Pre-Assessed Talent Pool Subscription

Updated: 2026-08-29  
Issue: #45  
Research status: **Comprehensive desk research complete; UAE/GCC supply freshness and employer subscription demand remain unproven**

## Executive conclusion

**Recommendation: retain at 86/100 and keep Stage = Researching until Talent Bridge proves a narrow, permissioned, continuously fresh talent pool that employers will pay to access repeatedly.**

The category is validated. Wellfound Curated explicitly sells access to vetted, ready-to-interview candidates and refreshes the pool twice weekly. TestGorilla also supports skills-tested candidate profiles and assessment-led talent discovery. The lesson is that employers do value pre-qualified talent, but the commercial product cannot be a static CV database. Its value must come from candidate freshness, evidence quality, role specificity and faster time-to-interview.

Talent Bridge has a credible wedge because recruitment already creates candidate relationships, screening evidence and assessment capability. The best first product is a narrow UAE/GCC pool for one recurring professional role family rather than a broad marketplace.

## Buyer problem

Employers repeatedly lose time on sourcing, CV screening and first-stage qualification. The strongest buyer need is not “more candidates”; it is access to a small set of relevant people whose availability, compensation expectations, key skills and assessment evidence are current enough to support an immediate interview decision.

## Product definition

> **A subscription talent pool of permissioned, recently verified UAE/GCC candidates in a narrow role family, with structured screening and assessment evidence that lets employers move directly to interview.**

Candidate records should have an expiry/reverification clock. “Pre-assessed” must state exactly what was assessed and when.

## Best first role families

Use roles where Talent Bridge already has repeat employer demand and evaluation competence, for example:

- finance/accounting managers and controllers;
- HR managers/HRBPs;
- hospitality finance roles;
- selected sales/business-development roles;
- selected engineering/commercial roles.

Do not launch multiple verticals until one pool produces repeated employer interviews.

## Operating model

```text
candidate opt-in
  ↓
CV/profile normalisation
  ↓
structured screening + availability/salary check
  ↓
licensed assessment / structured interview where relevant
  ↓
human QA
  ↓
permissioned employer profile
  ↓
weekly/biweekly freshness check
  ↓
interview request / ATS handoff
```

## Competitive landscape

- Wellfound Curated: vetted, ready-to-interview candidates with frequent refresh;
- Wellfound Reach/Autopilot: AI sourcing and managed recruiting;
- TestGorilla: skills assessment and skills-tested candidate profiles;
- LinkedIn Recruiter/job boards;
- recruitment agencies and internal talent pools.

The moat therefore cannot be access to CVs. It must be GCC relevance + freshness + evidence + responsiveness.

## Commercial model hypothesis

### Employer subscription

AED 1,500–5,000/month for access to one or more defined role pools, with search/profile views/interview requests included within limits.

### Premium/success layer

Optional placement/success fee when Talent Bridge manages engagement, negotiation or hiring.

### Enterprise

Private branded pool, custom assessments and recurring talent mapping.

These are test prices only.

## Unit economics

Track:

- candidate acquisition/reverification cost;
- assessment/licensing cost;
- human QA minutes/candidate;
- percentage of profiles still available after 30/60/90 days;
- employer searches/profile views;
- interviews per active candidate;
- hires;
- monthly support/account;
- contribution margin per subscriber.

Freshness is the major recurring operating cost.

## GTM

Build supply only after choosing the first employer segment. Secure 3–5 employers willing to review a defined pool, then recruit/assess candidates against that exact role profile.

Sell:

> A continuously refreshed shortlist of interview-ready UAE [role family] candidates with structured screening and assessment evidence—not a CV database.

## Defensibility

- permissioned candidate relationships;
- freshness/availability operations;
- role-specific assessment evidence;
- GCC salary/notice-period context;
- employer feedback improving calibration;
- hiring-outcome history;
- integration with Talent Bridge search/evaluation services.

## Risks

- stale candidate availability;
- candidate consent/privacy failures;
- employers bypassing Talent Bridge;
- low employer usage between hiring cycles;
- assessment licensing restrictions;
- candidate pool too small or homogeneous;
- supply built before demand;
- job boards/ATSs improving verified talent discovery.

## Evidence discipline

### Verified

- Wellfound sells vetted, ready-to-interview talent access and refreshes Curated candidates frequently.
- TestGorilla supports skills-based assessment/profile evidence.
- Talent Bridge already has candidate and employer relationships.

### DRF judgement

- Freshness is more important than database size.
- One UAE/GCC role family should be proven before marketplace breadth.
- Subscription must be employer-led; do not build supply speculatively.

### Unproven

- number of permissioned candidates available per role;
- employer willingness to subscribe;
- monthly usage/renewal;
- candidate-to-interview and interview-to-hire rates.

## Validation experiment

Choose one role family. Build a permissioned pool of 20–50 candidates with current availability and structured evidence. Offer it to at least five existing employers for 30–60 days.

Measure:

- employer logins/searches/profile views;
- interview requests;
- candidate response/availability rate;
- time from employer request to interview;
- hires;
- candidate reverification workload;
- paid conversion/renewal intent.

### Pass gate

Proceed when at least 2–3 employers pay or commit to recurring access and the pool generates real interviews without unsustainable reverification labour.

## Ranking implication

**Keep 86/100.** External category proof supports the model, but local two-sided liquidity remains the gating evidence.
