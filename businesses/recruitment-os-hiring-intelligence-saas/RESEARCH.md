## Current thesis

Talent Bridge already owns much of the process IP and real recruitment operating context. The opportunity is strongest as an intelligence/evaluation layer around existing ATS systems, not as another broad ATS rebuild.

## Supported evidence

- Internal recruitment workflows and evidence standards already exist.
- Existing ATS/candidate systems can remain systems of record.
- AI can remove repeated screening/reporting work while humans retain consequential hiring judgement.

## Key unknowns

- Which fixed module buyers will pay for first.
- External integration/support burden.
- Retention and scope discipline versus bespoke consulting.

## Minimum commercial test

Use the system internally until one module is stable, then sell that exact module to one boutique agency/HR team and measure setup time, weekly usage and support burden.
