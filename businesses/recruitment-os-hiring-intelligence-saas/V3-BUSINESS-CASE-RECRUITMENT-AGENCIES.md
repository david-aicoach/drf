## 1. Business definition

### Result sold

> **Install the hiring-intelligence layer around an agency's existing ATS so role calibration, interview evidence, shortlist comparison and client decision packs become faster and more consistent without migrating systems.**

### Payer

Founder/MD, recruitment director, delivery/operations leader or RPO lead at a UAE/GCC boutique recruitment/search business with roughly 5–50 recruiters and recurring professional vacancies.

### Revenue model

- setup/integration / reusable role templates: **AED1,500–5,000**;
- bounded managed intelligence module: **AED1,500–3,500/month**;
- candidate/assessment usage separate where relevant;
- optional assessment evidence supplied by the separate Talent Bridge Assessment & AI Interview Platform.

These are test hypotheses, not realised DRF pricing.

## 2. Product boundary

### Included

- role success profile/rubric;
- structured screening/interview templates;
- interview-note and candidate-evidence normalisation;
- evidence-completeness / next-action controls;
- comparative shortlist/client decision pack;
- ATS/email integration/writeback where reliable;
- recruiter approval before client-facing output.

### Excluded

- ATS/database replacement;
- autonomous candidate hire/reject decisions;
- duplicating licensed assessment/AI-interview product functionality;
- open-ended AI transformation consulting;
- bespoke portal building before the core module proves renewal.

## 3. External proof

EMP3 is supported by multiple current operators:

- **Metaview / The Bowdoin Group** — direct staffing/recruiting search-firm case, 25 Mar 2026, using custom interview notes and AI sourcing to scale search without scaling headcount and improve consistency/client deliverables.
- **Metaview / SoSafe** — current interview intelligence/scorecard evidence integrated into Ashby with large recruiter-time savings reported by the customer.
- **Metaview / Lemonade** — 15 Jul 2026 case reports lower sourcing cost and materially higher recruiter output.
- **IntelligencesTest** — agency-specific candidate-evaluation/client-report subscriptions at current $49/$109/$219 monthly founding tiers.
- **Manatal** — mature low-cost ATS/AI/workflow substitute at $15/$35/$55 per user/month billed annually.
- **Screenloop / Equitas** — Jan 2026 acquisition demonstrates interview-intelligence category consolidation.

Counter-evidence is material: generic ATS/AI features are cheap and direct agency evidence platforms already exist. The commercial wedge must therefore be **workflow/evidence implementation and client-ready operating intelligence**, not feature parity.

Detailed source: `research/external-market-proof-calibration-2026-09-02.md`.

## 5. GTM

1. Use existing Talent Bridge/recruitment relationships first.
2. Offer a one-role workflow/evidence benchmark against the agency's current ATS/process.
3. Sell one fixed module, not a platform transformation.
4. Keep the existing ATS as system of record.
5. Require real shortlist/client use plus renewal or second-role reuse before expanding modules.

Initial positioning:

> Keep your ATS. We install the hiring-intelligence layer that standardises role calibration, interview evidence and client shortlist packs so recruiters spend less time on administration and deliver more consistent evidence.

## 6. Delivery architecture

```text
existing ATS / email / call transcripts
        ↓
structured role + evidence schema
        ↓
deterministic extraction / workflow / completeness rules
        ↓
bounded AI synthesis
        ↓
human recruiter review
        ↓
client-ready comparative output
        ↓
ATS writeback + next action
```

Use native/API/MCP integration before browser automation. Candidate decisions remain human-reviewed.

## 8. Return Profile

No DRF revenue actual is claimed.

### Illustrative base case

`5 agencies × AED2,500/month = AED12,500 MRR`, before usage/setup revenue.

This is a scenario only. It does not establish achievable CAC, churn or support cost.

### Main cost drivers

- human QA/reviewer minutes;
- ATS integration/configuration;
- AI/API/transcription cost;
- client-specific template maintenance;
- support and exception handling;
- licensed assessment cost only when the separate assessment product is attached.

### Downside

If onboarding becomes bespoke, recruiters do not use the outputs with real clients, or human QA/support consumes the subscription contribution, do not expand the product.

### Capital

**US$0 new platform build before a paid external module commitment.** If a committed pilot requires a bounded integration cost, up to **US$3,000 Tier 1** may be considered with founder approval.

## 10. Next Proof

One unrelated UAE/GCC recruitment agency, one recurring professional role family, one fixed intelligence module around its existing ATS.

Pass only if:

- buyer pays or makes equivalent founder-approved current-market commercial commitment;
- onboarding is bounded and no ATS migration is required;
- at least one real client-facing shortlist/decision pack is used;
- recruiter time or evidence quality improves measurably;
- human QA/support remains within target contribution;
- buyer intends renewal or second-role reuse.

Fail/recycle if the agency mainly wants generic ATS features, a custom portal or the assessment product already covers the actual pain.

## 11. Current read
