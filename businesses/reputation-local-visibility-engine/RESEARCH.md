## Current thesis

Reviews AI and Content AI can support low-marginal-cost fulfilment while listings/GBP work remains naturally recurring. The unresolved question is not capability but whether a niche sees enough lead/reputation uplift to sustain monthly retention.

## Supported evidence

- Current HighLevel features cover reviews, content and recurring listings operations.
- Review recency and local profile upkeep are ongoing rather than one-off needs.
- AI can automate much of reply/content production.

## Key unknowns

- Incremental lead impact by niche/location.
- Retail price and churn.
- Human support required for profile/listing exceptions.

## Minimum commercial test

Use one niche/location, establish review/GBP/lead baselines, run the managed service for a bounded period and measure review velocity, profile actions, leads and support cost.
