# Revenue Recovery & Reactivation Engine

**Stage:** Candidate  
**Opportunity score:** 94/100

## Offer

Recover revenue already sitting inside a business: abandoned checkouts, failed payments, dormant leads/customers and stale opportunities.

## Customer and pain

Businesses with meaningful paid acquisition, existing databases, subscriptions, quotes or pipelines that leak revenue because follow-up is inconsistent or manual.

## Outcome sold

Incremental recovered contribution and conversion from existing demand without proportionally increasing acquisition spend.

## Delivery shape

- Customer channel: WhatsApp, SMS, email and voice as appropriate.
- System of record: CRM/payment/e-commerce platform with one canonical lifecycle state.
- Delivery layer: deterministic workflows first; native AI for qualification/conversation; external agents only for exceptions.

## Commercial model

Setup/onboarding fee plus recurring managed recovery subscription, optionally with a performance component where attribution is clean.

## Next action

Run one controlled recovery campaign against an existing database/order base and measure recovered contribution after messaging, AI and support cost.
