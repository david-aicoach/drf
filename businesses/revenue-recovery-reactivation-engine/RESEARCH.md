## Current thesis

HighLevel can automate abandoned-checkout, failed-payment/dunning, dormant-database and stale-opportunity recovery. Native workflows and AI make high-volume execution economically attractive; general computer-use agents should not be used for every repetitive recovery event.

## Supported evidence

- Recovery triggers and lifecycle workflows are available in the current HighLevel stack.
- Native AI economics reduce the marginal cost of high-volume conversation and follow-up.
- The offer ties directly to measurable recovered revenue, making ROI easier to evidence than generic automation.

## Key unknowns

- DRF recovery rate by niche and trigger.
- True contribution after messaging, AI and human exceptions.
- Client churn and willingness to share clean attribution data.

## Minimum commercial test

Use one existing client/database. Establish a holdout or pre-campaign baseline, run a bounded recovery sequence, and measure recovered gross contribution, cost per recovered customer and human intervention minutes.
