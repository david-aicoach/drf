## Counter-evidence / constraints

- Stripe and other billing processors already solve failed-payment retries well; DRF must not duplicate native dunning where the processor is already effective.
- Recovery value decays when lists are old, data is dirty, consent is unclear or staff already follow up consistently.
- Gross recovered revenue can overstate value; DRF must measure **incremental gross contribution after delivery cost**.
- A successful first backlog campaign can exhaust a one-time pool. MRR depends on recurring quote, renewal and lapsed-customer states replenishing every month.
- Attribution can be contested when client staff run parallel follow-up.

## Underwriting consequence

Broad-category validation is complete enough for P2 desk/backtest treatment. The live test should not ask “does revenue recovery work?” It should ask:

> **Can DRF recover enough incremental gross contribution from one clean UAE HVAC recovery trigger to support a recurring AED1,000–2,500/month managed service, with bounded support and credible attribution?**

## Current commercial design

- **Recovery Audit:** AED1,000–2,500, creditable against deployment.
- **Controlled campaign:** AED1,500–4,000 + direct usage.
- **Managed recovery:** AED1,000–2,500/month + direct usage.
- **Optional performance fee:** 5–15% of clearly attributable recovered gross profit when baseline and attribution are robust.

These are DRF test prices informed by current niche research; they are not claimed as market averages.
