## Current thesis

The project has strong niche/community logic and plausible listings, sponsorship and referral revenue. Commercialisation must not outrun community trust; traffic and engagement are the gating evidence before paid marketplace expansion.

## Supported evidence

- A defined South African/UAE community creates clearer positioning than a generic directory.
- Community content, local discovery and business listings are mutually reinforcing when trust is preserved.
- Research/content/directory maintenance can be substantially automated.

## Key unknowns

- Current traffic and active engagement.
- Local business willingness to pay and renew.
- Monetisation formats that do not damage trust.

## Minimum commercial test

Measure real engagement first, then offer one simple paid listing/sponsor package to a small set of relevant businesses and track conversion, response and community feedback.
