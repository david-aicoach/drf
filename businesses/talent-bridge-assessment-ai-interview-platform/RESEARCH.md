# Research — Talent Bridge Assessment & AI Interview Platform

Updated: 2026-08-29  
Issue: #45  
Research status: **Comprehensive desk research complete; self-service employer demand and recurring candidate economics still require live proof**

## Executive conclusion

**Recommendation: retain at 91/100 and treat this as one of the strongest proprietary-IP opportunities in the portfolio, but do not compete as another generic test library.**

The category is validated. TestGorilla sells subscription assessment software from a self-serve Core tier through higher-priced AI interview/customisation plans; HireVue combines assessments, simulations and structured video interviews; Talogy sells validated assessments through pay-as-you-go, bundles and consulting-led assessment centres, including in the GCC. Employers therefore already pay for online assessment, structured interviewing and interpretation.

Talent Bridge has a stronger starting point than a greenfield software founder because the business already has candidate evaluation workflows, psychometric/skills assessment experience, recruiter judgement, report formats, employer relationships and live pricing history. The defensible opportunity is to turn that operating IP into a repeatable employer assessment layer for UAE/GCC hiring—not to recreate hundreds of commodity tests.

The largest unresolved risks are validity, data rights/licensing, adverse-impact/fairness, candidate privacy, human oversight of AI-generated evaluation and whether employers will buy recurring access rather than ad-hoc consulting.

## Market and buyer problem

Hiring teams pay for three related outcomes:

1. reduce low-quality screening labour;
2. evaluate job-relevant competencies more consistently;
3. give hiring managers evidence that is easier to compare than CVs and unstructured interviews.

The software market demonstrates willingness to pay, but it is crowded. Test libraries and asynchronous video interviews are already widely available. Talent Bridge must therefore sell **role-specific decision intelligence** built on assessment evidence and professional judgement.

## Competitor evidence

### TestGorilla

Current public pricing shows:

- Free tier;
- Core at **US$142/month with annual commitment**;
- Plus **starting around US$400/month with annual commitment**;
- 350+ tests;
- customised AI interviews/scoring and job simulations in higher plans;
- ID verification, API/ATS integrations and unbranded assessment experience on Plus;
- AI interviews consume candidate credits.

Its candidate documentation describes AI interviews as structured interviews typically lasting around 10–20 minutes.

### HireVue

HireVue combines online tests, job simulations, structured video interviews, Virtual Job Tryout and AI-scored interview products. This validates enterprise demand for multimodal structured selection.

### Talogy

Talogy offers 500+ validated assessments, pay-as-you-go purchasing, bundles and volume options without necessarily requiring a long contract for smaller users. Its GCC/UAE presence and Saville Wave offering demonstrate that validated psychometric assessment is an established regional category, not merely a Western SaaS pattern.

## Talent Bridge differentiation

Potential moat:

- UAE/GCC role calibration and salary/recruitment context;
- recruiter-led evidence synthesis rather than raw percentile output;
- role-specific weighted fit scorecards;
- professional branded client reports;
- combined psychometric + skills + structured interview evidence;
- candidate integrity observations;
- follow-up clarification workflow;
- ability to attach live search/recruitment when assessment reveals a poor slate;
- proprietary benchmark data accumulated from consented assessment outcomes.

## Product definition

The buyer should be able to choose a role, invite candidates and receive a structured decision package.

```text
role/JD intake
   ↓
role competency + assessment configuration
   ↓
candidate invitation
   ↓
skills / cognitive / behavioural assessments where licensed
   ↓
structured asynchronous AI-assisted interview
   ↓
integrity + completion checks
   ↓
AI evidence synthesis
   ↓
human-reviewed fit report / shortlist recommendation
   ↓
employer dashboard + audit trail
```

The platform must preserve a human decision-maker. AI should assist evidence collection, scoring against explicit rubrics and report preparation; it should not silently make final employment decisions.

## Initial product wedge

Do not start broad. Use one high-frequency professional role family where Talent Bridge already understands competencies and hiring signals—for example finance/accounting, HR, sales/business development or operations.

Package:

- role-specific assessment bundle;
- structured AI interview;
- fit scorecard;
- two-page employer report;
- optional recruiter review;
- optional live reference/clarification stage.

## Commercial model hypothesis

Current Talent Bridge historical pricing provides a premium service anchor, while SaaS competitors demonstrate lower self-service pricing.

Test a hybrid ladder:

### Self-service employer subscription

AED 750–1,500/month including a candidate allowance and standard reports.

### Professional plan

AED 2,500–5,000/month including higher candidate volume, customised role packs, AI interviews and monthly consultant review.

### Usage overage

Per-candidate assessment/licensing costs passed through or marked up transparently.

### Enterprise/custom

Role architecture, custom validation, API/ATS, white-label portal and high-volume contracts.

These are research hypotheses, not approved Talent Bridge pricing.

## Delivery economics

Cost stack:

```text
assessment licence / candidate
+ AI interview/media cost
+ platform/hosting
+ AI synthesis
+ consultant QA/review
+ support
= cost per completed candidate
```

Recurring economics become compelling only if consultant review is bounded and standard role packs can be reused.

## Regulatory, ethical and quality controls

Employment assessment is a high-stakes domain. Required controls include:

- use only assessments Talent Bridge is licensed/authorised to administer;
- maintain clear job relevance for each assessed dimension;
- document scoring rubrics and version changes;
- human review of AI-generated conclusions;
- avoid protected-characteristic inference;
- candidate notice/consent and transparent data use;
- retention/deletion policy;
- access controls and audit trail;
- accommodation/accessibility path;
- ongoing review for disparate or unintended outcomes;
- never market an AI model's output as clinical diagnosis.

## GTM

Start from existing employer relationships rather than paid cold acquisition.

Offer one employer:

> A fixed assessment and structured interview pack for [role], giving you comparable evidence on every shortlisted candidate before manager interview.

The sales proof is time saved, quality of shortlist decision and hiring-manager confidence—not number of tests.

## Defensibility

Commodity assessment content is weak IP unless licensed/proprietary. Stronger moat:

- role pack library;
- local benchmark data;
- human-reviewed decision framework;
- employer-specific calibration history;
- integrated recruitment workflows;
- reporting quality;
- validated candidate experience;
- proprietary outcome feedback loops (hire/performance/retention where clients lawfully provide it).

## Key risks

- assessment licensing restrictions;
- weak scientific validity or role relevance;
- bias/adverse outcomes;
- employer over-reliance on a score;
- candidate resistance to AI interviews;
- video/storage privacy burden;
- ATS competitors bundling similar functions;
- too much consultant work per candidate;
- insufficient candidate volume for subscription economics.

## Evidence discipline

### Verified

- TestGorilla, HireVue and Talogy sell assessment/interview solutions, validating buyer spend.
- Self-service subscription, usage-credit, PAYG and consulting models all exist.
- Talent Bridge already has assessment/evaluation operations and client relationships, per canonical internal research.

### DRF judgement

- Talent Bridge should differentiate through UAE/GCC role intelligence and human-reviewed evidence synthesis.
- A hybrid managed/SaaS product is lower risk than fully autonomous screening.
- One role family should be proven before platform breadth.

### Unproven

- employer subscription willingness at proposed UAE price points;
- candidate completion rate for AI interview + assessment bundle;
- consultant QA minutes per candidate at scale;
- retention/renewal frequency;
- benchmark-data sufficiency.

## Validation experiment

Run one employer pilot across one live role family with at least 10–20 candidates if volume allows.

Measure:

- invite-to-completion rate;
- assessment/interview completion time;
- support incidents;
- consultant minutes/candidate;
- cost/completed candidate;
- employer time saved;
- employer agreement with shortlist ranking;
- interview-to-offer/hire progression;
- employer willingness to pay for the next role/month;
- candidate experience/complaints.

### Pass gate

Proceed when the employer pays or explicitly commits to recurring use, consultant QA remains bounded, candidate completion is healthy and no material validity/privacy failure emerges.

## Ranking implication

**91/100 remains appropriate.** External category validation is strong enough that evidence confidence can reasonably rise after this pass, but the commercial score should not increase until recurring employer use and per-candidate gross margin are demonstrated.
