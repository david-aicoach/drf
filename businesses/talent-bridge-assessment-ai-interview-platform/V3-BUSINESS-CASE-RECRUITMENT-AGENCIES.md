## Offer

### White-Label Candidate Evidence Pack

For one role family:
1. role/JD calibration and explicit scoring rubric;
2. licensed assessment bundle where appropriate;
3. structured asynchronous AI-assisted interview;
4. candidate integrity/completion checks;
5. evidence synthesis against the rubric;
6. bounded human QA;
7. comparative recruiter/client-ready report;
8. optional recruiter clarification / live escalation.

The agency keeps its ATS and client. Talent Bridge provides the evidence engine.

## Pricing / commercial model

**Desk hypothesis — not approved public pricing.**

- **Agency Starter:** AED1,500–2,500/month minimum, including a defined candidate allowance and standard evidence pack.
- **Professional / white-label:** AED3,000–5,000/month, including higher volume, role-pack customisation and branded reports.
- **Usage:** additional candidate credits; licensed third-party assessment costs passed through or transparently included.
- **Enterprise/API:** custom integration, SSO/security and validation work separately scoped.

Existing Talent Bridge historical per-candidate assessment/interview pricing gives a real internal anchor, but recurring plan willingness-to-pay remains unproven.

## GTM

First route is existing recruiter/agency relationships, not paid advertising.

Sell:

> “For your next recurring professional role, we give every shortlisted candidate the same structured assessment and interview evidence, then return a client-ready comparison pack — without changing your ATS.”

Sequence:
1. identify 10–20 agencies with repeat professional vacancies;
2. disqualify agencies already satisfied with an enterprise assessment stack;
3. choose one recurring role family;
4. sell one paid candidate cohort;
5. measure recruiter/client usage and QA time;
6. require next-role or next-month commitment before building broader platform features.

Founder approval is required before external outreach.

## Delivery architecture

```text
agency ATS / candidate list
→ role-specific rubric
→ licensed assessments
→ structured AI interview
→ deterministic scoring/evidence extraction
→ AI synthesis
→ human QA / exceptions
→ branded comparative report
→ agency/client hiring decision
```

No sole automated final hiring decision. Candidate notice/consent, access controls, retention/deletion and audit trails are required.

## Governance / counter-evidence

- Article 18 of UAE Federal Decree-Law 45/2021 gives data subjects rights relating to automated processing decisions; DIFC Regulation 10 addresses personal-data processing through autonomous/semi-autonomous systems. Product controls should be conservative and legal review obtained before relying on automated hiring decisions.
- Use only instruments Talent Bridge is licensed/authorised to deploy.
- Do not infer protected characteristics or present AI output as diagnosis.
- Maintain human review and transparent job-relevant rubrics.
- Low-cost tools mean the moat must be decision intelligence and client-ready evidence, not test access.
