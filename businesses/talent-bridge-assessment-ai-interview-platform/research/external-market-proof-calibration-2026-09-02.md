# Talent Bridge Assessment & AI Interview Platform — External Market Proof Calibration

**Date:** 2 September 2026  
**Issue:** #142  
**Parent:** `talent-bridge-assessment-ai-interview-platform`

## Decision

**EMP4 Established and transferable · EMP Confidence 94%.**

Online assessments, structured/asynchronous interviews, AI-assisted screening and assessment-platform subscriptions are established categories globally and in the UAE/GCC. The current opportunity is not to prove that employers/recruiters pay for assessment technology. The remaining DRF-specific uncertainty is whether Talent Bridge can combine its existing recruitment judgement, role packs and licensed assessment capability into a recurring **human-reviewed evidence product** with bounded QA cost and strong candidate/employer experience.

## Successful materially comparable operators

### TestGorilla
Current public pricing shows:
- Free plan with limited tools;
- Core **US$142/month** on annual commitment;
- Plus starting at **US$400/month** annual, including customised AI interviews with AI scoring, job simulations, custom tests, ID verification, ATS/API and unbranded experience;
- assessments and AI interviews consume candidate credits;
- published candidate completion is 85–90%.

Source: https://www.testgorilla.com/pricing/

### Talogy
Talogy has a current UAE/Middle East assessment presence, 75+ years of assessment experience, 150+ psychologists/psychometricians and 10,000+ organisations globally. Its Middle East Saville Wave offering is available in 37 languages including Arabic, and TalogyTech integrates with ATSs and supports customised assessment processes.

Sources:
- https://talogy.com/en-ae/
- https://talogy.com/en-ae/talent-management-solutions/assessments/saville-wave/
- https://talogy.com/en-ae/talent-management-solutions

### Discover Assessments — UAE-based direct regional comparable
Discover Assessments is headquartered in Ajman and states it has operated since 2015, completed 2M+ assessments across 100+ enterprise clients and serves UAE/GCC employers with gamified psychometrics, cognitive, EI and 360 tools. Treat client/volume claims as vendor-reported evidence, but its regional longevity and product breadth strengthen transferability.

Source: https://www.discoverassessments.com/

### Qabil — GCC AI screening comparable
Qabil markets UAE-hosted bilingual screening for GCC hiring. It currently lists a free tier and **AED299/month for up to 100 CVs**, with higher volume custom pricing. Its product explicitly emphasises UAE hosting, audit-ready reasoning and local hiring signals.

Source: https://qabil.ai/

### TIP by Qaitas — regional talent-intelligence comparable
TIP markets a UAE-hosted, Arabic-first talent platform combining psychometrics, assessment centres, candidate management and analytics, reinforcing regional demand for an integrated assessment layer rather than isolated tests.

Source: https://qaitas.com/tip-platform

### e& UAE / Oceain — low-price regional benchmark
The e& UAE business marketplace currently lists Oceain assessment/video-screening packages from **AED56/month to AED554/month**, with candidate/employee volume limits, showing that local buyers can access very low-cost assessment software. This is important counter-evidence against competing on commodity test access alone.

Source: https://prodnew.etisalat.ae/en/smb/marketplace/human-resources/oceain.html

## Regulatory / governance evidence

UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on Personal Data Protection includes Article 18, giving data subjects a right to object to decisions resulting from automated processing, including profiling, particularly where decisions have legal impact or adversely affect them, subject to stated exceptions. DIFC Regulation 10 addresses processing personal data through autonomous and semi-autonomous systems. DIFC also consulted in 2026 on further amendments clarifying AI/data governance.

Sources:
- https://www.uaelegislation.gov.ae/en/legislations/1972/download
- https://www.difc.com/business/registrars-and-commissioners/commissioner-of-data-protection/regulation-10
- https://www.difc.com/whats-on/news/difc-consultation-amended-data-protection-regulations

This is **not legal advice**. Product design should default to job-relevant assessment, candidate notice/consent, data minimisation, auditability and human review rather than sole automated hiring decisions.

## Transferability to Talent Bridge

### Transfers well
- employers/agencies already pay subscription and usage fees;
- UAE/GCC-specific products exist, reducing geography uncertainty;
- Talent Bridge already owns employer relationships, recruitment process knowledge, reporting patterns and assessment operations;
- candidate-volume/credit pricing maps naturally to recurring/usage economics;
- role-specific evidence + human synthesis is more defensible than commodity test access.

### Does not transfer automatically
- scientific validity of third-party or custom assessments;
- automated scoring fairness;
- enterprise security/compliance credibility;
- candidate completion/acceptance rates;
- recurring buyer volume;
- low human-review minutes at scale.

## Counter-evidence / failure modes

1. **Commodity pricing pressure:** TestGorilla, Oceain and many libraries make generic tests cheap.
2. **Strong regional incumbents:** Talogy, Discover, Qaitas/Qabil and others already have localisation/compliance narratives.
3. **High-stakes governance:** an opaque automated rejection or personality inference can create legal, ethical and reputational risk.
4. **Human-review cost:** if every candidate requires extensive consultant interpretation, MRR becomes managed-service labour.
5. **Assessment licensing:** Talent Bridge can only deploy instruments it is authorised/licensed to use.
6. **ATS/platform bundling:** buyers may prefer assessment inside their existing ATS/recruitment stack.

## Commercial conclusion

The category earns **EMP4**, so DRF should not spend time proving that assessment platforms exist or that somebody pays. The next proof should target the transferable wedge:

> role-specific, UAE/GCC-aware, human-reviewed candidate evidence delivered with enough repeatability and low enough QA cost to support recurring agency/employer usage.
