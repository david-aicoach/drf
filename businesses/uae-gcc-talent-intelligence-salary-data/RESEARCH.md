## Current thesis

Talent Bridge has direct UAE/GCC recruitment exposure and potentially valuable role/candidate/market observations. A recurring benchmark/data product has strong automation economics, but data quality, privacy-safe aggregation and willingness to pay are not yet sufficiently proven.

## Supported evidence

- Talent Bridge possesses real recruitment-market operating context.
- Data products are naturally updateable and subscription-compatible.
- AI can automate aggregation, summarisation and report generation once lawful source data is structured.

## Key unknowns

- Sample depth and representativeness by role/sector.
- Privacy/consent and safe aggregation thresholds.
- Buyer willingness to pay versus public salary guides.

## Minimum commercial test

Create one narrow, privacy-safe role/sector benchmark with transparent sample rules and sell or pre-sell access before expanding coverage.
