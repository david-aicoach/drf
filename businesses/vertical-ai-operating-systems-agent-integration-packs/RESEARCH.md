## Current thesis

Businesses increasingly want AI layered onto systems they already use. DRF/iMPLEMENTAi has strong fit because reusable agents, workflows, MCP connections and business operating knowledge can be packaged by vertical. The principal risk is excessive customisation.

## Supported evidence

- Existing SaaS/CRM/accounting systems can remain systems of record while AI operates at the interface/integration layer.
- DRF already has reusable workflow and agent assets.
- Operational embedding supports recurring support value better than one-off training.

## Key unknowns

- Repeatable boundary between product and bespoke consultancy.
- Support burden and integration drift.
- CAC/LTV and willingness to pay by vertical.

## Minimum commercial test

Choose one narrow workflow already proven internally, define a fixed input/output/integration boundary, sell it once externally and track customisation/support hours.
