## 5. Backtest and economic sensitivity

### Existing leakage scenario from the niche dossier

Illustrative only:

```text
40 monthly quotes
× AED 1,500 expected gross profit per quote
× 25% historical close probability
= AED 15,000 expected gross profit represented by the quote book

If 20% receives no disciplined follow-up:
≈ AED 3,000 expected-gross-profit exposure
```

This does not claim AED3,000 is normally recoverable. It shows why a **AED750–1,500/month** managed fee can be commercially coherent if a live audit proves comparable leakage.

### Delivery-cost boundary

Current DRF cost research shows that fixed software allocation can be tens rather than thousands of dirhams per account each month. The dominant margin variables are therefore:

1. onboarding hours;
2. data cleanup;
3. workflow exceptions;
4. retraining/support;
5. custom integration/reporting.

**Backtest pass condition:** keep the product standard enough that steady-state direct delivery/support cost does not consume more than roughly 40% of the managed recurring fee. This is a DRF test hurdle, not achieved margin.

## 6. Forward-test card

### Hypothesis

A qualified Dubai HVAC/AC service contractor with visible enquiry/quote/AMC leakage will commit money to a productised revenue-control system when the offer is anchored to recovered gross profit rather than CRM features.

### Offer tested

- Foundation implementation: **minimum AED 2,500**.
- Managed revenue core: **minimum AED 750/month** plus usage where applicable.
- Pilot deposit: **AED 1,000**, credited to implementation.

Use one consistent offer during the test; do not discount individual prospects to manufacture conversion.

### Audience

Up to **60 qualified Dubai/UAE HVAC accounts** meeting the atomic ICP from the niche dossier.

### Pass threshold for P3

Within 30 days:

- at least **8 qualified discovery/audit conversations**;
- at least **2 paid AED1,000 pilot deposits** against the same minimum commercial offer;
- acquisition + test spend remains within **$3,000 maximum Tier 1 capital**;
- founder time remains **≤ 50 hours**;
- no critical Meta/CRM/system-of-record blocker makes standard delivery impossible.

### Recycle / fail threshold

Recycle if the problem is validated but price, workflow or ICP is consistently wrong. Kill this niche/offer version if a properly qualified sample repeatedly rejects the economic problem itself or standard delivery requires bespoke implementation that destroys margin.
