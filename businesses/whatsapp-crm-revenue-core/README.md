## Business definition

This is a first-class DRF commercial opportunity, not merely infrastructure hidden underneath other offers.

Client-facing category:

# WhatsApp + CRM Revenue Core

Alternative descriptive label:

**Managed CRM-as-a-Service for WhatsApp-first businesses**

The customer is not buying software configuration. The customer is buying a continuously operated customer/revenue system where:

- every legitimate enquiry enters one customer record;
- WhatsApp conversations are visible to the authorised team;
- every qualified opportunity has a next action;
- ownership and pipeline stage are visible;
- reminders, follow-up and booking workflows operate continuously;
- management can see response, pipeline and conversion state;
- later outcome modules can be added without rebuilding the foundation.

Core promise:

> One customer record, one conversation history, one pipeline and a reliable next action for every qualified opportunity.

## Vendor-neutral architecture

The product is fixed. The backend is interchangeable.

```text
Agent layer
Grok Bot | CRM-native AI | other approved agent
        ↓
CRM layer
HighLevel | Kommo | Zoho CRM | HubSpot | approved incumbent CRM
        ↓
WhatsApp layer
CRM-native WhatsApp | WATI | respond.io | SleekFlow | CEQUENS | Unifonic | approved BSP
        ↓
Meta / WhatsApp Business Platform
```

**Rule:** do not force a client to migrate systems when preserving an existing CRM produces lower resistance and equal or better commercial outcomes.

## Why this is separate from Revenue Recovery or Missed Lead Conversion

Revenue Recovery, Missed Lead Conversion, Instant Quote, Voice AI, Reputation and Support are **outcome modules**.

WhatsApp + CRM Revenue Core is the **foundation service** that makes those modules easier to deploy, measure and retain.

```text
WhatsApp + CRM Revenue Core
        ↓
customer record + conversations + pipeline + calendars + workflows + attribution
        ↓
Revenue Recovery | Missed Lead | Instant Quote | Voice | Support | Reputation
        ↓
optional Grok Bot / external agent for cross-system operating work
```

The same client may therefore score twice in the niche register: once for the foundation and once for the outcome module. This is intentional rather than double-counting; they are separately sellable recurring layers.

## Commercial model

### Setup

Fixed implementation fee for CRM, WhatsApp, pipeline, workflows, permissions and reporting, regardless of approved backend selected.

### Monthly

Recurring managed fee covering platform access where bundled, monitoring, optimisation and bounded support.

### Usage

Meta messaging/calling and AI/agent usage should either be transparently passed through or rebilled with an explicit margin policy.

### Expansion

Outcome modules are sold as upgrades when the foundation data shows a measurable opportunity.

```text
Foundation MRR
+ outcome-module MRR
+ usage/rebilling economics
+ optional agentic-operations retainer
```

## Stack-selection rule

1. Start with the client's existing CRM and WhatsApp state.
2. Use **HighLevel** by default when iMPLEMENTAi owns the full system and no strong incumbent exists.
3. Preserve **Kommo, Zoho or HubSpot** when migration resistance exceeds the benefit.
4. Add **WATI/respond.io/SleekFlow/CEQUENS/Unifonic** only when specialist WhatsApp/CPaaS capability justifies another system.
5. Use CRM-native AI first for CRM-native tasks.
6. Add **Grok Bot** only for persistent cross-system/browser/research/exception workflows.
7. Re-check current vendor and Meta pricing before every material quote.

## Scale rule

Do not market this as generic CRM implementation.

Verticalise the promise:

> We install and operate the WhatsApp revenue system for [specific niche], so every enquiry is captured, every qualified opportunity has a next action and management can see exactly what is happening.

Then attach the highest-value outcome module for that niche.
