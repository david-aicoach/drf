## Current thesis

For WhatsApp-first UAE service businesses, a CRM-centred revenue core can capture, qualify, route, follow up, book, recover and retain customers with strong recurring economics. HighLevel is the simplest native route when its lifecycle, AI and billing features are sufficient; Kapso remains a strong composable option where portability, direct agent access or customer-owned WhatsApp onboarding materially matters.

## Supported evidence

- WhatsApp is a first-class commercial channel for the target market.
- HighLevel provides CRM lifecycle automation plus predictable per-location native AI economics.
- Kapso provides a more composable WhatsApp/agent layer with customer-owned onboarding and managed Meta billing.

## Key unknowns

- Real onboarding/support minutes by architecture.
- Live messaging/vendor cost per qualified or recovered revenue event.
- Retention and gross margin after exceptions and human handoff.

## Minimum commercial test

Run one HVAC/AC design-partner account and compare the smallest viable HighLevel-native and composable stacks on onboarding time, reliability, support burden, total delivery cost and attributable revenue outcome.
