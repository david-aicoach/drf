# LAYER 1 — Opportunity Discovery & Structural Selection

## Core question

> **Do we want this kind of business?**

## 1. Business definition

**Service/product/outcome sold:** A managed WhatsApp-first CRM and revenue-control system.  
**Pain solved:** Legitimate enquiries, quotations, follow-ups and AMC renewals are lost because conversations and pipeline ownership are fragmented.  
**Buyer/payer:** Owner, managing director, general manager, sales manager or operations leader at an owner-led UAE HVAC/AC service contractor.  
**User:** Office administrators, sales staff, estimators, service coordinators and management.  
**Why now:** WhatsApp is a primary customer channel in the UAE; CRM/automation platforms can now provide a repeatable managed foundation at modest software cost; AI can assist without replacing deterministic workflow ownership.  
**Current alternative:** Personal/team WhatsApp, spreadsheets, inboxes, disconnected field-service/accounting tools, memory and ad hoc follow-up.  
**Fatal condition:** The target account has too little lead/quote/renewal volume or gross-profit exposure to justify the fee, or deployment requires bespoke integration/support that destroys contribution.

**Why it wins:** The niche combines urgent and planned service demand, visible quote/renewal leakage, recurring customer moments, identifiable companies and high economic value per recovered opportunity. It can support an outcome-led promise rather than generic CRM implementation.

## 6. Recommended offer

**Offer name:** HVAC Enquiry-to-Revenue Control System  
**Promise:** Every legitimate enquiry, qualified quotation and AMC-renewal opportunity is captured, assigned and followed through one visible pipeline.  
**Included:** Revenue-leak audit, CRM/pipeline setup, WhatsApp capture, ownership/next-action rules, quote ageing, AMC reminders, core workflows, permissions, dashboard, staff onboarding and bounded monthly monitoring.  
**Excluded:** Full field-service/ERP/accounting replacement, unlimited bespoke integrations, guaranteed recovered revenue, unlimited content/campaign work and unsupported data cleansing.  
**Setup:** AED2,500–6,000.  
**Managed monthly:** AED750–1,500 plus transparent usage or rebilling.  
**Pilot deposit:** AED1,000 credited to implementation.  
**Upsells:** Revenue Recovery, missed-lead conversion, AI Voice, Instant Quote, Reputation and advanced reporting.  
**Pricing rationale:** One recovered profitable job, quote or AMC renewal can cover a meaningful share of the monthly fee; external CRM/SaaS operators demonstrate recurring willingness to pay, while the exact UAE price remains a DRF test.

## 7. Go-to-market

### First 10 customers

1. Build a qualified list of 60 Dubai/UAE HVAC contractors with visible enquiry channels and appropriate size.
2. Offer a short evidence-led Revenue Leak Audit rather than “CRM setup”.
3. Use direct founder/owner outreach through phone, WhatsApp, email and LinkedIn where lawful and appropriate.
4. Diagnose response ownership, quote ageing, AMC renewal, missed-call and follow-up leakage.
5. Present one consistent pilot offer and price.
6. Target eight qualified conversations and two paid deposits in the first 30-day test.
7. Convert pilots into the standard implementation + monthly managed core.
8. Use measured before/after pipeline and response evidence to acquire customers 3–10.

### First 100 customers

**Pending after first-10 evidence.** Likely routes include a verticalised referral/partner channel, search/content around HVAC revenue leakage, trade relationships, a repeatable audit funnel, client proof and controlled paid acquisition. No first-100 channel should be scaled before real CAC, delivery capacity and retention are known.
