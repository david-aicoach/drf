# White-Label Hiring Portal for Employers/Agencies

**Stage:** Researching  
**Opportunity score:** 88/100

## Offer

A branded employer/agency hiring portal combining assessment, AI interview, workflow status and candidate reporting.

## Outcome sold

A cleaner, recurring client-facing hiring experience without requiring the buyer to build its own recruitment technology.

## Commercial model

Setup/branding fee plus recurring portal subscription and candidate usage.

## Next action

Pilot one branded portal for an existing employer or agency and measure onboarding, usage, support and willingness to renew.
