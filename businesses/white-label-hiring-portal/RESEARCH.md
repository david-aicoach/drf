# Research — White-Label Hiring Portal for Employers/Agencies

Updated: 2026-08-29  
Issue: #45  
Research status: **Comprehensive desk research complete; one branded employer/agency renewal test required**

## Executive conclusion

**Recommendation: retain at 88/100 and define the portal as a branded delivery layer for Talent Bridge assessment/evaluation workflows, not a new ATS or generic candidate portal.**

The core software category is commoditised. Workable, Manatal and Recruit CRM already provide candidate portals, ATS workflows, AI features, sourcing, automation and API capability at accessible SaaS prices. A white-labelled portal with forms and candidate status therefore has little independent moat.

The opportunity becomes stronger when the portal packages something Talent Bridge already knows how to deliver: structured role intake, candidate screening, assessments, AI-assisted structured interviews, evaluation reports, shortlist comparison and hiring-manager decision support. For an employer or agency, the value is a branded, repeatable hiring evidence experience without having to build assessment/evaluation operations internally.

## Buyer problem

Boutique agencies and smaller employers may have an ATS but lack:

- professional candidate-facing assessment experience;
- structured screening packs;
- standard AI interview flow;
- branded candidate evaluation reports;
- employer/client shortlist comparison;
- evidence completeness tracking;
- consistent communication templates;
- a single customer-facing place to view decision-ready candidates.

## Product definition

> **A branded employer/agency hiring portal that delivers Talent Bridge's structured assessment, interview and candidate-evaluation workflow on top of existing recruiting systems.**

It is not the system of record by default.

## Portal modules

### Employer/client side

- role brief/status;
- shortlisted candidates;
- assessment/evaluation reports;
- interview questions;
- decision/status capture;
- secure document access;
- optional analytics/benchmarking.

### Candidate side

- invitation/consent;
- screening questionnaire;
- assessment links;
- AI-assisted structured interview;
- document/profile completion;
- status/next-step communication where appropriate.

### Talent Bridge/agency side

- evidence completeness;
- QA/review queue;
- role templates;
- report generation;
- client/candidate permissions;
- integration/writeback to ATS/CRM.

## Competitive landscape

- Workable: complete ATS/recruiting suite with employer workflows and AI;
- Manatal: inexpensive AI-assisted ATS/CRM with candidate portal/API at higher tiers;
- Recruit CRM: agency ATS/CRM with AI and portal/website capabilities;
- TestGorilla/HireVue: assessment/interview candidate experiences;
- no-code client portals and white-label SaaS builders.

The lesson is clear: **portal technology itself should be bought/integrated where possible**. Talent Bridge should invest in the evaluation workflow, content and branded experience.

## Strongest initial buyer

1. an existing Talent Bridge client wanting a branded hiring process;
2. a boutique recruitment agency without its own assessment/evaluation capability;
3. a retained search firm that wants high-quality candidate dossiers under its own brand.

## Commercial model hypothesis

### Setup

AED 5,000–15,000 for branding, role templates, integrations and permissions.

### Monthly subscription

AED 1,500–5,000/month including platform access, a candidate allowance and standard support.

### Usage

Per candidate for licensed assessments, AI interview/media cost and professional evaluation above included volume.

### Enterprise/agency white label

Higher retainer for custom domain, templates, integrations, multi-client permissions and monthly volume.

## Delivery economics

The portal itself should be low-cost. Variable economics come from:

- assessment licence/candidate;
- AI/video processing;
- report generation;
- human QA;
- integration support;
- client/candidate support.

The recurring thesis fails if every customer needs custom workflows or constant manual intervention.

## GTM

Use a “done-with-you hiring evidence portal” offer rather than selling software seats.

Example:

> Your candidates complete screening, assessment and structured interview through your branded portal, and your hiring managers receive consistent decision-ready reports without changing your ATS.

Start with one existing employer where trust already exists.

## Defensibility

- Talent Bridge role/evaluation content;
- branded report quality;
- assessment/AI interview workflows;
- reusable role packs;
- GCC-specific recruitment insight;
- cross-client benchmark data where lawful;
- integration with managed assessment/recruitment services.

## High-stakes controls

- human review on material candidate conclusions;
- role-relevant assessment criteria;
- candidate privacy/consent;
- secure access and tenant separation;
- clear retention/deletion;
- auditability of scoring/report versions;
- assessment licensing;
- accessibility/accommodation path;
- no hidden automated final hiring decision.

## Risks

- buyer already has equivalent portal functionality;
- custom branding/integration scope creep;
- low candidate volume makes subscription unattractive;
- candidate-support burden;
- security/privacy failures;
- customers treat portal scores as final decisions;
- assessment licences restrict white-labelling/resale;
- existing ATS vendors improve assessment/evaluation features.

## Evidence discipline

### Verified

- Generic ATS/candidate-portal functionality is widely available.
- Employers/agencies already pay for assessment and interview software.
- Talent Bridge already has evaluation/reporting assets and client relationships.

### DRF judgement

- Portal should be a productised delivery experience, not a platform rebuild.
- Existing employer/agency relationships materially lower GTM risk.
- Recurring economics depend on candidate flow and bounded human QA.

### Unproven

- willingness to pay for branded layer;
- candidate volume per account;
- renewal after first role;
- support minutes/client;
- licensing compatibility for white-label assessment bundles.

## Validation experiment

Deploy a minimal branded portal for one live employer/agency using existing tools wherever possible.

Measure:

- setup hours;
- candidates invited/completed;
- candidate support requests;
- employer logins/usage;
- report viewing/decision activity;
- consultant QA minutes;
- variable cost/candidate;
- buyer willingness to use on the next role/month;
- required custom changes.

### Pass gate

Proceed only if the buyer commits to repeat usage or subscription, most of the workflow is reusable and human/support cost preserves healthy margin.

## Ranking implication

**Keep 88/100, but Stage should remain Researching until one renewal/repeat-role test.** The crowded ATS market strengthens the case for the Talent Bridge intelligence layer while weakening any generic portal-build thesis.
