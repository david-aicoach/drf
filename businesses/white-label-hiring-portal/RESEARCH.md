## Current thesis

Existing assessment, AI interview, recruitment workflow and reporting components can be packaged into a recurring branded portal. This is commercially cleaner than a two-sided job-board build because the buyer and use case are defined.

## Supported evidence

- Talent Bridge already operates the underlying hiring/evaluation workflows.
- White-labelling creates a clear B2B buyer and recurring account relationship.
- Candidate usage can support subscription-plus-usage economics.

## Key unknowns

- Actual recurring buyer usage.
- Integration/support cost across client environments.
- CAC, churn and required customisation.

## Minimum commercial test

Configure one branded portal for a known employer/agency, process real candidates and measure setup hours, monthly active use, support tickets and renewal intent.
