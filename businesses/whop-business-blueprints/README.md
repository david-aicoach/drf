# Whop Business Blueprints

**Stage:** Candidate  
**Opportunity score:** 82/100  
**Evidence confidence:** 90%  
**Research completeness:** 100%

## Offer

Package proven DRF business systems, playbooks and deployable operating blueprints for sale/distribution through Whop, with recurring downstream economics and higher-value implementation/managed-service layers where practical.

## Outcome sold

A faster path for buyers to deploy a proven business system rather than start from a blank page.

## Commercial model

```text
Whop Blueprint distribution
→ recurring downstream Whop economics where applicable
→ iMPLEMENTAi implementation revenue
→ managed-service / software MRR where ongoing value exists
```

Whop's official Partners programme confirms recurring business-referral economics of up to 30% of Whop gross profit, 1% of referred-business ad spend and second-tier commissions.

Whop's live Blueprint page now also visibly exposes a dedicated **Docs** route alongside **Create a business**, **Community blueprints** and **Trending** discovery. Current ecosystem reporting converges on a Blueprint-specific formula of **10% of Whop's profit on qualifying Blueprint-attributed sales**, not 10% of gross sales. The exact Docs body was not directly retrievable in the 31 August evidence pass, so DRF retains that formula as strongly corroborated rather than first-party captured.

## Current evidence signal

- An early ecosystem operator reports 580+ businesses referred through Blueprints and more than $400/day recurring economics. Treat this as self-reported evidence, not typical or guaranteed creator earnings.
- Whop's current public positioning is explicitly AI-first: create or run businesses with AI.
- Whop CLI documentation confirms Claude Code, Cursor and Codex can operate Whop businesses programmatically.
- No native Blueprint-specific WebMCP implementation is yet verified.

## Score decision

**Hold at 82/100.**

The 31 August documentation evidence improves programme clarity but does not yet justify further score inflation. The reported 10% formula is narrower than early shorthand implied, and median conversion, retention, payout distribution, CAC and DRF live economics remain unproven.

## Operating rule

**Prove the workflow in DRF first; package it as a Blueprint second.**

Do not build a generic Blueprint catalogue merely because the distribution primitive exists.

## Next action

Capture the exact official Blueprint Docs/creator UI terms, then launch one Blueprint from a DRF workflow with existing economic proof and measure deployment, downstream activation, actual payout attribution, 30/60/90-day retention, support burden and iMPLEMENTAi implementation conversion.
