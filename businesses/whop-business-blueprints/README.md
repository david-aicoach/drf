## Commercial summary — read this first

**Do not model this as “Whop takes 10% of Blueprint revenue and we keep 90%.” That is not the current evidence.**

A Whop Business Blueprint is primarily a **distribution/referral asset**. The current commercial thesis has **three separate income streams**:

| Income stream | What we earn | What it is based on | Evidence status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Blueprint creator economics** | Reported **10% of Whop's qualifying profit** from sales generated by businesses deployed through our Blueprint | Whop's profit on qualifying Blueprint-attributed downstream sales — **not 10% of the business's gross sales** | Strongly corroborated; exact Whop Docs/creator UI still needs direct capture |
| **2. Whop Partner/referral economics** | Up to **30% of Whop gross profit** from successfully referred businesses, recurring while they earn, plus **1% of referred-business Whop ad spend** and second-tier commissions | Whop Partner programme economics | First-party Whop verified; separate eligibility/attribution rules apply |
| **3. iMPLEMENTAi services** | Our own **implementation/setup fee** plus optional **managed-service/software MRR** | Fees we charge the buyer for deployment, integration, customisation, support or ongoing operation | Controlled by us; must be priced and validated per offer |

### The simple money flow

```text
A business deploys our Blueprint
→ that business makes sales
→ the business owner receives its sales less applicable Whop/payment fees
→ Whop earns its own qualifying economics
→ we may receive the Blueprint creator share from Whop
→ we may also earn separate Whop Partner/referral economics where eligible
→ we can separately charge iMPLEMENTAi implementation and/or monthly managed-service fees
```

### What the 10% does NOT mean

```text
Business makes $100
→ Whop takes $10
→ we keep $90
```

**That is wrong.**

The current reported Blueprint formula is closer to:

```text
Business makes sales
→ Whop earns its qualifying profit
→ Blueprint creator receives 10% of Whop's qualifying profit
```

Until the exact live Blueprint Docs/creator UI is captured, do not forecast revenue using a fixed percentage of gross business sales.

### Strategic conclusion

The opportunity is attractive because **one Blueprint can potentially create multiple recurring economics at once**:

```text
Blueprint creator share
+ Whop Partner/referral share where eligible
+ iMPLEMENTAi implementation revenue
+ iMPLEMENTAi managed-service/software MRR where justified
```

Do not assume all layers automatically stack. Verify live Whop attribution and eligibility before financial modelling.

## Offer

Package proven DRF business systems and deployable operating configurations as Whop Business Blueprints, then monetise the resulting distribution through downstream Whop economics and higher-value iMPLEMENTAi services.

## Outcome sold

A faster path for buyers to deploy a proven business system rather than start from a blank page.

## Current evidence signal

- Whop's live Blueprint page exposes a dedicated **Docs** route alongside **Create a business**, **Community blueprints** and **Trending** discovery.
- Current ecosystem reporting converges on a Blueprint-specific creator formula of **10% of Whop's qualifying profit on Blueprint-attributed sales**, not 10% of gross sales. The exact Docs body was not directly retrievable in the 31 August evidence pass, so DRF retains this as strongly corroborated rather than first-party captured.
- Whop's official Partners programme confirms recurring business-referral economics of up to **30% of Whop gross profit**, **1% of referred-business ad spend**, and second-tier commissions.
- An early ecosystem operator reports 580+ businesses referred through Blueprints and more than $400/day recurring economics. Treat this as self-reported evidence, not typical or guaranteed creator earnings.
- Whop's current public positioning is explicitly AI-first and its CLI documentation confirms Claude Code, Cursor and Codex can operate Whop businesses programmatically.
- No native Blueprint-specific WebMCP implementation is yet verified.

## Score decision

**Hold at 82/100.**

The opportunity is compelling because Blueprints can become a distribution multiplier for proven DRF systems, but median conversion, retention, payout distribution, CAC and actual DRF economics remain unproven.

## Operating rule

**Prove the workflow in DRF first; package it as a Blueprint second.**

Do not build a generic Blueprint catalogue merely because the distribution primitive exists.

## Next action

Capture the exact official Blueprint Docs/creator UI terms, then launch one Blueprint from a DRF workflow with existing economic proof and measure:

- Blueprint deployments;
- downstream activation and revenue;
- actual Blueprint creator payout attribution;
- whether Whop Partner economics stack with Blueprint economics;
- 30/60/90-day retention;
- support burden;
- iMPLEMENTAi implementation conversion;
- managed-service/software MRR conversion.
