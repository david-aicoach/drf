# Whop Business Blueprints

**Stage:** Candidate  
**Opportunity score:** 82/100  
**Evidence confidence:** 90%  
**Research completeness:** 100%

## Business summary — read this first

We create a **business blueprint once** and use Whop to distribute it.

The commercial model has **three core income streams**:

| Income stream | Plain-English meaning | Current status |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Blueprint sales** | Sell the packaged Blueprint/IP as a paid digital product for an upfront price. Whop supports paid digital products. | **Commercially available on Whop generally. Native Business Blueprint gallery pricing is not yet verified.** |
| **2. Blueprint business royalties** | When somebody deploys our Blueprint and runs a business on it, we earn a recurring share from Whop based on that business's activity. Current reporting says **10% of Whop's gross profit** from businesses onboarded through the Blueprint. | **Core Business Blueprint model; exact first-party terms still need direct capture.** |
| **3. Whop referral commissions** | If the business is also attributed to us through Whop Partners, we can earn recurring referral commission from Whop, subject to eligibility and attribution rules. | **Official Whop programme. Do not assume it automatically stacks with Blueprint royalties until verified.** |

### The founder view

```text
BUILD ONCE
Business Blueprint

SELL IT
→ upfront Blueprint/digital-product sales

BUSINESSES USE IT
→ recurring Blueprint business royalties

WE REFER THE BUSINESS TO WHOP
→ recurring Whop referral commissions where eligible
```

That is the core business.

### Important distinction

Do **not** confuse the Blueprint royalty with the sale price.

If we sell a paid Blueprint package for $299, that $299 is **sale revenue**.

If the buyer then deploys the Blueprint and builds a business on Whop, the recurring **Blueprint business royalty** is a separate income stream based on Whop's economics from that business.

Whop's public launch message is essentially: **build a Blueprint once and get paid when businesses use it.**

### What is not yet proven

Whop definitely supports selling digital products through its marketplace. What is **not yet first-party verified** is whether the new native **Business Blueprint gallery itself** lets a creator set an upfront purchase price on the Blueprint listing.

Therefore our practical commercial model is:

1. use the native Blueprint for distribution and recurring royalties;
2. where useful, sell the packaged Blueprint/IP as a paid Whop digital product;
3. use Whop Partner referrals as an additional recurring commission layer where eligible.

## Optional fourth income stream

**Implementation / consulting is an optional upsell, not part of the core Blueprint business model.**

iMPLEMENTAi can charge for setup, customisation or managed service only when it improves the economics. Do not count it as one of the three core Whop income streams.

## Operating rule

**Prove the business system first. Package it second. Sell/distribute it third. Earn royalties as businesses use it.**

## Current evidence signal

- Whop officially launched Business Blueprints with the message: select a Blueprint to start a business; build a Blueprint and get paid when other businesses use it.
- Current ecosystem reporting consistently describes the creator royalty as **10% of Whop's gross profit** from businesses onboarded through the Blueprint, recurring while they operate.
- Whop separately supports paid digital products and subscriptions.
- Whop Partners separately pays recurring referral commissions for referred businesses.
- Early operator reporting claims 580+ Blueprint-referred businesses and more than $400/day recurring income; treat this as self-reported, not typical earnings.

## Score decision

**Hold at 82/100.**

The commercial model is attractive because one reusable Blueprint can create upfront sales plus recurring royalty/referral income. The remaining gap is live DRF proof: actual sale conversion, royalty attribution, retention and whether the income streams stack as expected.

Launch one proven DRF business system as a Blueprint and measure:

- Blueprint deployments;
- downstream business revenue;
- Blueprint business royalties;
- Whop referral commissions;
- whether royalty + referral income stack;
