## Current thesis

Whop's platform scale and Business Blueprints timing make productised business systems worth testing. The strongest DRF version is not merely a one-off information product: each blueprint should, where natural, feed a recurring service/software/community or operating layer.

## Supported evidence

- Whop has meaningful digital-product distribution and an emerging Blueprint format.
- DRF continuously produces reusable business systems that can become packaged IP.
- Blueprint production and maintenance can be highly AI-assisted.

## Key unknowns

- Definitive first-party creator payout/economics for Blueprints.
- Conversion and refund/retention behaviour.
- Whether downstream recurring products outperform pure blueprint sales.

## Minimum commercial test

Publish one blueprint from a system DRF already uses, track discovery/conversion/support, and attach only one justified recurring upsell.
