# Assets

Canonical inventory of assets DRF already owns, controls or can use to create commercial value before buying, building or adding new acquisition channels.

## What belongs here

- paid AI/subscription capacity;
- hardware and local/runtime capability;
- business systems and commercial infrastructure;
- owned/warm distribution and relationship assets;
- existing lead, research, brand and commercial material;
- founder leverage that materially improves revenue outcomes.

## What does not belong here

- credentials, tokens, cookies, card details or customer secrets;
- per-surface integration testing or authenticated-account status;
- duplicated CRM, connector or runtime documentation.

Operational surface capability belongs in [`technical/CCO Integration Readiness Checklist.md`](../../technical/CCO%20Integration%20Readiness%20Checklist.md).

## Rule

Use existing assets first. Prove demand and exploit owned capability before adding cost or custom infrastructure.
