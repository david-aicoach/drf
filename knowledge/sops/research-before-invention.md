# SOP — Research Before Invention

## Purpose

Turn uncertainty into an evidence-backed commercial or implementation decision before DRF invents new infrastructure, offers or operating methods.

## Procedure

1. Read the governing Issue and relevant DRF context.
2. State the exact decision that must be made.
3. Check existing DRF assets and native/vendor capability first.
4. Research current solutions and market evidence where material.
5. Compare only viable options on fit, cost, speed, maintenance, reversibility and commercial impact.
6. Choose the smallest viable path: **SELL / USE / INTEGRATE / AUTOMATE / BUILD**.
7. Persist substantial reusable evidence under `research/<issue>-<topic>/research.md`; otherwise keep it in the Issue.
8. State the recommendation, evidence, risks and any founder decision still required.

## Pass condition

The next executor can act without needing the research chat history.

Do not repeat settled research unless material facts changed.
