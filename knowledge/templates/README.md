# Templates

Standard structures for recurring DRF outputs such as business opportunities, business experiments, research handoffs, campaign briefs and commercial reviews.

Templates standardise useful repeated work; they must not create mandatory paperwork without a practical purpose.

## Canonical business opportunity pack

Use these for **every new business opportunity** and for the systematic rebuild of existing opportunity dossiers.

1. [`business-opportunity-readme.md`](./business-opportunity-readme.md) — founder-facing `README.md` template. The money model must be understandable in under 20 seconds.
2. [`business-opportunity-research.md`](./business-opportunity-research.md) — canonical comprehensive `RESEARCH.md` template covering sales/revenue, pricing, unit economics, customer, market, competition, GTM, delivery, AI/automation, recurring revenue, risks, evidence, validation, scoring and the existing-portfolio rebuild checklist.
3. [`business-opportunity-worked-example-whop-blueprints.md`](./business-opportunity-worked-example-whop-blueprints.md) — worked founder-language example based on Whop Business Blueprints: Blueprint sales, Blueprint business royalties, Whop referral commissions and optional implementation/consulting upside.

### Required order

```text
MONEY MODEL FIRST
→ what we sell
→ who pays
→ price / calculation basis
→ upfront vs recurring
→ sale / subscription / royalty / commission / usage
→ core vs optional
→ costs and contribution

THEN RESEARCH
→ customer and outcome
→ market and competition
→ sales and distribution
→ delivery and automation
→ recurring revenue and moat
→ risk and evidence
→ validation
→ score and next action
```

For commercial/business opportunity research, these templates take precedence over the generic `research-output.md` structure.
