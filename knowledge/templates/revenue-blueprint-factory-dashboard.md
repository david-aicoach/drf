# 6. Kill, recycle and learning quality

| Opportunity | Stage stopped | Cash lost | Founder hours | Reason | Lesson captured | Reusable asset/evidence |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|---|---|
| | | | | | | |

A healthy factory kills weak businesses early. Track:

- percentage killed before paid build;
- average loss on killed ideas;
- repeated failure causes;
- false confidence caused by weak evidence;
- templates, audiences, tests or data reused elsewhere.

Do not treat a high kill rate as failure when losses are small and learning is reused.

# 8. Factory health decision

Choose one:

- **Increase scanning** — insufficient quality input.
- **Increase underwriting/backtest capacity** — good ideas waiting for evidence.
- **Increase forward tests** — too much research, too little market contact.
- **Increase sales** — P3 opportunities are not converting to payment.
- **Improve delivery** — payments exist but customer value/contribution fails.
- **Improve repeatability** — P4 businesses depend on founder heroics.
- **Scale winners** — P5 economics/capacity pass.
- **Increase Blueprint distribution** — P6 inventory exists but sales/royalties lag.
- **Reduce WIP/capital** — too many active bets or excessive downside.
