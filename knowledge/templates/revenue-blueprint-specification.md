# 12. Buyer/operator launch checklist

## Before spending

- [ ] Buyer, offer and revenue streams are clear.
- [ ] Current commercial/platform terms are verified.
- [ ] Downside/base/upside model is complete.
- [ ] Maximum loss and stop condition are accepted.
- [ ] Required licences/compliance are resolved.

## Before launch

- [ ] Product/offer is ready.
- [ ] Checkout/payment works.
- [ ] Acquisition channel and tracking work.
- [ ] Delivery and quality check work.
- [ ] Customer support/refund path exists.
- [ ] Weekly KPI sheet is ready.

## Before scaling

- [ ] P5 repeatability is evidenced.
- [ ] CAC/payback is measured.
- [ ] Contribution is positive.
- [ ] Delivery capacity is known.
- [ ] Founder dependency is bounded.
- [ ] Tier 3 use of funds is tied to the proven constraint.
