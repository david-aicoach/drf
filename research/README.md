# Research

Shared cross-portfolio research and intelligence controls only.

## Ownership rule

- **Parent-opportunity research belongs with the opportunity:** `businesses/<opportunity>/RESEARCH.md` and, where supporting studies are needed, `businesses/<opportunity>/research/`.
- **Niche research belongs under:** `research/niches/`.
- **Recurring cross-portfolio run history belongs under:** [`research/recurring-intelligence/`](./recurring-intelligence/).
- **Root `research/` is reserved for genuinely reusable cross-portfolio studies** such as portfolio scoring, execution economics, market maps and cross-business opportunity analysis.
- Do not keep a second flat copy of opportunity-owned or niche-owned research here. Move the file and update every reference instead.
- Supporting historical/deep studies may remain separate from the canonical `RESEARCH.md`, but must live inside the owning opportunity or niche folder.

## Recurring intelligence

`recurring-intelligence/` contains concise navigation/audit logs for:

- Golden Opportunity Discovery runs;
- rejected and held discovery candidates;
- existing-portfolio refresh runs.

These logs do not replace detailed source evidence, GitHub Issues, opportunity dossiers or `businesses/PORTFOLIO-V3.md`. A partial/failed run preserves the last successful conclusion.

This structure keeps one obvious source location for every research asset while allowing the Revenue Factory to retain cross-portfolio automation history.
