# Research

Shared cross-portfolio research only.

## Ownership rule

- **Parent-offer research belongs with the offer:** `businesses/<offer>/RESEARCH.md` and, where supporting studies are needed, `businesses/<offer>/research/`.
- **Niche research belongs under:** `research/niches/`.
- **Root `research/` is reserved for genuinely reusable cross-portfolio studies** such as portfolio scoring, execution economics, market maps and cross-business opportunity analysis.
- Do not keep a second flat copy of offer-owned or niche-owned research here. Move the file and update every reference instead.
- Supporting historical/deep studies may remain separate from the canonical `RESEARCH.md`, but must live inside the owning offer or niche folder.

This structure keeps one obvious source location for every research asset.
