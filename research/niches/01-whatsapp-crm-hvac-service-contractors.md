## Sniper definition

HVAC/AC maintenance and emergency-service contractors with frequent inbound enquiries, recurring maintenance/AMC customers and a meaningful quotation pipeline, where customer conversations are fragmented across WhatsApp, calls, email and staff phones.

Sell the outcome: **every legitimate enquiry becomes a visible customer/opportunity with an owner and next action; quotes and recurring maintenance follow-up stop depending on staff memory.**

## Market and buyer evidence

Grand View Research currently estimates the UAE HVAC maintenance services market at **$1.465B in 2026**, rising to **$2.632B by 2033** at **8.7% CAGR**. UAE FM research also shows hard FM/MEP services remain a major share of a very large outsourced facilities market.

Public Dubai AC AMC pricing confirms a recurring commercial pattern even at residential/service-business level: contracts range from hundreds to several thousand dirhams annually depending on equipment and coverage. Commercial HVAC/MEP accounts can be materially larger, but DRF should obtain real contractor quotation/AMC samples before using an average value in sales ROI calculators.

## Reachability

Prospecting can use:

- Google Maps and local service directories;
- Dubai/UAE contractor and FM directories;
- DEWA/eSupply and government procurement supplier ecosystems;
- HVAC equipment distributors and maintenance-partner networks;
- LinkedIn titles: owner, GM, operations manager, service manager, sales manager.

The ICP should exclude one-man technicians with no meaningful recurring database or quote volume.

## Delivery fit

Preferred architectures:

1. **HighLevel native WhatsApp + CRM** for the lowest-complexity all-in-one deployment.
2. **Kapso + HighLevel CRM** when WhatsApp-native MCP/API access, portability or embedded onboarding matters.
3. Add Grok Bot only for irregular cross-system work; routine lead capture, pipeline state, reminders and follow-up should stay deterministic/native.

## What is still missing

- exact qualified contractor count in Dubai/UAE;
- median monthly inbound enquiry volume;
- median quote value and AMC value for the target ICP;
- CRM adoption and current WhatsApp workflow;
- staff adoption/support minutes after implementation.

## Live-validation gate

Interview 10–20 contractors and obtain at least three anonymised workflow samples. Pilot one account and measure: enquiry capture %, first-response time, opportunities without next action, quote follow-up completion, AMC renewal follow-up, staff usage and monthly support burden.
