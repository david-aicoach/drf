# WhatsApp + CRM × Specialist MEP Contractors

**Date:** 29 August 2026  
**Issue:** #41  
**Decision:** **Sniper**  
**Niche Score:** **91/100**  
**Evidence Confidence:** **84%** (revised from 80%)

## Atomic ICP

Specialist UAE MEP contractors handling repeated project quotations plus maintenance/service enquiries, where WhatsApp, email and individual staff own fragmented customer and opportunity history.

**Outcome:** one visible enquiry/quote/customer pipeline with ownership and next action, before adding Revenue Recovery, AR or Tender/RFQ modules.

## Evidence

Mordor estimates the UAE facility-management market at **$23.59B in 2026**, with hard services including MEP/fire/asset work accounting for **60.92%** of the prior-year mix and outsourced FM **64.88%**. Ken Research separately estimates a large and growing UAE FM market. The fit-out sector is also estimated at **$3.02B in 2025**, supporting a large contractor ecosystem around MEP integration and interior delivery.

Dubai eSupply serves **40+ government entities**, while DEWA and other authorities operate procurement portals. These surfaces demonstrate a dense, identifiable contractor/procurement ecosystem and repeated quote/tender activity.

## Score evidence

Pain 9; Pay 9; Reach 8; Growth 9; Volume 9; Underserved 8; ROI 10; Product Fit 10; Recurring 10; Simplicity 8. **91/100 unchanged.**

Confidence rises because market size, hard-services share, outsourcing and actual procurement channels are now supported. Exact SME specialist-contractor count, CRM penetration and quote workflow remain Missing.

## Delivery fit

Use HighLevel/Kapso/approved CRM stack for:

- enquiry capture;
- account/contact history;
- quotation/opportunity stages;
- owner and next action;
- WhatsApp/email follow-up;
- maintenance renewal reminders;
- management pipeline dashboard.

Do not attempt to replace estimating/ERP/accounting systems. CRM should own commercial state; specialist systems keep technical/financial truth.

## Live gate

Interview 10 specialist contractors across HVAC, electrical, plumbing/fire and fit-out MEP. Map current quote volume, channels, CRM/ERP, missed handoffs and desired management reporting. Pilot only where at least one measurable commercial leak exists.
