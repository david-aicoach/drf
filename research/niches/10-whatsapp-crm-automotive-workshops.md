# WhatsApp + CRM × Automotive Workshops

**Date:** 29 August 2026  
**Issue:** #41  
**Decision:** **Sniper**  
**Niche Score:** **89/100**  
**Evidence Confidence:** **84%** (revised from 81%)

## Atomic ICP

Organised independent/multi-brand UAE workshops with significant service volume where booking calls, estimate approvals, customer updates and service reminders are split between DMS, front desk, WhatsApp and staff memory.

**Outcome:** a communications/revenue layer that gives one customer history and visible commercial next actions without replacing the workshop DMS.

## Evidence

Ken Research estimates **$1.29B** UAE aftermarket service revenue in 2025, roughly **2,675 players** and 3.75M registered vehicles. Large multi-brand workshops are a growth segment; digital booking and standardised customer experience are cited as competitive advantages.

The strongest product fit comes from recurring service and approval conversations. The main architecture risk is overlap with DMS/workshop-management software.

## Factor read

Pain 9; Pay 8; Reach 9; Growth 8; Volume 9; Underserved 8; ROI 9; Product Fit 10; Recurring 10; Simplicity 9. **89/100 unchanged.**

## System boundary

**DMS owns:** vehicle/service history, job card, parts, technician/workshop state, invoice.

**CRM/WhatsApp owns:** lead/customer communication, booking intent, estimate follow-up, next action, campaign eligibility, relationship history and management visibility.

Integrate rather than duplicate.

## Live gate

Map 3–5 DMS products/workflows used by target workshops. Measure monthly inbound contacts, bookings, unapproved estimates, reminder volume and staff time spent switching systems. Pilot only with an explicit system-of-record map.
