## Atomic ICP

UAE specialist MEP/HVAC subcontractors repeatedly bidding for projects and maintenance contracts across inboxes, customer portals and government procurement systems.

**Outcome:** find/triage authorised RFQs, extract requirements/deadlines, produce bid/no-bid brief and submission checklist, and prepare evidence for human pricing/submission.

## Evidence

Dubai eSupply is the official procurement portal for **40+ government entities** and supports the complete online tender/RFX process. UAE government guidance points suppliers to eSupply plus entity-specific systems such as DEWA. A March 2026 DEWA HVAC AMC RFQ provides a concrete example of recurring, document-heavy MEP procurement.

The broader addressable ecosystem is large: UAE FM is estimated at $23.59B in 2026, with hard services/MEP a major component.

## Factor read

Pain 9; Pay 9; Reach 8; Growth 9; Volume 8; Underserved 9; ROI 9; Product Fit 9; Recurring 10; Simplicity 6. **87/100 unchanged.**

## Why Grok Bot fits

This work is intermittent, high-value and cross-system: read portals/email/docs, compare qualification criteria, prepare structured packs. That is stronger economics for a general computer-use agent than high-frequency transactional clicking.

Routine portal downloads, CRM updates and document transforms should be deterministic/API-driven where practical. Human approval remains mandatory for pricing, legal commitments and final submission.

## Live gate

Benchmark one real tender process end-to-end: human minutes, Bot quota consumed, auth failures, successful job completion, missed requirements, recovery time and value of analyst time saved. Require at least 10 completed tender-prep jobs before treating the 87 score as operating evidence.
