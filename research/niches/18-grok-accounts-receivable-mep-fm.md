## Atomic ICP

UAE SME MEP/FM/B2B contractors with recurring invoices, statements, promises-to-pay and disputes where admin/finance staff repeatedly reconcile aging and chase status across email, WhatsApp, CRM and accounting systems.

**Outcome:** better collection follow-up continuity and fewer invoices disappearing from the next-action queue; not autonomous financial decision-making.

## Evidence

Atradius' July 2026 UAE B2B payment report says **47% of B2B sales are made on credit**, nearly all surveyed businesses report delayed payments and around **two in five invoices are settled late**. Construction/industrial sectors are among the most affected, with longer payment terms prominent in construction. Bad debt is just over 2%, while DSO is lengthening for more firms.

This is unusually direct evidence for a recurring measurable pain. The value is cash acceleration, reduced collection admin and preserved follow-up history.

## Factor read

Pain 10; Pay 8; Reach 8; Growth 8; Volume 9; Underserved 8; ROI 10; Product Fit 8; Recurring 10; Simplicity 5. **87/100 unchanged.**

## Agent boundary

Grok Bot can:
- prepare aging/priority list from authorised sources;
- gather invoice/context evidence;
- draft reminders;
- summarise disputes/promises-to-pay;
- prepare escalation queues.

Accounting/ERP/CRM APIs should make deterministic state updates. Humans approve settlement, credit notes, legal threats, payment-plan exceptions and any change to invoice truth.

## Live gate

Use one controlled contractor dataset. Track DSO baseline, overdue balance, admin minutes, agent quota/cost, follow-up completion, promise-to-pay adherence and cash collected. Require explicit role/access controls.
