## 1. Executive conclusion

The mover market has excellent quotation volume and structured price drivers, but specialist mover software already contains estimating, video surveys and quoting. Ken Research estimates **US$163M**, **300+ players**, and **270k paid moves** in 2025. Public Dubai pricing also shows repeatable property-size bands.

The opportunity is not a generic quote calculator. It is:

> **give standard local-move prospects an immediate credible range, collect the exact survey inputs and route complex jobs to video/site survey—then push the qualified opportunity into the mover's existing system.**

Score reduces to 82 because incumbent mover CRMs already offer quoting/AI survey features.

## 2. Atomic ICP

Residential/SME-office movers with high local enquiry volume, repeatable price rules and enough historical quotes to train deterministic bands. Exclude international relocation, piano/art/special handling, major commercial moves and operators whose existing system already delivers instant/video quotes effectively.

## 3. Market / reach

Ken Research: US$163M 2025, 300+ players, 270k paid moves, ~US$604 blended revenue/move, 10.6% modeled CAGR. Dubai tenancy churn creates recurring demand.

## 4. Timing

Customers comparison-shop rapidly and expect price guidance. However, moving-tech products are improving, so value is implementation/localisation and conversion rather than novel quoting software.

## 5. Buyer economics

One booked local move commonly generates hundreds to thousands AED revenue. Quote-speed ROI must be measured against conversion and gross margin, not just quote volume.

## 6. Workflow

`enquiry → structured property/access/inventory inputs → instant range OR survey trigger → final quote → follow-up → booking`.

## 7. Pain model

Track time-to-first-price, % requiring survey, predicted range vs final invoice, quote abandonment and booking conversion by quote speed.

## 8. Competition

Movepro, QuoteIQ and DriveSales already provide mover estimating/CRM; DriveSales markets AI video surveys. Local mover sites also publish calculators/forms. This cuts Competition Gap to **5/10**.

## 9. Wedge

UAE-specific rule engine and conversion layer that works with incumbent mover software and avoids false precision.

## 10. Offer

Instant **range**, move-date availability capture, survey scheduling and automated quote follow-up. Human/site survey for abnormal access, large/bespoke inventory or uncertain scope.

## 11. Architecture

Website/WhatsApp form → deterministic quote rules → range/survey decision → mover CRM/dispatch system → booking.

## 12. Onboarding

Need 100–300 historical quotes/invoices, price drivers, exclusions, capacity/service areas and margin floors.

## 13. Unit economics

Very low marginal compute cost; main cost is initial pricing-rule construction and maintenance. Protect margin with hard exception thresholds.

## 14. Pricing hypothesis

AED3k–8k setup; AED1k–3k/month for maintained quoting/conversion module, usually bundled with mover Revenue Core.

## 15. Acquisition

Buyer: owner/GM/sales manager. Audit hook: **“How long does a standard apartment-move enquiry wait before receiving a useful price range?”**

## 18. Risks

Under-scoping, margin loss, access/fire/building restrictions, special items, misleading “fixed” price claims and stale price tables.

## 19. Retention / expansion

Quote rules/prices require maintenance; bundle with CRM/follow-up/reputation. Standalone retention is weaker than the CRM layer.
