## Atomic ICP

UAE, priority Dubai, drywall/gypsum partition and false-ceiling installers receiving frequent standardised fit-out enquiries where estimators repeatedly calculate similar scopes but site conditions can materially change final price.

**Outcome:** fast indicative estimate/range and structured survey/quotation intake, not an unconditional final construction price.

## Evidence

Ken Research estimates the UAE interior fit-out market at **$3.02B in 2025**, forecast **$4.59B by 2031** at **7.23% CAGR**, with around **420 players**. Its scope explicitly includes partitioning and ceilings, directly supporting demand for the target trade.

Dubai is the dominant fit-out hub, supported by high property transaction/completion, hospitality and office activity. What remains weak is trade-specific firm count and historical quote-driver data.

## Quote model hypothesis

Potential structured inputs:
- area/linear metres;
- partition type/layers;
- board specification and moisture/fire rating;
- ceiling type/height;
- insulation;
- doors/openings/access;
- finish/paint level;
- location and mobilisation;
- programme/working hours.

A site survey must be triggered for hidden MEP conflicts, abnormal heights, fire-rating complexity, difficult access, bespoke details or uncertain quantities.

## Why confidence remains below 80%

Market demand is now well evidenced, but the sniper thesis depends on **how often a useful estimate can be generated without a site visit**. That is still Missing and cannot be solved by macro research.

## Live gate

Collect 100 historical quotations from 3–5 specialist contractors. Identify the minimum input set, frequency of post-survey price changes and acceptable estimate error band. If fewer than ~60–70% of enquiries can receive a commercially useful range before survey, downgrade Product Fit.
