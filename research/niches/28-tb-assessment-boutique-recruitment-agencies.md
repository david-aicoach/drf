## Atomic niche

**Outcome/product:** role-specific assessment + structured AI-assisted interview + human-reviewed candidate evidence pack  
**Vertical:** recruitment / staffing  
**Sub-niche:** boutique recruitment agencies and RPO providers with roughly 5–50 recruiters and recurring professional vacancies  
**Geography:** UAE first, then GCC  
**Payer:** agency founder, managing director, practice lead or delivery director  
**Trigger:** agency needs faster evidence-backed screening, client differentiation and consistent candidate reports without buying/building enterprise assessment infrastructure.

## Why this niche is stronger than a generic employer launch

Recruitment agencies experience the screening problem every month across multiple clients, so recurring candidate volume is structurally higher than an occasional direct employer. Talent Bridge also understands agency economics and can package the product as a **white-label evidence layer** rather than asking an agency to replace its ATS.

This does not turn the product into a generic ATS. The wedge is assessment/interview evidence and client-ready reporting.

## Comparable market evidence

- TestGorilla sells assessment + AI interview capability through recurring plans and candidate credits.
- Talogy provides assessment technology, regional Middle East delivery and consulting/assessment-centre services.
- UAE/GCC products including Qabil, Qaitas TIP and Discover Assessments validate regional localisation and demand.
- Low-cost local products such as Oceain demonstrate strong pricing pressure on undifferentiated assessment access.

## Commercial wedge

> **White-label Candidate Evidence Pack for recruiters:** configure one recurring role family, invite candidates, combine licensed assessment evidence + structured AI interview + explicit scoring rubric, apply bounded human QA, and return a recruiter/client-ready comparative report.

The agency keeps its ATS and client relationship. Talent Bridge supplies the evidence engine.

## Pricing hypothesis

Start with usage plus minimum recurring commitment:

- **Agency Starter:** AED1,500–2,500/month minimum including a defined candidate allowance and standard evidence packs.
- **Professional / white-label:** AED3,000–5,000/month including more volume, role-pack customisation and branded reports.
- licensed assessment/third-party costs: pass through or transparently include in candidate credits;
- custom enterprise/API work: separate scope.

These are test hypotheses, not approved public prices.

## Acquisition

First 10:
1. use existing UAE recruiter/agency network and founder relationships;
2. target agencies handling repeat finance, HR, sales, operations or technical vacancies;
3. lead with a live-role assessment/report demo using synthetic or permissioned data;
4. sell one role-family pilot rather than platform access;
5. require recurring intent for the next vacancy/role pack.

Founder approval is required before outreach.

## Delivery

```text
agency ATS / candidate list
→ role pack / explicit rubric
→ licensed assessments where applicable
→ structured AI-assisted interview
→ evidence synthesis
→ human QA
→ comparative candidate report
→ agency/client decision
```

## Risks / counter-evidence

- agency may already use TestGorilla/Talogy/other tool;
- client may not value added evidence enough to pay;
- consultant QA may become expensive;
- assessment licensing and candidate consent must be controlled;
- agency/client may over-interpret scores;
- platform may be perceived as a competitor if Talent Bridge does not clearly separate white-label technology from recruitment delivery.

## Live proof

One unrelated agency, one recurring professional role family, **10–20 candidates** if volume allows.

Pass when:
- agency pays or signs a recurring minimum;
- candidate completion is acceptable;
- human QA remains bounded;
- report is used in real shortlist/client decision;
- agency commits to next role/month or a second role pack;
- no material privacy/validity/compliance issue emerges.
