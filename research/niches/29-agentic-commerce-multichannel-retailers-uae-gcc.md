# Agentic Commerce Visibility & Conversion × UAE/GCC Multi-Channel Retailers

**Research version:** 1.0  
**Date:** 2 September 2026  
**Issue:** #142  
**Commercial layer:** Commerce growth / AI discovery  
**Decision:** **Strong/Testable — instrument before scale**  
**Niche Score:** **83/100**  
**Evidence Confidence:** **85%**

## Atomic niche

**Outcome/product:** AI-commerce readiness, cross-platform catalogue truth, visibility monitoring and referral/conversion attribution  
**Vertical:** ecommerce / retail  
**Sub-niche:** mid-market multi-channel retailers and DTC brands with roughly 500–50,000 SKUs, meaningful existing SEO/paid acquisition spend, and catalogue presence beyond a simple single-store Shopify setup  
**Geography:** UAE first, then GCC  
**Payer:** ecommerce director, head of growth, digital commerce lead or founder  
**Trigger:** AI-search/shopping traffic is appearing but the business cannot see whether product data is accurate across AI surfaces or whether AI discovery produces attributable revenue.

## Why not ordinary Shopify merchants

OpenAI's current Shopping documentation states Shopify product data is already integrated through Shopify Catalog and **individual Shopify merchants do not need additional work for that integration**. Therefore “submit/fix your Shopify feed for ChatGPT” is not a defensible product.

The stronger niche has catalogue complexity across multiple systems/channels where data consistency, visibility measurement and cross-platform optimisation remain non-trivial.

## Current proof

- Adobe Brand Visibility became generally available in August 2026; Adobe reports AI traffic to U.S. retail sites up 1,324% from Oct 2024 to May 2026.
- Semrush AI Visibility is US$99/month/domain.
- AthenaHQ Starter is US$295/month.
- Scrunch Core is US$250/month.
- Managed GEO providers charge recurring retainers from low thousands of dollars per month, while Dubai-local offers exist from hundreds to several thousand AED/month.

This proves a market for visibility/monitoring. It does not prove UAE merchant conversion lift.

## Offer

### AI Commerce Readiness & Revenue Attribution

- catalogue/source-of-truth audit;
- feed/schema/product-detail consistency across channels;
- 30–100 representative high-intent buyer-query baseline;
- AI mention/product/merchant appearance monitoring;
- referral-source instrumentation where technically observable;
- conversion/assisted-revenue measurement;
- fix stale price/availability, identifiers, trust/policy and product-detail gaps;
- recurring monthly issue resolution and reporting.

## Pricing hypothesis

- audit/setup: **AED5,000–12,000**;
- managed monitoring/optimisation: **AED3,000–8,000/month**;
- larger catalogue/custom connector work: separately scoped;
- avoid performance-only pricing until attribution is reliable.

## Acquisition

Target merchants already spending on SEO/PPC and operating meaningful ecommerce revenue. Lead with a measurable audit:

> “Across the buyer questions that matter to your category, which AI systems currently show your products, what product data do they see, and can you connect any resulting referral to revenue?”

First proof should use 3–5 merchants, not broad paid acquisition.

## Delivery

```text
merchant catalogue / PIM / commerce platform
→ cross-channel product truth QA
→ AI-surface query monitoring
→ deterministic issue detection
→ bounded optimisation actions
→ analytics / referral attribution
→ monthly commercial report
```

## Risks / counter-evidence

- Shopify Catalog already handles ChatGPT product data for ordinary Shopify merchants;
- Adobe/Semrush/Athena/Scrunch can absorb monitoring;
- AI rankings are opaque and personalised;
- regional traffic may be too small to support ROI;
- attribution/correlation can be mistaken for causation;
- platform/feed rules can change quickly.

## Live proof

3–5 UAE/GCC merchants with meaningful existing traffic, preferably including at least two multi-channel/non-simple-Shopify catalogues.

Pass when:
- at least one merchant shows a measurable improvement in qualified AI appearance/referral/conversion signals after justified fixes;
- at least two merchants agree to pay for recurring monitoring/optimisation;
- the monthly delivery workflow remains repeatable with bounded integration/support effort.
