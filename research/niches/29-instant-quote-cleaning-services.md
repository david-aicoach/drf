## Atomic ICP

UAE, priority Dubai, move-in/out and deep-cleaning operators selling standardised apartment/villa packages where prospects comparison-shop and staff repeatedly price by property size, furnishing, condition and add-ons.

**Outcome:** instant transparent estimate/price and available slot for standard jobs, with photo/video/site review for abnormal scope.

## Evidence

IMARC estimates UAE cleaning services at **$2.2B in 2026**, forecast **$3.2B by 2034** at 4.9% CAGR; residential represented 32% of the 2025 market.

Current Dubai provider pricing is highly structured. Public 2026 guides commonly price by studio/bedroom/villa size, with move-in/out/deep-clean ranges from a few hundred dirhams to several thousand. Some operators already publish fixed price by property type and accept WhatsApp/photo inputs. This is strong evidence that the quote logic is parameterisable.

## Quote model

Core fields:
- property type/bedrooms/bathrooms;
- furnished/unfurnished;
- move-in/out/deep/post-renovation;
- approximate area;
- condition/photos;
- windows/balcony/appliances;
- sofa/carpet/add-ons;
- location/access;
- date/urgency.

Use fixed prices for well-defined packages and ranges/inspection for abnormal condition.

## Live gate

Collect 100 completed quotes from 3 operators. Measure quoted vs final invoice, gross margin, cancellation/no-show rate and booking conversion. The offer only works if faster quoting does not destroy margin through under-scoping.
