## Purpose

This folder is the evidence layer beneath the DRF niche register. Each current niche/product combination has one canonical research dossier so a high Niche Attractiveness Score cannot hide weak sub-niche evidence.

Atomic research target:

`market -> vertical -> sub-niche -> geography -> firmographic ICP -> pain/trigger -> product -> delivery rail -> measurable outcome`

Each dossier distinguishes:

- **Verified** — current first-party, government, regulatory, industry or credible market evidence;
- **Estimated** — directional evidence or a reasonable proxy, but not directly measured for the exact ICP;
- **Missing** — must be obtained from prospect interviews, client systems or live operating tests.

Desk research can strengthen Evidence Confidence, but it cannot prove product-market fit. Live conversion, onboarding, CAC, support, retention and gross-margin evidence remain separate gates.

## Comprehensive v3 requirements

Every dossier that claims Comprehensive v3 status must support an investment/market-entry decision and include:

- atomic ICP, exclusions and buying triggers;
- market size/density and reachable prospect universe;
- buyer economics and willingness-to-pay proxies;
- workflow anatomy and quantified pain;
- direct competitors, incumbent vertical software, agencies/services and manual substitutes;
- underserved gap and defensible outcome wedge;
- offer, delivery architecture and system-of-record boundaries;
- onboarding, unit economics, pricing, acquisition, risks and retention;
- **SEO:** transactional/problem/local keyword themes, current SERP competition, content gaps, money page and supporting cluster;
- **AI discovery / GEO:** normal crawl/index access, entity clarity, sourceable facts, original data/case studies, reviews/directories/third-party authority, comparison/use-case content and fixed-prompt citation monitoring;
- full 10-factor Niche Attractiveness scoring rationale;
- Verified / Estimated / Missing evidence;
- source ledger;
- live-validation plan with pass/fail/stop conditions.

## Research rules

1. Prefer UAE/Dubai government, regulators and first-party sources, then current high-quality industry research and credible market/operator evidence.
2. Do not invent keyword volumes, business counts or workflow statistics.
3. A large market does not prove the exact workflow pain.
4. A deeper pass may **lower the score while increasing Evidence Confidence** when strong incumbent software or delivery friction is discovered.
5. Use native/API automation for deterministic high-volume work and general agents only where cross-system context, research or exceptions justify the variable cost.
6. No dossier assumes a secret AI-ranking mechanism. AI discoverability follows normal indexability, authority, clarity and source-quality principles.
7. The next material confidence increase should come from interviews, workflow samples and live operating data.
