## Purpose

This folder is the evidence layer beneath the DRF niche register. Each current niche/product combination has its own research file so a high Niche Attractiveness Score cannot hide weak sub-niche evidence.

Atomic research target:

`market -> vertical -> sub-niche -> geography -> firmographic ICP -> pain/trigger -> product -> delivery rail -> measurable outcome`

Each file distinguishes:

- **Verified** — supported by current first-party, government, regulatory, industry or credible market evidence;
- **Estimated** — directional evidence or a reasonable proxy, but not directly measured for this exact ICP;
- **Missing** — must be obtained from prospect interviews, client systems or live operating tests.

Desk research can strengthen Evidence Confidence, but it cannot prove product-market fit. Live conversion, onboarding, support, CAC, retention and gross-margin evidence remain separate gates.

## Comprehensive v3 additions

In addition to market, ICP, economics, workflow, competitors, offer, architecture, onboarding, pricing, risks, score and live validation, every dossier must now include:

1. **SEO opportunity and competition** — transaction/problem/local keyword themes, current SERP surface and named competitors, content gaps, money page and supporting cluster. No invented volume numbers.
2. **AI discovery / GEO** — crawl/index access, entity clarity, structured sourceable facts, first-party data/case studies, reviews/directories/authority citations, comparison/use-case pages and fixed-prompt monitoring across major answer engines.
3. **More explicit competitive segmentation** — direct outcome providers, incumbent vertical software, agencies/services and manual/substitute methods.

## Research priority rule

1. Rebuild 11–31 in vertical research packs so shared market evidence is reused but exact workflow conclusions remain niche-specific.
2. Add v3 SEO/GEO analysis to 01–10 and refresh any material competitive evidence.
3. Evidence Confidence below 80% receives extra primary-evidence emphasis.
4. A strong incumbent can lower the niche score even when Evidence Confidence rises.
5. The next confidence increase above the desk-research ceiling should come from interviews, workflow samples or live client data.
