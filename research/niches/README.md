## Purpose

This folder is the evidence layer beneath the DRF niche register. Each current niche/product combination has its own research file so a high Niche Attractiveness Score cannot hide weak sub-niche evidence.

The research standard is:

`market -> vertical -> sub-niche -> geography -> firmographic ICP -> pain/trigger -> product -> delivery rail -> measurable outcome`

Each file distinguishes:

- **Verified** — supported by current first-party, government, regulatory, industry or credible market evidence;
- **Estimated** — directional evidence or a reasonable proxy, but not directly measured for this exact ICP;
- **Missing** — must be obtained from prospect interviews, client systems or live operating tests.

Desk research can strengthen Evidence Confidence, but it cannot prove product-market fit. Live conversion, onboarding, support, CAC, retention and gross-margin evidence remain separate gates.

## Comprehensive v2 requirements

A v2 niche dossier must support an investment/market-entry decision and include exact ICP/exclusions, market/density, buyer economics, workflow anatomy, quantified pain, competitor/current-tool landscape, underserved gap, offer/delivery architecture, onboarding, unit economics, pricing, acquisition, objections/risks, retention, full scoring rationale, Verified/Estimated/Missing evidence and a pass/fail live-validation plan.

## Research priority rule

1. Upgrade remaining v1 files in score-ranked batches.
2. Evidence Confidence below 80% gets extra attention during its v2 pass.
3. A strong incumbent can lower the niche score even when Evidence Confidence rises.
4. The next confidence increase above the desk-research ceiling should come from interviews, workflow samples or live client data.
