# DRF Niche Research Standard v2

**Status:** Governing standard for comprehensive niche dossiers  
**Date:** 29 August 2026

A niche dossier is not a source list or summary note. It must support an investment/market-entry decision.

Each dossier must include:

1. Executive conclusion and current decision.
2. Atomic ICP, exclusions and trigger conditions.
3. Market definition, size, density and reachable prospect universe.
4. Growth and timing/tailwind analysis.
5. Buyer economics, job/treatment/order/contract values and willingness-to-pay proxies.
6. Current customer journey / workflow map and failure points.
7. Quantified pain and revenue-leak model with scenario math.
8. Existing systems, incumbents and competitor landscape.
9. Underserved-gap analysis: why current tools/services do not fully solve the problem.
10. Offer design and measurable customer outcome.
11. Delivery architecture options and selection logic, including WhatsApp/CRM/agent layers where relevant.
12. Implementation/onboarding requirements and likely friction.
13. Unit economics, software/usage cost assumptions and gross-margin sensitivity.
14. Pricing hypotheses and commercial model.
15. Acquisition strategy, prospecting channels and decision-maker map.
16. Objections, switching barriers, compliance and operational risks.
17. Retention/expansion logic and adjacent modules.
18. Full 10-factor niche scoring rationale with evidence for each factor.
19. Evidence classification: Verified / Estimated / Missing.
20. Source ledger with dates and direct URLs.
21. Live-validation plan with sample size, metrics, pass/fail gates and stop conditions.

Research quality rules:

- Prefer UAE/Dubai government and first-party sources, then high-quality industry data, then credible current market/operator evidence.
- Use broad global benchmarks only when local evidence does not exist, and label transferability limits.
- Do not raise Evidence Confidence because a large market exists if the exact workflow pain remains unverified.
- Distinguish market attractiveness from delivery feasibility.
- Lead with measurable business outcomes, not AI/software novelty.
- A comprehensive file should contain enough evidence and reasoning that another operator can understand why the niche is ranked where it is without opening the parent register.
