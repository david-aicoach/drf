## Method

### What was not done

No formula such as `70% Opportunity + 30% Niche` was introduced. The canonical niche framework explicitly prohibits merging the niche score into the parent 100-point Opportunity Score.

### What was done

For every parent opportunity:

1. start from the existing 15-factor v1.5 score;
2. read the comprehensive offer research;
3. where a matching v3 niche dossier exists, use it to test broad parent assumptions;
4. change parent factors only when niche evidence materially contradicts or sharpens the assumption;
5. separately retain the exact niche score/evidence confidence for market selection;
6. recalculate AI Autonomy from the revised AI factors;
7. update Evidence Confidence based on the completed offer+niche research;
8. keep live commercial evidence as the next gate.

The niche layer most directly affected **Willingness to Pay, AI Marketability, AI Deliverability, Low Human Dependency, Speed to Revenue, Paid Growth Potential, Defensibility/Moat and MRR quality** where strong incumbent software or a narrower wedge was found.

## Major corrections

### HighLevel Vertical SaaS Snapshot Business-in-a-Box: 93 → 89

The platform mechanics remain excellent, but the first fully researched vertical—Dubai holiday-home operators—scores only 82/100. Mature PMS/vertical systems make replacement/integration harder. Vertical SaaS should be the packaging layer after outcome proof, not the first product thesis.

### AI Support & Sales Assistant: 93 → 90

The first v3 niche, holiday-home operators, scores 78/100 with 95% evidence confidence. Native PMS and vertical AI already cover much repetitive support. The sellable wedge must prove incremental resolution/booking value.

### Reputation & Local Visibility Engine: 88 → 85

The clinic niche falls to 79/100 after finding Birdeye, Podium, Weave and UAE-native Fidelia plus heavy local SEO competition. Retain as an attached trust/conversion module, not a standalone sniper offer.

### Grok Bot AI-First Revenue Operations Business-in-a-Box: 87 → 81

Seven v3 agentic niches now exist. None reaches Sniper; the best is tender/RFQ at 84, while generic car-rental/ecommerce/holiday-home back-office theses fall to 70–74. Grok Bot is a delivery rail for bounded cross-system work, not a broad outcome business.

### Recruitment OS / Hiring Intelligence SaaS: 91 → 87

Generic ATS capability is already commoditised. The score only remains strong when Talent Bridge sells proprietary evaluation/intelligence modules around existing ATS infrastructure.

### AI Job Board + Screened Talent Marketplace: 84 → 76

Software can be rented cheaply; the hard problem is two-sided liquidity, paid employer demand and screened-supply density. The prior structural score over-rewarded buildability/scalability before liquidity.

### Business Blueprints — website/launch-kit product type (historical working thesis): 79 → 71

Generic AI websites are now commodity. The only strategically useful form is a vertical conversion launch kit attached to recurring CRM/lead-conversion/optimisation revenue.

### AI-First Marketplace Directory: 77 → 72

AI search/interface is not a moat. Cheap marketplace/directory software solves the build layer while permissioned supply, buyer demand and distribution remain unproven.

## Positive / stable signals

### WhatsApp + CRM remains #1 — 95

The v3 niche layer strengthens rather than weakens the thesis. HVAC scores 92, specialist MEP and aesthetic clinics 90, dental/real estate 89 and movers/car rental 87. The correction is architectural: do not replace capable ERP/DMS/PMS systems; own the customer conversation, pipeline and next-action layer where a real gap exists.

### Revenue Recovery remains #2 — 94

HVAC scores 91, MEP 87 and automotive 86 after incumbent-system corrections. The strength is that the offer can operate through existing systems instead of requiring replacement.

### AI Voice remains #3 — 94

Emergency HVAC is a 91/100 Sniper niche. Automotive voice is only 84, which makes the gating rule clearer: sell overflow/after-hours only where missed-call economics are measured.

### Missed Lead remains #4 — 93

Aesthetic 88, car rental 87 and dental 87 give the offer multiple strong measurable markets. It remains one of the easiest modules to instrument because response/contact/booking baselines are straightforward.

### Instant Quote rises to #5 — 91

The new gypsum/drywall/false-ceiling dossier is 87/100 and expands the concept from a simple calculator into a **Gypsum Quote-to-Cash Revenue System**: structured intake, safe indicative estimate, survey gating, quote control, follow-up and commercial visibility.

```text
1. HVAC WhatsApp + CRM foundation
2. Revenue Recovery or AI Voice based on measured leakage
3. Missed Lead / Instant Quote where the niche workflow supports it
4. Prove second-client repeatability
5. Package the proven stack as Vertical SaaS

```

`Assessment-as-a-Service + Partner-Delivered Recruitment → cash + employer evidence → later platform/data products`
