# DRF Layer 2 — Commercial Underwriting, Proof, Stage and Capital

**Owner:** `skills/drf-opportunity-factory/SKILL.md`  
**Applies after:** a Business Opportunity advances Layer 1 and a Business × Niche is selected/designable.

## Purpose
Answer:

> **Is this selected Business × Niche commercially strong enough, sufficiently evidenced and sufficiently proven for the next bounded action?**

RBS does not replace Opportunity Score or Niche Score.

## RBS — Revenue Blueprint Score /100
Score the commercially designed Business × Niche.

| Factor | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Demand / market evidence | 15 |
| Pain, willingness to pay and pricing | 10 |
| Revenue quality / retention | 10 |
| Unit economics / margin | 15 |
| Acquisition | 10 |
| Delivery repeatability | 10 |
| Scalability / founder independence | 10 |
| Capital efficiency / return | 10 |
| Moat / defensibility | 5 |
| Risk / downside resilience | 5 |
| **Total** | **100** |

Each factor must cite the evidence type: verified external fact, credible estimate, inference, DRF actual, or missing.

## Return Profile
Do not reduce economics to one optimistic ROI number. Record at minimum:
- startup/test capital;
- setup cost and recurring delivery cost;
- acquisition assumption/CAC where known;
- contribution per customer/unit;
- expected time to first cash;
- downside/base/upside outcome;
- maximum practical downside for the next stage;
- whether figures are estimates or DRF actuals.

Use the Skill's financial-model asset only when the decision warrants a model. Do not create a spreadsheet merely for completeness.

## External Market Proof vs DRF Proof
External Market Proof belongs to Layer 1/market evidence and can support desk underwriting. It never awards live DRF execution proof.

### DRF Proof P0–P6

| Level | Name | Minimum meaning |
|---:|---|---|
| P0 | Captured | Opportunity deliberately recorded; no underwriting claim |
| P1 | Desk Underwritten | Business × Niche, offer, price, GTM, delivery and major economics researched enough for a responsible desk decision |
| P2 | Backtested | Strong external/operator evidence and historical/benchmark/backtest work reduce the main desk uncertainties |
| P3 | Forward Tested | Current-market test creates real intent/commitment evidence; still not successful paid delivery |
| P4 | Revenue Proven | DRF has delivered the offer for payment and captured real delivery/cost/outcome evidence |
| P5 | Repeatable | Multiple successful deliveries show repeatable acquisition/delivery/economics with bounded support |
| P6 | Scale Proven / Blueprint Certified | Scale economics and operating system are sufficiently proven for repeatable expansion/packaging |

Documentation completeness never raises DRF Proof. External operators may support P1/P2 only.

## Founder-facing Stage
Use these operating stages:

```text
REJECT → RESEARCH → TEST → PILOT → FUND → SCALE → BLUEPRINT
```

Suggested mapping:
- REJECT: fatal/weak thesis
- RESEARCH: material desk uncertainty remains
- TEST: bounded current-market/transferability test authorised
- PILOT: paid/real-client pilot evidence required
- FUND: capital expansion justified by sufficient proof
- SCALE: repeatability economics justify growth
- BLUEPRINT: operating system proven enough for packaging/certification

Stage is a decision, not merely a P-level label. Founder approval boundaries still apply.

## Default capital staircase
These are **maximum bounded defaults**, not mandatory spend:

| Tier | Purpose | Additional capital | Cumulative ceiling |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Tier 1 | Validate | up to US$3,000 | US$3,000 |
| Tier 2 | Launch / paid pilot | up to +US$7,000 | US$10,000 |
| Tier 3 | Scale | up to +US$20,000 | US$30,000 |

Unlock only the minimum capital required for the next proof. Strong EMP can reduce test scope; it does not justify spending more.

## Decision gates
Use explicit decisions:
- **GO** — proceed to the next bounded proof action.
- **HOLD** — one material uncertainty must be resolved first.
- **KILL** — evidence invalidates viability/transferability.
- **RECYCLE** — underlying opportunity may survive but niche/offer/channel/architecture must change.

## Next Proof
Every active Business × Niche must have exactly one clear next proof milestone that targets the **largest remaining DRF-specific uncertainty**.

Good examples:
- 2 unrelated paid deposits at a pre-committed price;
- one paid pilot proving contribution after support/delivery cost;
- 10 qualified prospect conversations with an explicit pass/fail threshold;
- one controlled data backtest demonstrating enough recoverable value to justify a pilot.

Avoid vague next steps such as “do more research”.

## Evidence hierarchy for capital/proof
Prefer, in order:
1. DRF actual cash/customer/delivery/cost evidence;
2. comparable operator economics and customer evidence;
3. first-party pricing/market/platform data;
4. strong independent research/benchmarks;
5. credible estimates;
6. inference.

A higher-quality new source can change RBS or Return assumptions without changing DRF Proof.

## Output
Layer 2 underwriting must leave:
- selected Business × Niche + Niche Score/confidence;
- offer/pricing/revenue streams;
- GTM/customer-acquisition plan;
- delivery architecture;
- RBS /100 with evidence basis;
- downside/base/upside Return Profile;
- EMP shown separately;
- DRF Proof P0–P6;
- Stage;
- Capital ceiling / use;
- one Next Proof + pass/fail/stop condition;
- material risks/counter-evidence.
