# DRF Market Intelligence Research Standard

**Owner:** `skills/drf-opportunity-factory/SKILL.md`

Use this reference when a new/refresh opportunity requires comprehensive current market intelligence beyond the core scoring questions.

## Objective
Build enough current evidence to answer:
- Is there a real payer and painful outcome?
- Are materially similar businesses already making money?
- How do they package, price, acquire, deliver and retain customers?
- Which niches are most attractive and reachable?
- What is changing in the market right now?
- What would make this fail for DRF?

## Research sequence

### 1. Define the commercial hypothesis
State buyer, pain/outcome, likely price/revenue type, geography, current alternatives and why now.

### 2. Find successful comparable operators
Prefer multiple independent examples. Capture:
- company/operator;
- niche/geography;
- exact offer/promise;
- setup/upfront price;
- recurring/usage/licence/commission/royalty/upsells;
- acquisition channels/funnel/CTA;
- customer/review/case-study/sales/transaction/longevity/expansion evidence;
- delivery/onboarding/support pattern;
- retention/repeat logic;
- source/date/quality;
- what may not transfer to DRF.

### 3. Find counter-evidence
Search deliberately for:
- failed/closed operators;
- weak reviews/complaints/refunds/churn;
- high support or low margin;
- platform/AI cost or reliability problems;
- regulation/privacy/data-rights constraints;
- incumbent software/substitutes;
- acquisition saturation/high CAC;
- founder/brand/audience/data advantages that cannot be copied.

### 4. Market size / growth / timing
Use current first-party/government/credible industry sources where possible. Avoid false precision. Distinguish:
- global category size;
- target geography;
- reachable ICP count/density;
- current growth/durability;
- timing catalysts/risks.

### 5. Pricing / willingness to pay
Prioritise live operator pricing, marketplace listings, current product plans, public contracts/case studies and direct customer economics. Separate actual observed price from DRF proposed price.

### 6. Demand / search / discovery
Where relevant assess:
- Google search intent/SEO competition;
- directories/marketplaces/review platforms;
- social/community demand signals;
- paid-ad presence and commercial keywords;
- AI-answer/agent discovery surfaces and structured-data opportunities;
- whether buyers search for the category, the pain, the outcome or the vendor.

Do not use one viral post as proof.

### 7. Customer acquisition
Map credible routes by niche:
- warm network / existing clients;
- outbound/list-based prospecting;
- SEO/content;
- paid search/social;
- partnerships/referrals/associations;
- marketplaces/directories;
- product-led or channel-led distribution.

Estimate only when the basis is explicit. Do not invent CAC.

### 8. Delivery / AI economics
For AI-enabled businesses investigate representative production workload:
- API/model/provider cost;
- quotas/rate limits;
- deterministic alternatives;
- exception/recovery rate;
- human minutes;
- support/onboarding burden;
- security/authentication dependencies;
- vendor concentration;
- fully loaded cost per successful outcome.

### 9. Niche research
For each serious niche quantify/assess:
- pain/urgency;
- purchasing power;
- reachable prospect volume;
- current systems/incumbents;
- measurable outcome economics;
- recurring pain;
- regulatory/data friction;
- niche-specific successful comparables;
- counter-evidence.

Then use `niche-scoring.md`.

### 10. Transferability
For every strong external example ask:
- same payer?
- same purchase trigger?
- similar geography/customer economics?
- reachable through similar channel?
- comparable delivery quality/cost possible?
- legal/platform/system constraints transferable?
- unique founder/brand/audience/data advantage?

## Source discipline
Use labels:
- **Verified fact** — directly supported by reliable source/record.
- **Credible estimate** — range with explicit basis.
- **Inference** — interpretation from facts.
- **DRF actual** — observed in DRF/Talent Bridge/iMPLEMENTAi operation.
- **Missing** — unknown.

Prefer current first-party/operator/government/public-company/credible market evidence. Use community discussion for experience/reaction signals, not as sole proof of major numeric claims.

## Freshness
Current market/pricing/platform/regulatory evidence should be dated. Re-search when a material current decision depends on a fact that could have changed.

## Research output
The research should be sufficient to populate:
- Layer 1 scoring + EMP;
- ranked niches;
- offer/pricing/revenue model;
- GTM/acquisition;
- delivery architecture;
- RBS/Return;
- risks/counter-evidence;
- one Next Proof.

Do not produce research for its own sake. Stop when evidence is sufficient for the next decision or when a genuine uncertainty requires live testing rather than more desk research.
