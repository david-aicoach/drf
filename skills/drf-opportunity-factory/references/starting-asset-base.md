# DRF Starting Asset Base

**Recorded:** 2026-08-29  
**Source:** founder-reported operating assets unless explicitly verified elsewhere.

DRF is not starting from zero. Revenue strategy, operating cost and build decisions must account for the capability, infrastructure, distribution, relationships and founder leverage already available before adding new cost or complexity.

## Governing principle

```text
USE WHAT IS ALREADY OWNED
-> prove the route
-> exploit existing distribution
-> integrate what already works
-> automate where useful
-> buy/build only for a measured gap
```

## Human commercial advantage

David does not need to become an email marketer.

David's highest-value commercial role is where human judgement, credibility and relationships materially improve revenue outcomes:

- strategic conversations;
- diagnosing the prospect's real business problem;
- trust and relationship building;
- positioning and offer shaping;
- commercial judgement;
- negotiation;
- closing;
- high-value client and partner conversations;
- deciding where founder attention creates disproportionate return.

The factory should automate or agent-assist the repetitive surrounding work wherever safe and useful:

- lead research and enrichment;
- segmentation and prioritisation;
- campaign preparation;
- first-draft outreach and follow-up copy;
- scheduling and reminders;
- CRM/lead-list hygiene;
- research briefs before calls;
- meeting preparation and follow-up;
- reporting and next-action tracking.

The objective is not to remove David from commercial activity. It is to reserve founder time for the points where David materially improves conversion, trust, judgement or deal value.

## Asset-first revenue test

Before DRF adds a paid tool, custom service, new runtime, new lead source or bespoke platform component, ask:

1. Do we already own an asset that solves most of the problem?
2. Can an existing subscription surface perform the work?
3. Can HighLevel, GitHub, OneDrive, Microsoft, FolderDesk, an MCP or an existing CLI handle it?
4. Can an existing AI profile, Hermes or another existing runtime execute it?
5. Can the current LinkedIn, Talent Bridge, iMPLEMENTAi or other warm relationship base validate demand first?
6. Does this action move a real commercial opportunity forward?
7. Is the remaining gap measured and worth new cost or code?

Only then add infrastructure or spend.
