# The Strategic Flywheel

This is the core DRF commercial flywheel and the scalable asset to protect, prove and improve.

```text
OUR OWN LEADS
      ↓
AI BD ENGINE
      ↓
QUALIFIED MEETINGS
      ↓
DAVID CLOSES
      ↓
REVENUE
      ↓
PROOF
      ↓
IMPROVE AI ENGINE
      ↓
MORE MEETINGS
      ↓
MORE REVENUE
      ↓
SELL THE ENGINE
      ↓
CLIENT IMPLEMENTATIONS
      ↓
MORE PROOF
```

## Governing interpretation

The scalable asset is not merely lead generation or individual client work. It is the **AI BD Engine** itself.

DRF should first use the engine on our own existing lead and relationship assets to produce qualified meetings, revenue and proof. That proof is fed back into the engine to improve it. Only after the engine produces repeatable commercial evidence should the proven system be sold and implemented for clients.

```text
OWN LEADS → PROVE → IMPROVE → SCALE → SELL THE PROVEN ENGINE
```

## Operating boundary

- AI owns research, targeting, outreach, follow-up, qualification, booking and the operating work around the meeting.
- David owns the high-value human conversation, diagnosis, trust, negotiation and close.
- Revenue and qualified meetings are the primary proof signals.
- Client implementations create a second proof loop that further improves the engine.
- Do not detach productisation from real commercial proof.
