# Revenue Blueprint Factory — Compatibility Reference

**Status:** Supporting compatibility reference — not the canonical end-to-end workflow  
**Superseded as top-level workflow by:** [77.2] #79  
**Canonical workflow:** [`drf-opportunity-factory.md`](./drf-opportunity-factory.md)

## Why this file remains

Earlier DRF files and links referenced `workflows/revenue-blueprint-factory.md` as the complete process. That framing caused the deeper RBS/P0–P6 underwriting layer to be mistaken for the identity of DRF.

DRF is **David's Revenue Factory**. The canonical process now begins with business-opportunity attractiveness, then selects the niche, then designs/underwrites the selected Business × Niche, then produces a structured business case.

Use:

```text
workflows/drf-opportunity-factory.md
```

for every:

- new founder-supplied business opportunity;
- automated opportunity discovery;
- existing-portfolio refresh;
- business-model pivot/addition;
- Business × Niche underwriting;
- execution/proof progression;
- optional Blueprint packaging.

## Where the former RBF-7 stages now live

The useful proof progression remains inside the canonical workflow:

| Former RBF-7 stage | Current DRF location |
|---|---|
| Capture | Layer 1 opportunity capture |
| Underwrite | Layer 1 structural selection + Layer 2 Business × Niche underwriting |
| Backtest | Layer 2 P2 comparative/transferability backtest |
| Forward Test | DRF P3 current-market test of remaining uncertainty |
| Live Pilot | DRF P4 paid delivery/value proof |
| Prove Repeatability | DRF P5 repeated acquisition/delivery/contribution |
| Scale and Certify | DRF P6 scale proof and optional Blueprint certification |

```text
DRF = the Revenue Factory

= deeper underwriting and execution controls inside DRF

= an optional packaging output of a sufficiently evidenced business

Business Blueprints

```
